a claim that a quote exists.

**Provenance caveat.** Organic platforms carry documented sampling bias, and
review sites carry incentivised-review contamination. Quotes here are evidence
of *what some customers say*, weighted by how loudly they say it, and are not a
sample of the market. Where a quote is the sole support for a positioning move,
that move is labelled low confidence.
