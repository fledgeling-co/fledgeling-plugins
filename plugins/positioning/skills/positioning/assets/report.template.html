Vetoes — evaluated first, and not compensable

A territory fails a veto and is eliminated rather than penalised: the product cannot deliver it, the company cannot be credible in it, the go-to-market motion it implies does not match the unit economics, or it breaks a stated constraint. A score cannot express this, which is why it runs first.
