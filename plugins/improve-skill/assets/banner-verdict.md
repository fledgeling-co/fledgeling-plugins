One liability inherited from the icon rather than introduced here, **cleared on 19 August by the icon's round 21**: `improve-skill`'s `icon.svg` failed the structure gate at 1042 paths against a 400-path soup guard and 231KB against a 200KB envelope, and now passes at 314 paths / 142,554 bytes. It was always an icon defect on the audit's list and not a banner one, but a banner derived from a path-soup master cannot be re-derived cleanly from named constants, which is why it was recorded here.

**No constant this banner derives from moved with it, and one thing did.** Round 21 re-emitted the icon's grain and left `ANGLE` 33°, `EDGE_MID` (543, 604), `BLADE_LEN` 640, the boundary at (0, 957) → (1024, 292) and every palette entry exactly as they were, so the 33° split this page draws on the extension of the icon's own cut still lands where it did, and the grain that `banner-src.html` generates in JavaScript comes off its own constants rather than the master's and is unaffected. What did move is the **600px raster of the tile embedded in the lockup**, which is a render of the master and is now one increment further from it: measured against the round-21 master it reads mean |ΔRGB| 2.414/255 with p99 6→17, against 1.292/255 for the round-20 master it was already drifting from. The drift is the same class this verdict opens with and it is the same size — sub-3/255, visible only to a hash — so it is recorded rather than fixed, because round 21 was scoped to the icon and re-rendering the banner is a banner change with its own verdict. **The fix is one re-render of `banner-src.html`, and it should be taken the next time the banner is touched for any reason.**
