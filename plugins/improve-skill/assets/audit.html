Shipping: C2 (icon.png), by the user's decision. Highest-scoring take: A (icon.svg) at 11/12. The two are not the same take, and this block records that rather than smoothing it over. C2 ships because it won the material comparison by a distance: dense physical graphite, worn corners, a convincing red under-glow, and a shaving curl that gives the tile mid-pass life. Known liabilities to revisit, all real and all inherited from shipping a raster: (1) #10 hard-fails. It is a flat pre-masked raster, so identity is a colour relationship with no layers behind it, and it will not survive Dark, Clear or Tinted rendering. This is the corpus's single most common failure and the icon now has it; the layered master at icon.svg is the route back. (2) Its own lighting works against the concept. Sampled on the masked 1024, the un-planed side measures L 0.426 and the trued side L 0.363, so the side that is supposed to be brighter and truer is in fact slightly darker, because a warm bloom sits on the rough side and a vignette sits on the trued one. The before-and-after reads as a texture change rather than an improvement. The banner compensates by carrying a wider split in its own ground. (3) A hard specular bloom top-left is legacy-era drag under Tahoe grammar (#5, #9), and the focal sits at roughly half the tile, low and left of centre (#2). (4) At 16px it survives as a dark bar with a warm edge, which is the read that matters, but the shaving and the grain go to mush well before that. (5) It cannot be edited. Any future change means a new generation and a new audit, not a diff.

Rubric: the 12-point icon audit from icon-directions.md (mask discipline, grid, silhouette, 16px identity, single light model, palette discipline, figure-ground, depth coherence, era fit, variant robustness, personality, no-text) — checks 1–4 non-negotiable, delivery bar ≥10/12. Raster takes audited on their squircle-masked 1024 versions. Render commands: rsvg-convert -w {1024,256,64,32} for SVGs, the same via an embedded-image wrapper for rasters.

The shared spec all four takes were built from

  • Direction: Tahoe gel-glass sub-register (a), porcelain plus gel object, crossed with device bank #16 (the icon performs the verb) and #5 (dual-function primitive). Runner-up and near-neighbour: Direction 7, Diagonal Tool, which the catalogue names the most template-worn move in the corpus. This enters that territory deliberately and inverts it: in Direction 7 the tool crosses an inert field and the tool is the identity. Here the tool has changed the field, and the identity is the change.
  • Glyph: the whole tile is the workpiece. A worn plane iron lies mid-pass on a rising diagonal, 640 × 168 at 33°, crisp corners on the honed edge and worn rounding with a shallow sag on the back.
  • Signature: the before-and-after boundary is the diagonal, and the one vermilion hone line is that boundary. One shape doing three jobs: the tool's cutting edge, the division between two states, and the icon's only accent.
  • Light: one soft top-left source, plus the sanctioned second source, an emissive interior; here the hone itself, spilling onto the surface it has just cut.
  • Palette: two families. Warm-neutral porcelain (#DCD8CD→#B2AB9B un-planed, #FFFFFE→#F7F2E7 trued), achromatic graphite (#4B5159→#101215), and one vermilion reserved strictly for the honed edge (#C4341A/#F2542D/#FF7C46).
  • #10 layer plan: bg the two-state cushion tile; mid the grain and the hone's spill; fg the blade, the carrying shape; highlight the hone line and rim light.

Failure-mode anti-checklist

  • Template genericism: clear. No stock gel glyph, no saturated gradient tile.
  • Diagonal-tool default: entered knowingly, not by reflex, and inverted so the tool is not the identity. Stated as the run's one aesthetic risk.
  • White-on-hue with no translucency cues: not applicable, porcelain register.
  • Tone-on-tone collapse: measured, not assumed. 4.82:1 and 7.41:1.
  • Metaphor pile-up: two objects on the tile, surface and blade. The third, a shaving, was authored and cut.
  • Sibling non-differentiation: five of the six siblings share this porcelain register, which is the marketplace's house ground rather than a borrowed one. Differentiation is carried by the split, which no sibling uses.
  • Flat pre-masked raster: avoided; the raster takes are alternates, and the shipping master is layered vector.
  • Baked text: none.
