# Research to decision

Read [the evidence contract](../follow-through/references/evidence-contract.md).
Start with the choice to make, the user's constraints and what would change the
answer. Reuse prior research with its dates and source identities. A historical
comparison identifies candidates, not today's prices or compatibility. Preserve
the requested product and task; do not turn a narrow choice into a market survey.

Separate hard constraints from preferences: exact platform/version, workload,
capacity, compatibility, budget, region, ongoing costs, delivery time and risk.
Resolve discoverable facts yourself. Use `clarify:clarify` only for a material
user-owned constraint. Include the keep-current/do-nothing option if viable;
do not manufacture a purchase requirement.

Research only gaps that can change the decision. Verify unstable prices,
availability, specifications and supported versions at current sources. Prefer
manufacturer documentation for compatibility and specifications and independent
measured tests for performance claims. Record exact SKU/configuration, test
workload, software version and date. Syndicated summaries of one benchmark are
one source, not independent corroboration. Separate facts, calculated estimates
and inference; conflicts remain visible. Stop when a feasible recommendation is
supported and remaining uncertainty would not change it, or name the missing
measurement that prevents choosing. Do not keep browsing until every possible
product has been ranked.

Storage decisions need actual access paths: local SSD, network share, platform
library support and network speed may be different constraints. Separate usable
capacity, redundancy and backup; verify whether games/apps can run or only be
stored on the proposed path. Computing comparisons use the target workload,
thermals, sustained performance and total cost rather than headline chip names.
Testing tools use observable coverage, supported target/runtime, CI/foreground
requirements, evidence export and recurring cost; a demo screenshot is not proof
the tool can observe the needed effect. A safe read-only compatibility probe can
proceed; installing software, starting paid trials or changing environments needs
applicable authority.

Deliver a compact comparison and a verdict: recommended option, why it satisfies
the constraints, cost with check time/currency, weaknesses, uncertainty and the
condition that would change the answer. If evidence cannot settle it, propose
one discriminating test with a success criterion. Route a requested published
research page to `dossier-report:dossier-report` only after confirming its
publishing and paid-research scope; a decision brief does not grant that scope.

The recommendation ends the research phase. Buying, starting subscriptions,
provisioning infrastructure or publishing requires separate applicable authority.
If retaining decisions, use [the ledger format](../follow-through/references/ledger-format.md)
with domain `decision`, and retain superseded conclusions with their reason so a
new run improves the same decision instead of reopening it from scratch.
