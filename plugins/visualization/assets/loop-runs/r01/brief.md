# r01 — the halo well

EDIT CLASS: the rule's light on the porcelain. One class only.

MEASURED, not assumed. Chroma (R−B) on the porcelain at x=180, left of the bars,
stepping down from the rule's hottest row:

| dy from rule | reference | master (r00) |
|---|---|---|
| 0   | 197 | 198 |

| +40 |  77 | — |

The rule itself matches (198 vs 197 at its own row). What is missing is everything
the rule DOES to the surface: the reference's halo is an order of magnitude
stronger and reaches only ~110px, where the master's `bloom` is faint and 182px
tall. A weak wide veil instead of a bright tight well.

That is the apple-26 construction — each Reminders dot sits in a soft tinted halo
well that bleeds onto the porcelain — and it is the whole signature. A baseline
that does not light the surface it lies on is a coloured bar, not an emitter.

Laterally the reference falls from 64 to 9 within 20px past the rule's end, so the
well is bounded by the object rather than being a free-floating glow.
