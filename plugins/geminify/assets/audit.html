Recommendation: ship C (icon.png), with A (icon.svg) as the editable master. C is the take that survives the shelf: this marketplace tile is a decorative PNG with no macOS compositor downstream, so the material has to be in the file, and C has it where A does not. Checks 1-4 audited on the real renders above rather than on the 1024 hero: mask discipline holds because the raster was masked with the set's own superellipse path, the pair is optically centred at 63% of the tile wide, the silhouette names itself filled black, and the 32px render is the strongest of the three. Neither take has been judged by a blind panel yet, and the fidelity composite is measured on luminance + SSIM + edges only (no torch/LPIPS on this machine), which is documented as blind to exactly the material differences this commission turned on.
