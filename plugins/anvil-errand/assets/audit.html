Recommendation: ship A (icon.svg). It is the only take that is both on the family register and Icon-Composer–shaped: four named layers, the anvil solid entirely in #mid so dropping #fg and #highlight leaves a graphite anvil on porcelain that still names itself, no <image> embeds, 58 paths and 40 KB inside the complexity envelope, and fidelity.py structure passing. Checks 1–4 were audited on the renders above rather than on the source: silhouette verified by filling the object solid black, 16px identity verified at 0.258 RMS luminance contrast against a family median of 0.152. Material provenance is the macOS 26 corpus, sampled rather than described — porcelain ramp from apple-10 / 06 / 18 / 23, satin dark metal from apple-15's hammer head, emissive-on-porcelain behaviour from apple-06, contact-shadow depth and hue from apple-12 — with the horn's cone taken from B and C agreeing against the master.

Known liabilities to revisit: (1) the step's far strip is occluded by paint order, not by computed geometry, so its boundary against the horn's top band is a straight cut rather than a real overlap — visible at 1024, invisible by 128. (2) The near flank is still the largest low-variation region in the tile; it carries the key-axis ramp, one satin band and the porcelain up-bounce, and it would take a real surface treatment rather than another global gradient to improve. (3) The arc's tail tapers to zero opacity above the horn, so at 16px it reads as a detached warm smudge rather than as a trajectory leaving the tip — deliberate (a graspable loop reads as a handle, twice proven by Arrow) but it does cost the out-and-back reading at the smallest size. (4) The graphite is cooler than the family's warm palette, chosen so it stays separable from improve-skill's warm charcoal whetstone; if the shelf ever reads cold, that is the constant to move. (5) No fidelity loop ran: LPIPS engages only at 256 and 1024 and torch is not installed here, so the gate would refuse the degraded tier rather than guess — the material was scored against the corpus and by eye, and was never measured against the winning raster.
Rubric: the 12-point icon audit from icon-directions.md (mask discipline, grid, silhouette, 16px identity, single light model, palette discipline, figure-ground, depth coherence, era coherence, variant robustness, personality, no-text) — checks 1–4 non-negotiable, delivery bar ≥10/12. What moved, measured the same way before and after
