# Notes for the site — Saltbush Ceramics

Owner: Mira Okafor. Studio at 14 Pakenham Street, Fremantle WA. Open Wed–Sun, 10am–4pm.

Classes:
- Wheel taster, 2 hours, $95, Saturdays 10am. Max 6 people.
- Six-week wheel course, Tuesday evenings 6–8.30pm, $540, clay and two firings included.
- Hand-building for kids (8–12), school holidays only, $60.

Shop: small-batch tableware glazed in a speckled saltbush green and a dark iron glaze. Mugs from
$48, bowls from $64. Online shop is on Shopify at shop.saltbushceramics.com.au — link out to it.

About: Mira trained in Mashiko, Japan for two years, opened the studio in 2019. Works with local
clay from a pit near Bindoon.

Wants: a website that doesn't look like every other pottery site. Phone-first; most bookings come
from Instagram. Would love the hero to be a short video of a bowl being thrown on the wheel, ending
on the finished bowl. Budget for generated media: up to $15. Don't publish anything yet.

Booking: links to her existing Bookwhen page, bookwhen.com/saltbush.
