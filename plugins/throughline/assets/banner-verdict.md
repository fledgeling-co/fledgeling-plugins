# Banner verdict: throughline

Ships. Checked on the sheet at 1600, 900, 400 and 200 css px and the 1:1 lockup crop.

What holds: the wordmark reads at every size down to the 200px thumbnail, and the ember on "line" is the only warm thing on the left half, so the eye lands on it. The icon and the bench stack share one silhouette (four offset slabs, one cord), so the banner and the marketplace tile read as the same object at two scales. Newsreader is a linked font and the renderer measured it as loaded against a fallback control.

Known liabilities: at 200px the bench stack's cord thins to a line and the stack reads as a second icon rather than a scene, which is acceptable at thumbnail but worth knowing; the tagline is three lines at 900px README width, one more than the family's shorter banners carry. No em dash anywhere in the copy.
