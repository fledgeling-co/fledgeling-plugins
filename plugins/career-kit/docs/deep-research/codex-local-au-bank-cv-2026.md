# Australian banking CV, LinkedIn and SEEK strategy
**Research cut-off: 20 November 2026.**

The strongest application for this candidate is a clearly targeted, evidence-led CV that makes her progression, product decisions and people leadership easy to find. Prepare separate product, delivery/change and people-leadership versions from one accurate career record.

**Confidence key:** **High** = directly supported by primary documentation or the retrieved employer advertisement; **Medium** = bounded evidence or an explicitly identified professional inference; **Low/unconfirmed** = insufficient evidence for a bank-specific conclusion. Confidence in an advertisement's contents is separate from confidence that the vacancy is still open. “Undated; accessed 20 November 2026” means the publisher supplied no reliable publication date.

## 1. Executive summary

- **Use a single-column, text-based CV.** Keep contact details in the document body, use conventional headings and check the fields populated after upload. Both DOCX and selectable-text PDF can work; the employer's instructions take precedence. **High.** [SEEK, 29 October 2026](https://au.seek.com/career-advice/article/tips-for-creating-an-ats-friendly-resume); [Greenhouse, 2 March 2026](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse).
- **Aim for two pages; allow a third for relevant evidence.** Three pages is a practical allowance for this career, not a verified Australian-bank requirement. Keep her recent role on page one. **Medium — recommendation**, informed by [SEEK, 28 July 2026](https://au.seek.com/career-advice/article/how-long-should-a-resume-be) and [Hays, undated; accessed 20 November 2026](https://www.hays.com.au/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/how-to-write-a-resume).
- **Show one employer and several distinct appointments.** Give each substantial role its own title, business area and dates; identify genuine promotions, lateral moves and secondments. Lead with relevant capability rather than “20+ years of loyalty”. **Medium — inference** from the candidate's career and the progression/technology stereotypes reported by [AHRI/AHRC, July 2025](https://www.ahri.com.au/resources/hr-research/older-and-younger-workers-report-2025).
- **Target the actual job, not all six titles at once.** Product applications need prioritisation and customer/commercial outcomes; people-leader applications need direct-report scope and performance development; delivery applications need dependencies, governance and predictable execution. **High for the distinctions in the sample; Medium for the recommendation.** [Westpac PO, November 2026](https://au.linkedin.com/jobs/view/product-owner-at-westpac-group-4489492753); [CBA Team Manager, closing 22 October 2026](https://au.linkedin.com/jobs/view/team-manager-associate-private-bankers-at-commonwealth-bank-4478383584); [ANZ Delivery Lead, closing 26 November 2026](https://careers.anz.com/job/Melbourne-Agile-Delivery-Lead,-VIC/1370615266/).
- **Assume software may structure and prioritise the application, but do not assume automatic rejection.** CBA discloses AI-assisted screening with human oversight; LinkedIn documents qualification summaries. Neither establishes a universal bank “ATS pass score”. **High.** [CBA, 30 July 2026](https://www.commbank.com.au/content/dam/commbank-assets/about-us/careers/docs/candidate-privacy-statement.pdf); [LinkedIn Hiring Assistant FAQ, accessed 20 November 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a7109304).
- **Use the advertisement's terminology inside evidence.** “Prioritised a product backlog using customer impact and risk” communicates more than a list of “Agile, leadership, transformation”. In the seven product advertisements sampled, stakeholder influence appeared in all seven and risk/governance/compliance in six. **Medium — manual sample analysis**, with sources and counting rules in sections 2.4 and 4.
- **Make banking risk practical.** Describe controls, remediation, operational resilience and customer outcomes she actually delivered. Add CPS 230, CPS 234 or DDO only where she can explain her contribution. **Medium — recommendation**, grounded in [APRA CPS 230, effective 1 July 2026](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/cps-230), [CPS 234, effective 1 July 2019](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/cps-234) and [ASIC, August 2026](https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/corporate-publications/newsletters/market-integrity-update/miu-issue-178-august-2026).
- **AI assistance is not the demonstrated problem; unsupported, interchangeable claims are.** Australian evidence shows recruiter concern about AI-generated CVs, not reliable authorship detection or a blanket penalty. **High for the survey finding; Low/unconfirmed for bank-specific detection.** [Robert Half, 10 February 2026](https://www.roberthalf.com/au/en/about/press/hiring-fatigue-hits-as-overqualification-and-cvs-flood-the-market).
- **For internal moves, use the employee route and check the actual mobility policy.** CBA advertisements require Sidekick; ING publishes a normal 12-month tenure expectation with exceptions. A universal “tell your manager before applying” rule could not be verified. **High for those two examples; unconfirmed elsewhere.** [CBA advertisement, November 2026](https://au.linkedin.com/jobs/view/senior-product-owner-at-commonwealth-bank-4486479897); [ING, 18 November 2026](https://careers.ing.com/en/job/sydney/product-area-lead-credit-cards/3121/47012715520).
- **Choose profile visibility deliberately.** LinkedIn's recruiters-only setting is not guaranteed confidential. SEEK Public can expose basic profile details outside SEEK; Hidden/Applications only sacrifices unsolicited discovery. **High.** [LinkedIn Open to Work, accessed 20 November 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a507508/let-recruiters-know-you-re-open-to-work); [SEEK visibility, undated; accessed 20 November 2026](https://au.seek.com/profile-privacy).

## 2. Detailed findings

### 2.1 Screening technology: what the evidence actually establishes

There are four different operations to prepare for:

| Operation | What it does | Candidate implication |
|---|---|---|
| Parsing | Extracts employer, title, dates, education, skills and contact fields. | Make these explicit and correct extraction errors. |
| Eligibility screening | Uses answers about matters such as work rights, location or required experience. | Answer accurately; CV keywords cannot compensate for an ineligible answer. |
| Search, matching and prioritisation | Finds candidates or orders applications against recruiter criteria. | Use recognisable terminology and show where and when the skill was used. |
| Human selection | Evaluates evidence, judgement, motivation and fit, potentially assisted by summaries. | Give a hiring manager concrete decisions and outcomes to discuss. |

**Confidence: High for the documented functions; Medium for this combined model.** Greenhouse describes field extraction; NAB describes role-specific questions; LinkedIn describes criteria-based evaluation and human decisions. [Greenhouse, 2 March 2026](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse); [NAB, undated; accessed 20 November 2026](https://www.nab.com.au/about-us/careers/apply-for-job); [LinkedIn FAQ, accessed 20 November 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a7109304).

**An ATS logo does not establish which AI features are enabled.** These are documented vendor capabilities, not confirmed deployments at every customer:

| Product | Verified capability | Limit on the claim |
|---|---|---|
| Workday HiredScore | August 2026 release describes an LLM evaluating individual qualifications against parsed CV content, with fit/gap explanations. | Does not prove CBA or ING enabled HiredScore. |
| Oracle Recruiting | Generative-AI ratings and summaries can use parsed education, experience and skills; recruiters can filter by ratings. | Does not prove Westpac or AMP enabled this feature. |
| SAP SuccessFactors | Configurable CV parsing, including Textkernel; 2H 2026 documentation describes AI-assisted skills matching. | Does not prove ANZ or Bendigo use AI matching. |
| LinkedIn Hiring Assistant | Uses hiring criteria, profiles, CVs, screening responses and recruiting notes to summarise qualification matches. | An additional licensed capability, not something every LinkedIn advertiser uses. |

**Confidence: High for capabilities; bank activation unconfirmed.** [Workday, 5 August 2026](https://doc.workday.com/hiredscore/en-us/workday-hiredscore/hiredscore-release-notes/2026/august/wednesday--5th-august.html); [Oracle, undated; accessed 20 November 2026](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/talent-management/farqa/evaluate-candidate-applications-using-ai-matching-ratings.html); [SAP parsing documentation, undated](https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-recruiting/setting-up-and-maintaining-sap-successfactors-recruiting/working-with-resume-parsing) and [SAP 2H 2026](https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_SUCCESSFACTORS_RECRUITING/8477193265ea4172a1dda118505ca631/8134bbc02518434a8ef1c28f3fd2c7a1.html?locale=en-ES); [LinkedIn FAQ, accessed 20 November 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a7109304).

**Formatting recommendation — Medium, based on direct parser guidance:** use one column, ordinary bullets, descriptive headings and a consistent date style such as “Jan 2022 – Present”. Put name, telephone, email and LinkedIn URL in the main body. Avoid skills bars, logos, text boxes and tables for career history. A page number may sit in a footer because losing it would not lose evidence.

This is risk reduction, not a claim that every two-column CV fails. Greenhouse specifically lists these layouts as possible causes of partial or unsuccessful parsing; it also says the file remains attached if parsing fails. [Greenhouse, 2 March 2026](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse).

**DOCX versus PDF:** SEEK calls DOCX often the safest option but also accepts text-based PDF and prioritises the employer's instructions. There is no comparative bank-specific test here proving one universally wins. Keep both. If both are accepted without guidance, I would upload a simple DOCX and check the extracted fields; use a text PDF when requested or when direct human circulation benefits from fixed pagination. **Medium — recommendation.** [SEEK, 29 October 2026](https://au.seek.com/career-advice/article/tips-for-creating-an-ats-friendly-resume).

**Keywords and semantic matching coexist.** LinkedIn offers title/skills filters, while its AI tools assess qualifications in context. Write “Product Owner” in full, followed by “PO” if useful; do the same for genuine specialist terms. Preserve official employment titles and add a truthful functional explanation where an internal title is obscure. **High for platform functions; Medium for writing recommendation.** [LinkedIn filters, undated; accessed 20 November 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a414428?lang=en); [LinkedIn AI agents, accessed 20 November 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a7437598/how-linkedin-uses-ai-agents-to-connect-job-seekers-and-hirers).

**Knockout questions deserve a separate check.** Platforms support screening questions, but this research cannot identify each bank's automatic rejection rules. Treat work rights, office attendance, location, required experience and mandatory credentials as substantive questions. Count actual PO experience, not total employment tenure. **High for question functionality; Medium for this checklist; exact knockout logic unconfirmed.** [LinkedIn screening questions, accessed 20 November 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a410766).

### 2.2 Australian conventions

| Choice | Recommendation for this candidate | Evidence and confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Length | Start with two pages; use three if necessary to demonstrate relevant progression and achievements without tiny type. | Hays recommends approximately two; SEEK allows longer for relevant senior experience. **Medium recommendation**, not a banking rule. |

Omit photograph, DOB, marital status, gender and full street address from the CV. | Victorian guidance discourages unnecessary personal details. **Medium**, including city/state instead of street address as a privacy recommendation. |

Do not write “security cleared”, “APRA approved” or “eligible for all banking checks” merely because she currently works in financial services. A background check is not a transferable government security clearance. For these target roles, the retrieved evidence establishes employer checks, not a universal clearance requirement. **Medium — recommendation; universal requirement unconfirmed.**

### 2.3 Twenty-plus years with one employer

**Recommended structure: a grouped employer heading with distinct role blocks.** For each recent role, retain the employer name on the title line or immediately below it. This gives humans the progression story while keeping each appointment intelligible when text is extracted. In application forms, enter separate employment records where the form permits. **Medium — inference** from parsing requirements and this career's structure; no source proves one grouping format always ranks better.

Give roughly the last 10–15 years the most space, especially recent PO and leadership appointments. Compress earlier work into a clearly labelled early-career section; expand an older role if it supplies a requirement missing from recent experience. These are relevance decisions, not rules for hiding age. **Medium — recommendation**, consistent with [Hays's guidance to remove irrelevant history, undated; accessed 20 November 2026](https://www.hays.com.au/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/how-to-write-a-resume).

Show change through facts:

- Different business areas, products, customer groups or operating models.
- Increasing decision scope, team size, budget or delivery complexity.
- Specific promotions, competitive appointments or secondments, where true.
- Recent learning and use of current tools, with an example of application.

**Medium — inference.** AHRI/AHRC's 2025 research found both positive stereotypes about older workers and negative assumptions about adaptability and technology. A recent delivery example addresses those concerns more credibly than describing herself as “adaptable”. [AHRI/AHRC, July 2025](https://www.ahri.com.au/resources/hr-research/older-and-younger-workers-report-2025).

Omit graduation years where optional and irrelevant. Do not change employment dates, invent gaps or imply she spent 20 years as a PO. The employer's full-history/background-check form should receive the full accurate history it requests. **Medium — integrity and relevance recommendation.**

### 2.5 AI-written CVs: detection, penalties and voice

**No verified evidence was found that the named banks routinely run validated AI-authorship detectors on CVs or reject applications simply because AI helped write them. Confidence: Low/unconfirmed on deployment; this is a knowledge gap, not proof of absence.**

The strongest Australian evidence found concerns assessment difficulty. Robert Half's survey of 500 Australian hiring managers, conducted in October 2025 and released on 10 February 2026, found 37% identified AI-generated CVs as making candidate quality harder to assess. It also found support for tailored experience and clear communication. These are self-reported views, not an experiment measuring detector accuracy or a bank-only sample. **High.** [Robert Half, 10 February 2026](https://www.roberthalf.com/au/en/about/press/hiring-fatigue-hits-as-overqualification-and-cvs-flood-the-market).

Nor is AI screening universal across Australian employers. AHRI's December-quarter 2025 survey reported 17% using AI for screening/shortlisting, 14% for CV/cover-letter review and 49% using none in recruitment/selection. Those historical, cross-sector figures cannot estimate an individual bank's November 2026 practice. **High for the historical finding; Low for extrapolation.** [AHRI, December quarter 2025](https://www.ahri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/AHRI-WorkOutlook-Report-2025-Q4_digital.pdf).

**Recommended workflow — Medium, informed by those findings:**

1. Write an evidence record first: situation, personal responsibility, action, result and how the result was measured.
2. Use AI to organise, shorten and map that evidence to the advertisement.
3. Check every title, date, tool, number and implied ownership.
4. Replace generic claims with a decision, constraint or outcome she can explain.
5. Keep confidential employer/customer information out of unapproved AI services.
6. Prepare interview examples that substantiate the final wording; follow any employer-specific AI rules.

“Strategic, results-driven leader passionate about transformation” adds little. “Prioritised the onboarding backlog across two squads, balancing conversion, control remediation and delivery capacity” gives a recruiter something assessable, provided it is true.

### 2.6 Internal transfers

**Verified examples:** CBA's sampled advertisements tell existing Group employees to apply through Sidekick for a valid application. ANZ's public careers page has an employee login, but does not establish the private approval workflow. ING's November advertisement normally expects 12 months in the current role and allows exceptions after manager consultation. **High.** [CBA P1, November 2026](https://au.linkedin.com/jobs/view/senior-product-owner-at-commonwealth-bank-4486479897); [ANZ careers, accessed 20 November 2026](https://careers.anz.com/); [ING, 18 November 2026](https://careers.ing.com/en/job/sydney/product-area-lead-credit-cards/3121/47012715520).

**Not verified:** whether her employer uses exactly the same ranking for internal and external applicants; when her manager receives a notification; eligibility based on tenure/performance; release periods; or whether informal conversations are required. An employee portal sharing a vendor does not settle those questions.

**Internal CV recommendation — Medium inference:**

- Keep the same standard of evidence as an external CV. Familiarity with her name is not familiarity with her work.
- Include business units, internal appointments and relevant enterprise initiatives, explaining acronyms for another department.
- Show why her organisational knowledge helps the new team: useful dependencies, controls, customer journeys and delivery experience.
- Explain the move positively: the work she wants to contribute to, rather than dissatisfaction with the current team.
- Identify potential internal referees with direct knowledge of the relevant work and obtain their agreement.
- Check the mobility policy or confidentially ask Talent Acquisition about notification timing before assuming a conversation can remain private.
- Where policy leaves discretion, discuss the move with her manager before formal reference/release discussions, and prepare a sensible handover. This is professional etiquette advice, not a universal bank rule.

### 2.7 LinkedIn and SEEK

**LinkedIn discovery — High:** recruiters can filter by titles, location and skills, including skill terms in relevant profile sections. Hiring Assistant can also evaluate the evidence in profiles, CVs and application answers. A complete profile therefore matters independently of the attached CV. [LinkedIn filters, undated; accessed 20 November 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a414428?lang=en); [LinkedIn AI agents, accessed 20 November 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a7437598/how-linkedin-uses-ai-agents-to-connect-job-seekers-and-hirers).

**Recommended headline — Medium inference:**

> Product Owner | People Leadership | Banking Operations & Change | Customer Outcomes

Use “Senior Product Owner” only if her actual scope supports that positioning. Do not present a desired title as a current appointment. A delivery-focused alternative is:

> Delivery & Change Leader | Agile Delivery | Banking Operations | Team Development

**About section — Medium recommendation:** use approximately 150–220 words in first person. Cover her current contribution, breadth of internal moves, two substantiated outcomes and the problems she wants to work on. For example:

> I work at the intersection of banking operations, product delivery and people leadership. Across several roles within one financial-services organisation, I have moved from frontline and operational work into team leadership and product ownership.
>
> My work includes translating customer and colleague needs into delivery priorities, managing backlogs and helping teams deliver within risk and compliance requirements. I also bring direct people-management experience, including coaching and capability development.
>
> Recent examples include [one verified product outcome] and [one verified people or service outcome]. I am interested in roles where I can combine that operational understanding with product ownership, delivery or team leadership.

This is a drafting framework, not a finished factual profile. Replace the bracketed examples and remove any sentence her history cannot substantiate.

Create separate Experience entries for substantial appointments, selecting the same employer organisation; keep titles and dates consistent with the CV. Use fewer achievements than the CV, written for an external reader. Prioritise a focused set of skills and attach them to relevant experience. A professional LinkedIn photograph is a reasonable profile choice; it does not imply adding a photograph to the CV. **Medium — editorial recommendations.**

**Open to Work — High:** recruiters-only attempts to conceal the signal from recruiters at the current employer but gives no guarantee. Public visibility includes colleagues and adds a frame. For an internal-first search, she may prefer to leave it off; if beginning a discreet external search, recruiters-only is the more limited option. **Medium recommendation, conditional on her privacy preference.** [LinkedIn Open to Work, accessed 20 November 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a507508/let-recruiters-know-you-re-open-to-work).

**SEEK — High for functionality:** Talent Search supports natural-language and Boolean/free-text searches, with filters including titles, companies and salary expectations. Profiles expose work history, skills and right-to-work information. Its approachability signal estimates openness to opportunities; it is not a competence score. [SEEK Talent Search, undated; accessed 20 November 2026](https://au.employer.seek.com/page/talentsearch).

**SEEK recommendation — Medium:** complete the structured career history, skills, licences/certifications, location, work rights, availability and realistic preferences. Keep a broadly applicable default CV and deliberately select the targeted version for each application. Do not use salary or location fields to appear in searches for jobs she would not accept.

| Visibility | Practical consequence |
|---|---|
| Public | Basic details can be discoverable beyond SEEK. |
| Standard | Employers can discover the profile and default CV. |
| Limited, where offered | Employers can discover the profile, but the detailed FAQ says they cannot view the CV. |

## 6. Before/after achievement rewrites

**These eight examples are invented illustrations, not candidate achievements.** Replace every number and circumstance with verified evidence. The formula is: personal action + scope + measurable result + timeframe/constraint. **Medium — writing recommendation.**

| Vague duty | Evidence-led illustrative rewrite |
|---|---|
| “Managed the backlog.” | “Reprioritised a 180-item servicing backlog using customer impact, control urgency and effort; reduced median request-to-release time from 10 to 6 weeks over two quarters.” |
| “Improved customer experience.” | “Led discovery with 24 frontline colleagues and used complaint themes to redesign the address-change journey; reduced repeat contacts from 18% to 11% within three months of release.” |
| “Delivered projects on time.” | “Coordinated four squads and three supplier dependencies for a servicing-platform release; completed migration of 42 workflows by the agreed date and maintained service availability through cutover.” |
| “Managed a team.” | “Managed 16 direct reports across two sites, introducing weekly coaching and skills-based work allocation; increased service-level attainment from 82% to 94% over six months.” |
| “Developed staff.” | “Created individual development plans and a cross-training program for 12 analysts; reduced time to independent case handling from 10 to 7 weeks and supported three successful internal appointments.” |
| “Ensured compliance.” | “Partnered with Risk and Operations to prioritise 23 control-remediation actions, establish owners and retain closure evidence; closed all actions by the agreed governance deadline.” |
| “Led change.” | “Led readiness, communications and training for 240 users adopting a new case-management workflow; achieved 91% active use in six weeks and reduced processing rework from 9% to 5%.” |
| “Used AI to improve efficiency.” | “Piloted an approved AI-assisted knowledge-search tool with 35 service colleagues, with human review and restricted data inputs; reduced median information-search time from 6 to 4 minutes in a four-week evaluation.” |

Report percentage-point changes correctly: 82% to 94% is **12 percentage points**, not 12%. ## 8. Knowledge gaps and limits

1. **Bank-specific AI configuration:** public URLs establish several ATS routes, not enabled modules, ranking weights, rejection thresholds or which recruiter uses AI on a particular requisition.
2. **NAB's transition:** PageUp guidance and a branded Eightfold deployment coexist. The evidence does not establish a single current system for every Australian department.
3. **Macquarie:** Avature has corroborative vacancy evidence, but the primary application destination was inaccessible. Current bank-wide configuration remains unconfirmed.
4. **Revolut and Beem:** no current Australian target-role screening setup verified. Revolut's direct product evidence is from 2024; Beem's ownership transition makes older assumptions particularly unreliable.
5. **Advertisement coverage:** 11 deliberately selected roles; seven product, two delivery, two people-management. The sample includes closed 2026 roles, is weighted toward large-bank product work and does not support population-level frequency estimates. Dedicated project/change roles and several smaller banks remain under-sampled.
6. **Vacancy freshness:** public pages and search indexes sometimes supplied different relative dates or inventories. Future closing dates were retrieved for ING and ANZ D2; no applications were submitted to test availability.
7. **Parsing performance:** no controlled DOCX-versus-PDF or grouped-role experiment was run against bank production systems. Formatting advice minimises documented risks; it does not guarantee a rank.
8. **AI authorship:** no validated bank-specific detection/penalty evidence found. Recruiter opinion surveys cannot establish detector accuracy.
9. **Internal policy:** her employer is unspecified and its private mobility rules were unavailable. Manager notification and eligibility need checking internally.
10. **Candidate evidence:** her actual titles, dates, achievements, qualifications and preferred location were not supplied. The skeleton and rewrites are examples to populate, not claims ready to submit.
