# **Rebuilding Your Banking Career Portfolio for Late 2026: CV, LinkedIn, and SEEK Strategy for Australian Financial Services**

To get past automated and AI screening at Australian banks in late 2026 while persuading human hiring managers, rebuild your portfolio around one date-consistent master career record deployed across four tailored channels: an **internal transfer CV** naming your organisation's systems and governance forums; a **2.5-to-3-page, single-column, A4, Australian-English external CV** (maintained in .docx for enterprise ATS portals and text-based .pdf for direct human sharing); a **skills-first LinkedIn profile** aligned to LinkedIn's Hiring Assistant 2; and a **SEEK Pass-verified SEEK profile** kept Hidden during your internal search and switched to Standard for external roles. Across Australia's major and challenger banks, recruitment now combines fragmented enterprise ATS parsers with semantic AI ranking amid record application volumes, while corporate restructures are compressing pure administrative management layers in favour of hands-on "player-coach" Product Owners and Delivery Leads who embed APRA CPS 230 operational resilience, scams prevention, and responsible AI into delivery.  
The recommendations below are grounded in live late-2026 bank careers portals, more than 50 live banking and fintech job listings, Australian regulator publications (APRA, ASIC, ACCC, OAIC), SEEK and LinkedIn product releases, and Australian HR research from the Australian HR Institute (AHRI), the Australian Human Rights Commission (AHRC), and Hays. Where vendor blogs, resume-service analyses, or third-party aggregators are cited, they are labelled as lower-grade sources, pre-2025 baseline evidence is flagged, and analytical inferences are marked explicitly.

#### **How Modern Bank ATS Parsers and AI Rankers Process Your CV**

Rather than running a mythical "auto-reject percentage bot," enterprise banking portals screen candidates across three sequential technical gates:

> 1. **Structured Field Parsing:** When you upload a CV to Workday or Oracle Recruiting Cloud, the parser extracts raw text into structured database fields—Name, Contact Details, Job Title, Employer, Dates, Education, and Skills ([ATSHiring, 14 Oct 2026 — CV vendor blog](https://www.atshiring.com/en/learn/workday-ats-guide-2025)42; [CVEdge, 2026 — CV vendor blog](https://www.thecvedge.com/blog/workday-resume-tips-2026)43). Because recruiters query those **parsed fields** when filtering large applicant pools, a two-column or table-based layout that drops dates or scrambles titles can hide a qualified candidate from search filters even though the original file remains attached ([CVEdge, 2026 — CV vendor blog](https://www.thecvedge.com/blog/workday-resume-tips-2026)43) *(Medium confidence)*. Always verify and manually repair every pre-filled Workday or Oracle field—particularly start/end months, the "I currently work here" toggle, and the searchable Skills field—before submitting ([ApplyGOAT, 27 Aug 2026 — CV vendor blog](https://applygoat.com/blogs/how-workday-applications-actually-work-in-2026-parsing-knockout-questions-and-faster-filling)44) *(High confidence)*.  
> 2. **Knockout Questions:** Automated elimination in Workday and Oracle happens primarily through structured **knockout questions** covering Australian working rights, location/hybrid availability, and minimum relevant years of experience, which either disqualify ineligible applicants or generate a numeric shortlist score that determines recruiter reading order ([ApplyGOAT, 27 Aug 2026 — CV vendor blog](https://applygoat.com/blogs/how-workday-applications-actually-work-in-2026-parsing-knockout-questions-and-faster-filling)44; [JobMentis, 15 May 2026 — CV vendor blog](https://www.jobmentis.com/en/guide/workday-ats)45) *(High confidence)*. Answer screening questions slowly and accurately rather than rushing them.  
> 3. **Semantic Matching and LLM-Generated Summaries:** Legacy exact-keyword counting has largely been superseded by semantic and LLM-assisted ranking across Eightfold AI (NAB), SAP Business AI (SuccessFactors), Oracle AI (Westpac/AMP), SEEK AI, and LinkedIn Hiring Assistant 2 ([SAP Community, 13 Aug 2025 — vendor product blog](https://community.sap.com/t5/human-capital-management-blog-posts-by-members/ai-applicant-screening-in-sap-successfactors-a-new-level-of-support-for/ba-p/14176313)46; [Bosley Australia / Eightfold, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.bosley.com.au/news/eightfold-partnership)32; [LinkedIn Newsroom, 29 Sep 2026](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/hiring-assistant-2)8; [CV People Australia, 2026 — CV service blog](https://www.cvpeople.com.au/post/ats-ai-generated-resumes)9) *(High confidence)*. SAP SuccessFactors' AI applicant screening extracts target skills from the job requisition, evaluates resumes for matching, related, and missing skills, and ranks candidates on a recruiter dashboard ([SAP Community, 13 Aug 2025 — vendor product blog](https://community.sap.com/t5/human-capital-management-blog-posts-by-members/ai-applicant-screening-in-sap-successfactors-a-new-level-of-support-for/ba-p/14176313)46). Enterprise add-on evaluators that integrate with Workday and SuccessFactors (such as SeekOut and HireLayer) go further, writing criterion-by-criterion match scores and plain-language evidence summaries directly into the recruiter's requisition view ([SeekOut, 2026 — vendor guide](https://www.seekout.com/guides/recruiting-tools-workday-sap-successfactors/)47; [HireLayer, accessed Nov 2026 — vendor docs](https://hirelayer.co/integrations/sap-successfactors)48). Because semantic models evaluate *proven context*, writing *"led a 12-person squad through a core payments migration"* signals leadership and technical delivery far more effectively than stuffing bare keywords into a sidebar ([CV People Australia, 2026 — CV service blog](https://www.cvpeople.com.au/post/ats-ai-generated-resumes)9).

### **2.2 Australian CV Conventions for Late 2026**

Australian recruitment uses "CV" and "resume" interchangeably outside academia, and departs sharply from both the US one-page norm and European photo-bearing CVs ([University of Queensland Resume Fact Sheet, accessed Nov 2026](https://employability.uq.edu.au/files/176803/Resume%20Fact%20Sheet%20%26%20Example.pdf)59; [ResumeStudio, 4 Nov 2026 — CV guide](https://resumestudio.io/blog/australian-resume)60) *(High confidence)*.  
On **length (2 pages versus 3 pages)**, Australian guidance splits along experience lines. SEEK Career Advice (July 2026\) and ANZHire advocate **two pages** as the disciplined baseline—arguing recruiters scan quickly and treat conciseness as a prioritisation signal—while SEEK explicitly allows highly experienced professionals to extend beyond two pages if every line adds concrete value ([SEEK Career Advice, 27 Jul 2026](https://au.seek.com/career-advice/article/how-long-should-a-resume-be)12; [ANZHire, 2026 — CV guide](https://www.anzhire.au/blog/australian-cv-format-2026)56). By contrast, Hays Australia, StrongHire Labs, and CrispResume recognise **two to three pages** as the standard range for mid-to-senior Australian professionals ([Hays Australia, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.hays.com.au/blog/insights/cv-writing-9-common-questions-answered)11; [StrongHire Labs Australia, accessed Nov 2026 — CV guide](https://www.stronghirelabs.com.au/blog/australian-resume-format-what-to-include-and-what-to-leave-out)53; [CrispResume Australia, 2026 — CV guide](https://crispresume.com.au/how-long-should-a-resume-be-in-australia/)57), and Macquarie restricts only *early-careers* applicants to two pages ([Macquarie Careers, accessed Nov 2026](https://recruitment.macquarie.com/en_US/careers/RegistrationMethods)58). **Recommendation:** Aim for **2.5 to 3 full A4 pages** (never trailing a few lines onto Page 4). Compressing 20+ years across Product Ownership, Delivery, and People Leadership into two pages forces out the quantified context that semantic ATS models and bank interview panels look for; instead, ensure Page 1 carries your complete value proposition (Contact Details, Summary, Key Skills, and Current Role). *(High confidence)*  
For **personal details, opening profile, and spelling**, place only your **Full Name** (never write "Resume" or "Curriculum Vitae" above it), **Mobile**, **Professional Email**, **Suburb/City and State** (e.g., Sydney, NSW—omitting your full street address for privacy), and **LinkedIn URL** at the top of Page 1 ([Hays Australia, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.hays.com.au/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/how-to-write-a-resume)61; [StrongHire Labs Australia, accessed Nov 2026 — CV guide](https://www.stronghirelabs.com.au/blog/australian-resume-format-what-to-include-and-what-to-leave-out)53) *(High confidence)*. **Strictly omit** a headshot, date of birth or age, marital status, gender, religion, nationality, health status, and salary expectations; age and marital status are protected attributes under the *Fair Work Act* covering prospective employees, so Australian employers actively prefer them left off ([StrongHire Labs Australia, accessed Nov 2026 — CV guide](https://www.stronghirelabs.com.au/blog/australian-resume-format-what-to-include-and-what-to-leave-out)53; [ResumeFast / UQ Guide, 2026 — CV guide](https://www.resumefast.io/blog/australian-resume-format)51) *(High confidence)*. Open with a tailored **3–5 line (50–150 word) Professional Summary** outlining your value proposition and target keywords rather than an outdated "Career Objective" ([Hays Australia, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.hays.com.au/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/how-to-write-a-resume)61; [ANZHire, 2026 — CV guide](https://www.anzhire.au/blog/australian-cv-format-2026)56) *(High confidence)*. Use consistent **Australian English** (organise, prioritise, analyse, optimise, behaviour, customised) by setting Word's language to *English (Australia)*, while preserving standard Australian banking usage of program for software and enterprise change programs ([Righteo Australia, 2026 — CV guide](https://www.righteo.com.au/post/australian-resume-format-2026)62; [ResumeAdapter, 8 Sep 2026 — CV guide](https://www.resumeadapter.com/blog/ats-resume-australia)52) *(High confidence)*.  
On **referees**, Australian advice also presents two options: some guides still suggest listing two named referees at the bottom of the CV ([ResumeStudio, 4 Nov 2026 — CV guide](https://resumestudio.io/blog/australian-resume)60; [ApplyVita, accessed Nov 2026 — CV guide](https://www.applyvita.com/resume-format-guide/australia)63), whereas SEEK's October 2026 reference guide and Righteo advise **omitting referee contact details** unless specifically requested, writing "Referees available on request" and keeping a separate formatted Referee Sheet ready for late-stage checks ([SEEK Career Advice, 28 Oct 2026](https://au.seek.com/career-advice/article/a-guide-to-resume-references)13; [Righteo Australia, 2026 — CV guide](https://www.righteo.com.au/post/australian-resume-format-2026)62). **Recommendation:** Use **Referees available on request** on all external CVs—publishing colleagues' phone numbers while still employed risks confidentiality leaks and premature calls—and always use the Australian term **"Referees"** rather than US "References" ([ResumeFast, 2026 — CV guide](https://www.resumefast.io/blog/australian-resume-format)51). *(High confidence)*  
For **work rights and banking probity checks**, Australian bank listings routinely specify "Australian working rights" (e.g., Judo Bank4) and enforce visa knockout questions inside ATS forms44. Adding a brief tag—Australian Citizen or Full Australian Working Rights—on your contact line is optional for citizens but removes any recruiter doubt ([CrispResume Australia, 2026 — CV guide](https://crispresume.com.au/best-investment-banking-resume-template/)65; [StrongHire Labs Australia, accessed Nov 2026 — CV guide](https://www.stronghirelabs.com.au/blog/australian-resume-format-what-to-include-and-what-to-leave-out)53) *(High confidence)*. Every Australian bank conducts mandatory conditional pre-employment checks after a verbal offer—typically identity verification, 5–7 years of employment history verification, financial-services **probity checks**, and National Police Checks (taking at least three weeks at CBA and run via third parties such as First Advantage at Westpac) ([CBA Application Support, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.commbank.com.au/about-us/careers/support.html)29; [Westpac Recruitment Process, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.westpac.com.au/about-westpac/careers/how-to-apply/recruitment-process/)66; [Final Round AI, 2026 — third-party guide](https://www.finalroundai.com/blog/commonwealth-bank-interview-process)67) *(High confidence)*. **(Inference):** Do not claim "probity cleared" or attach police checks to your CV, as every bank conducts fresh screening upon offer; instead, make sure your exact role titles and Month YYYY dates across your CV, LinkedIn, and SEEK match HR employment records so verification clears cleanly.

### **2.3 Structuring 20+ Years at One Employer and Countering Age Bias**

Two decades with one financial-services employer creates a specific narrative risk in late-2026 Australia: external hiring panels may quietly wonder whether your habits are locked into one bank's bureaucracy, while age-based recruitment biases narrow access for experienced women. Primary Australian workforce research shows this barrier is acute and measurable.  
In the joint **AHRI and Australian Human Rights Commission (AHRC)** *Older and Younger Workers Report* (July 2025), **24%** of Australian HR professionals classified workers aged **51–55** as "older"—more than double the **10%** recorded in 2023 (*pre-2025 baseline*)—while the proportion of HR professionals who hired any worker aged 55+ in the prior 12 months fell from **68% in 2023 to 57% in 2025** ([AHRI & AHRC, Jul 2025](https://www.ahri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/Older-Younger-Workers-Report-2025-FINAL.pdf)14) *(High confidence)*. Only 57% of HR respondents reported no organisational age reluctance threshold, 20% acknowledged their own recruitment practices negatively affect older applicants to some extent, and only 28% were open "to a large extent" to hiring over-65s ([AHRI & AHRC, Jul 2025](https://www.ahri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/Older-Younger-Workers-Report-2025-FINAL.pdf)14). The follow-up **AHRC and Diversity Council Australia** report (30 June 2026\) found that **older women** face the sharpest compounding exclusion: only **41%** had access to career development opportunities and just **13%** had access to mentors, making them the demographic least likely to receive workplace recognition ([AHRC & DCA, 30 Jun 2026](https://humanrights.gov.au/resource-hub/older-peoples-rights/age,-assumptions-and-access-at-work-employee-experiences-of-age-inclusionin-the-workplace)15; [AHRC Media Release, 30 Jun 2026](https://humanrights.gov.au/about-us/media-centre/media-releases/older-peoples-rights/age-bias-leaves-younger-workers-excluded-and-older-workers-overlooked)68) *(High confidence)*. Critically, the AHRI/AHRC data reveals *where* the stereotype sits: employers already rate older workers highly on **loyalty (74%), reliability (64%), experience (64%), professional knowledge (63%), and coping with stress (62%)**, while stereotyping younger workers as **tech-savvy, innovative, and offering fresh insights** ([AHRI & AHRC, Jul 2025](https://www.ahri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/Older-Younger-Workers-Report-2025-FINAL.pdf)14). Your CV does not need to prove loyalty—your tenure already does. Your CV must prove **modern digital fluency, agile adaptability, and AI-era innovation**.  
![][image1]  
To translate 20+ years at one bank into a compelling story of breadth and adaptability—without triggering parser errors or age-bias signals—apply four structural rules. First, **use a stacked (nested) employer layout on the visual CV, paired with discrete entries inside ATS forms**. Place the employer name and city once at the top of the section (plus a single line noting your promotions across divisions), then list each recent role underneath in reverse-chronological order with its own bold Job Title, Division/Domain, exact Mon YYYY – Mon YYYY dates, and tailored achievement bullets ([ResumeMate, accessed Nov 2026 — CV guide](https://www.resumemate.io/blog/list-multiple-roles-at-same-company-on-resume-examples/)69) *(High confidence)*. Because Workday and Oracle parsers can occasionally misattach dates when multiple titles sit beneath one employer header, manually split each recent role into its own form entry (repeating the exact employer spelling) whenever you review pre-filled ATS fields so recruiter filters calculate your Product Owner and People Leader tenure accurately ([ATSHiring, 14 Oct 2026 — CV vendor blog](https://www.atshiring.com/en/learn/workday-ats-guide-2025)42; [CVEdge, 2026 — CV vendor blog](https://www.thecvedge.com/blog/workday-resume-tips-2026)43) *(Medium confidence)*.  
Second, **detail only the last 10 to 15 years and condense earlier roles truthfully**. Australian 2026 CV guidance recommends focusing detailed bullet points on the most recent 10–15 years—typically your three to four most recent substantive roles, with 4–6 bullets for your current role and 3–4 for earlier roles ([Righteo Australia, 2026 — CV guide](https://www.righteo.com.au/post/australian-resume-format-2026)62; [ANZHire, 2026 — CV guide](https://www.anzhire.au/blog/australian-cv-format-2026)56) *(High confidence)*. Group roles older than roughly 12–15 years into a compact 2–3 line **"Earlier Career Progression at \[Employer\]"** subsection naming the domains and foundational roles without early-2000s start dates on your external CV. This remains completely honest, avoids any discrepancy during 5–7 year bank employment verification67, and concentrates reader attention on your modern agile and leadership impact.  
Third, **strip unnecessary age markers while dating modern learning**. Consistent with AHRI and AHRC's explicit recommendation to remove unnecessary age proxies such as dates of birth, graduation years, and raw years-of-experience fields ([AHRI & AHRC, Jul 2025](https://www.ahri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/Older-Younger-Workers-Report-2025-FINAL.pdf)14) *(High confidence)*, omit graduation years from university degrees completed more than 10–15 years ago, while prominently dating **2024–2026 certifications and short courses** (AI fluency, Agile/PO credentials, CPS 230 risk workshops) to signal continuous reinvention ([Righteo Australia, 2026 — CV guide](https://www.righteo.com.au/post/australian-resume-format-2026)62). **(Inference):** Open your summary with *"15+ years of progressive delivery, product ownership, and people leadership experience"* rather than *"20+ years with one bank"*—strictly accurate ("15+" includes 20+), while anchoring the reader on senior leadership rather than tenure length.  
Fourth, **frame internal moves as distinct mini-careers**. Explicitly highlight how many operating models, customer segments, and technology eras you have transitioned across—from frontline or operations leadership into agile squads, digital product ownership, and GenAI-enabled workflows—calling out every promotion and expansion of squad, budget, or regulatory scope.

### **2.4 Product Owner, Delivery Lead, and People Leadership in Australian Banking (2025–2026)**

Analysis of over 50 live late-2026 banking and fintech listings (including more than 25 detailed role excerpts across CBA, Westpac, NAB, ANZ, Macquarie, AMP, Bendigo/Up, Judo, and Australian Payments Plus) alongside 2025–2026 APRA, ASIC, and OAIC obligations reveals four sharp shifts in what Australian banks prioritise when recruiting Product Owners, Delivery Leads, and People Leaders.  
First, **operating-model vocabulary varies meaningfully by bank and should be mirrored per application**. **CBA** structures delivery around **Squads, Crews, Domains, and Chapters**, hiring *Product Owners*, *Senior Product Owners*, *Executive Product Owners*, *Product Leads*, *Product Stream Leads*, and *Chapter Leads* who partner with *Crew Tech Leads* and *L1 Risk* ([CBA Senior Product Lead, Nov 2026](https://cba.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/CommBank_Careers/job/Senior-Product-Lead_REQ267800)70; [CBA Senior PO, Nov 2026](https://cba.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/CommBank_Careers/job/Senior-Product-Owner_REQ264797)19) *(High confidence)*. **ANZ** built Australia's benchmark **Tribes, Squads, and Chapters** agile model (*pre-2025 baseline*, [ANZ Bluenotes, Aug 2021](https://www.anz.com.au/bluenotes/2021/08/anz-agile-nwow-transformation-shayne-florian/)71) and in November 2026 actively hires **Agile Delivery Leads** alongside digital and retail operational risk leads ([ANZ Careers, Nov 2026](https://careers.anz.com/go/ANZ-Jobs-List/4739210/)37) *(High confidence)*. **Westpac** anchors transformation around its **UNITE** (business-led, tech-enabled simplification) and **Catalyst** programs, classifying roles across *Product Ownership*, *Product Management*, *Agility*, *Transformation & Agility*, and *Delivery* ([Westpac Careers, Nov 2026](https://ebuu.fa.ap1.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX/jobs)2; [Westpac UNITE, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.westpac.com.au/about-westpac/careers/)72) *(High confidence)*. **NAB** centres its Product Owner roles on representing the *"voice of the customer"*, leading discovery, and owning domain vision and roadmaps ([NAB Eightfold, accessed Nov 2026](https://nab.eightfold.ai/careers/job/563980770280116)73) *(High confidence)*.  
Second, **binding 2025–2027 regulatory deadlines have made "controls by design" a core Product Owner and Delivery capability**. Senior bank PO postings consistently pair commercial delivery with *"security, privacy, resilience, compliance, and controls by design"* in complex regulated environments ([CBA REQ264797, Nov 2026](https://cba.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/CommBank_Careers/job/Senior-Product-Owner_REQ264797)19; [CBA REQ268168, Nov 2026](https://cba.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/CommBank_Careers/job/Product-Lead--Deposits_REQ268168)26) *(High confidence)*. Four statutory pillars dominate 2026 banking backlogs: **APRA CPS 230 (Operational Risk Management)**—which commenced 1 July 2025 and entered **full scope on 1 July 2026** (incorporating pre-existing Material Service Provider contracts and APRA's 30 April 2026 CPG 230 updates)—requires registers of critical operations, Board-approved tolerance levels, severe-but-plausible scenario testing, BCP/disaster recovery, and strict incident notification clocks (24 hours for critical-operation tolerance breaches and 72 hours for material operational risk incidents) ([APRA CPS 230, 30 Apr 2026](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/cps-230)17; [Drova, 2026](https://www.drova.com/resources/operational-resilience-apra-guide)76; [Interactive, 2026](https://www.interactive.com.au/insights/a-practical-guide-to-cps-230/)77) *(High confidence)*. **APRA CPS 234 (Information Security)**—in force since 1 July 2019 (*pre-2025 baseline*)—faces intensified 2025–2026 supervision across third-party asset classification, MFA/privileged access controls, executive accountability (including APRA's June 2025 letter to RSE licensees requiring explicit FAR Accountable Persons), and pre-deployment CPS 234 impact reviews for AI tools ([Cybernion, 2026](https://cybernion.com.au/insights/apra-cps-234-guide/)78; [Cliffside, 2026](https://www.cliffside.com.au/insights/apra-cps-234-compliance-guide/)79; [Otto IT, 2026](https://www.ottoit.com.au/blog/apra-cps-234-explained-financial-services-australia/)80) *(High confidence)*. Finally, ASIC's **Design and Distribution Obligations (DDO)** require **Target Market Determinations (TMDs)**, 10-business-day significant-dealing reporting, and 7-year distribution record-keeping across lending, credit, deposit, insurance, and super products ([ASIC DDO FAQs, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/financial-services/giving-financial-product-advice/faqs-design-and-distribution-obligations-for-advice-licensees-and-financial-advisers)84) *(High confidence)*.

SEEK Chief Economist data (November 2026\) documents a **73% year-on-year surge** in Australian job ads mentioning AI skills even as overall ad volume softened ([Dynamic Business / SEEK, 18 Nov 2026](https://dynamicbusiness.com/news/what-seek-s-new-jobs-data-reveals-about-which-roles-ai-is-taking)6) *(High confidence)*. Showing practical AI workflow adoption directly counters age-based assumptions about technical currency.

### **2.5 AI-Written CVs: Detection, Penalties, and Keeping a Human Voice**

No primary evidence indicates that Australian bank ATS platforms run automated "AI-text detectors" that auto-reject CVs written with ChatGPT or Claude *(High confidence)*. Instead, AI-written CVs suffer a severe dual penalty in late 2026 driven by **application-volume overload, semantic scoring mechanics, and human recruiter fatigue**.  
SEEK data shows **25%** of Australian candidates already use AI tools to write resumes and cover letters ([SEEK Career Advice, 2026](https://au.seek.com/career-advice/article/5-workplace-trends-to-watch)7), while AHRI cites Robert Walters ANZ research finding that **over half of jobseekers in Australia and New Zealand apply to 20 or more roles at a time** using AI tools ([AHRI, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.ahri.com.au/articles/responding-to-ai-generated-applications)89) *(High confidence)*. Combined with cooling job-ad volumes, an Australian resume-writing analysis reported average Australian applications per ad surged to **184 in April 2025** (up from \~41 in late 2024), and SEEK's Chief Economist confirmed applications per ad climbed for eight consecutive months through September 2026 to new record highs ([The Resume Writers Australia, 4 Nov 2025 — CV service blog](https://theresumewriters.com.au/why-recruiters-are-facing-a-surge-in-job-applications/)90; [Dynamic Business / SEEK, 18 Nov 2026](https://dynamicbusiness.com/news/what-seek-s-new-jobs-data-reveals-about-which-roles-ai-is-taking)6) *(High confidence for trend; Medium for 184 exact figure from secondary blog)*.

That flood has triggered acute "AI fatigue" among recruiters ([SEEK Career Advice, 2026](https://au.seek.com/career-advice/article/5-workplace-trends-to-watch)7). Employer survey data cited by CV People Australia—drawing on a March 2026 Robert Half survey of over 2,000 hiring managers and Resume Vera polling—indicates **84%** of HR teams carry heavier workloads from AI applications, **67%** say reviewing them slowed hiring, **65%** report candidates' real skills are harder to verify, and **62%** reject unpersonalised AI-generated resumes ([CV People Australia, 2026 — CV service blog](https://www.cvpeople.com.au/post/ats-ai-generated-resumes)9) *(Medium-High confidence; international/cross-market employer surveys cited via an Australian CV service)*. Ban the 2026 AI cliché register (*spearheaded, leveraged, synergised, results-driven, operational excellence*) in favour of plain, verifiable Australian verbs (*led, delivered, reduced, migrated, embedded, coached, resolved*) anchored to real banking outcomes ([ApplyVita, accessed Nov 2026 — CV guide](https://www.applyvita.com/resume-format-guide/australia)63).

### **2.6 Internal Transfers at Large Australian Banks**

Because an internal move is your **Priority 1**, treat it as a structured, formal campaign rather than an informal favour. Large Australian banks run internal job boards directly inside their enterprise HRIS/ATS: CBA explicitly stipulates that existing Group employees (including Bankwest and x15ventures) **must apply via Sidekick** to submit a valid application ([CBA Careers, Nov 2026](https://cba.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/CommBank_Careers/job/Customer-Banking-Specialist---Randwick_REQ268712)20; [CBA Career Pathways, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.commbank.com.au/articles/careers/career-pathways-at-commbank.html)93); AMP routes staff through "I am an employee" inside Oracle Recruiting Cloud5; ANZ and Bendigo use dedicated "Employee Login" portals37; Macquarie's Avature tracks internal and redeployment channels separately34; and NAB's Eightfold AI supports internal skills-based mobility ([Bosley Australia / Eightfold, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.bosley.com.au/news/eightfold-partnership)32) *(High confidence)*.  
Even when you know the hiring manager, internal applications pass through the same ATS workflow for compliance, levelling, and equal-consideration auditing ([YesWriting, accessed Nov 2026 — CV guide](https://www.yeswriting.com/resume-for-internal-promotion/)95; [CVERA, accessed Nov 2026 — CV guide](https://cv-era.com/blog/en/how-to-write-a-resume-for-an-internal-promotion-or-transfer)96). Moreover, during late-2026 restructures, displaced staff inside formal **redeployment pools** (such as ANZ's 6-week redeployment period or NAB's On Deployment period) frequently hold priority internal matching rights ([FairWork Mate, 3 Oct 2026](https://fairworkmate.com.au/blog/bank-redundancy-packages-2026-cba-anz-nab-westpac-bendigo)21) *(High confidence)*. Your optimal sequence is therefore **manager-conversation-first, portal-submission-second**: request a 15-minute exploratory chat with the target hiring manager (or a peer in that tribe) to clarify team pain points and whether redeployment pools apply before submitting your formal internal CV ([SEEK Career Advice, accessed Nov 2026](https://au.seek.com/career-advice/article/what-to-do-when-youre-gunning-for-an-internal-position)22; [Monster Career Advice, accessed Nov 2026 — CV guide](https://www.monster.com/career-advice/resume/resume-for-internal-position)97).

#### **LinkedIn Strategy for Late 2026**

On 29 September 2026, LinkedIn announced **Hiring Assistant 2** (rolling out globally in English from November 2026), shifting recruiter discovery away from rigid job-title filters toward **skills, capabilities, and AI-evaluated fit signals** ([LinkedIn Newsroom, 29 Sep 2026](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/hiring-assistant-2)8) *(High confidence)*. Write your **About** section in warm, direct first person—opening with a two-sentence positioning hook, highlighting 4–5 quantified results spanning Product Ownership, Delivery, and People Leadership, and closing with a natural **Core Capabilities** cluster so AI agents parse your full breadth ([Coursera, 30 Dec 2025](https://www.coursera.org/articles/how-to-let-recruiters-know-youre-open-on-linkedin)23). ## **7\. Actionable Implementation Checklists**

### **(a) ATS and AI-Safe Formatting Checklist**

* \[ \] **Page size & length:** A4 paper (not US Letter), **2.5 to 3 full pages** (no awkward 3-line spill onto Page 4).  
* \[ \] **Layout purity:** Strict single-column layout; zero tables (including borderless layout tables), zero text boxes, zero sidebars, zero icons/graphics/skill bars, zero photo.  
* \[ \] **Contact details placement:** Name, Suburb/State, mobile, email, and LinkedIn URL placed in the **main body** at the top of Page 1—never inside Word's Header or Footer.  
* \[ \] **Standard headings:** Exact conventional headings (Professional Summary, Key Skills, Professional Experience, Education & Certifications, Referees).  
* \[ \] **Dates & typography:** Consistent Mon YYYY – Mon YYYY format on every detailed role; standard round (•) bullets; clean font (Calibri, Arial, or Aptos) at 10.5–11pt body.  
* \[ \] **Dual export & plain-text test:** Master saved in **.docx** (for Workday, Oracle, SuccessFactors, Eightfold uploads) and **"Save As PDF"** (for direct human email); Ctrl+A → Ctrl+C → paste into Notepad confirms clean linear text order.  
* \[ \] **Portal verification routine:** After every Workday or Oracle upload, manually check and repair auto-populated dates, job titles, the "I currently work here" toggle, and the searchable Skills field before submitting.  
* \[ \] **Australian English & privacy hygiene:** Word proofing set to *English (Australia)* (organise, prioritise, analyse, optimise, behaviour); zero DOB, age, photo, marital status, or full street address.

### **(b) Internal-Application Version Checklist (Phase 1\)**

* \[ \] **Internal portal confirmed:** Submitting exclusively via your bank's internal mobility portal (e.g., Sidekick, Employee Login, internal Workday/Eightfold/Oracle) rather than the external site.  
* \[ \] **Current manager briefed proactively:** Checked internal mobility policy; held a constructive, retention-focused conversation with your current manager before applying or interviewing.  
* \[ \] **Hiring manager coffee chat completed:** Spoke informally with the target hiring manager (or a peer in that tribe/department) to confirm team priorities and whether redeployment pools apply.  
* \[ \] **Institutional leverage foregrounded:** Named specific internal systems, transformation programs, Line 1 risk forums, committees, and internal leadership academies proving zero ramp-up time.  
* \[ \] **Zero assumption of familiarity:** Spelled out team sizes, budgets, metrics, and role scope in full—treating the panel as if they sit in a separate division.  
* \[ \] **Internal referees briefed:** Lined up 2–3 internal advocates (current manager plus a cross-functional stakeholder or previous leader).  
* \[ \] **External visibility locked down:** LinkedIn Open to Work set to **Off** (or Visible only to you); SEEK profile visibility set to **Hidden**.

### **(c) External-Application Version Checklist (Phase 2\)**

* \[ \] **Confidentiality sanitised:** Stripped proprietary internal project code names, unreleased product roadmaps, and confidential data (retaining accurate percentages/scales and standard banking terms).  
* \[ \] **Internal jargon translated:** Replaced employer-specific band/grade codes and internal acronyms with market-standard Australian banking titles and frameworks.  
* \[ \] **Age-bias hygiene applied:** Detailed last 10–15 years only; earlier roles condensed into a 2–3 line "Earlier Career Progression" block; university graduation year omitted; 2024–2026 certifications/AI upskilling prominently dated.  
* \[ \] **Bank-specific tailoring completed:** Adjusted Summary, Key Skills, and top 3–4 bullets to reflect the target bank's operating model (e.g., CBA *Crew/Squad/Controls by Design*, Westpac *UNITE*, ANZ *Agile Delivery Lead*, Judo *SME commercial acumen*, AP+/Revolut *Fraud & Scams*).  
* \[ \] **Referees protected:** States "Referees available on request" only; separate 1-page Referee Sheet kept offline until late-stage checks.  
* \[ \] **Human voice verified:** Read aloud to strip AI clichés (*spearheaded, leveraged, results-driven, operational excellence*) and confirm every bullet has a ready STAR example for panel or video interviews.  
* \[ \] **Direct portal submission prioritised:** Applied directly through the target bank's ATS rather than relying on LinkedIn Easy Apply or automated bulk-apply bots.

### **(d) LinkedIn and SEEK Alignment Checklist**

* \[ \] **Exact factual consistency:** Employer name, core role titles, and Month YYYY dates match identically across CV, LinkedIn, and SEEK (ready for 5–7 year bank probity verification).  
* \[ \] **LinkedIn Headline & About tuned for Hiring Assistant 2:** Headline combines target titles \+ banking domain \+ 2026 differentiators; About written in first person with 4–5 quantified results and a natural skills cluster.  
* \[ \] **LinkedIn Skills & Company Page linked:** 15–25 role-relevant skills mapped to recent positions; current role linked to your employer's **official LinkedIn company page**; current headshot uploaded.  
* \[ \] **LinkedIn Open to Work timed carefully:** Never use public green \#OpenToWork banner while employed; activate **Recruiters only** only in Phase 2 (after profile polish is finished), noting LinkedIn does not 100% guarantee employer invisibility.  
* \[ \] **SEEK Profile complete & verified:** All structured career-history and skills fields completed; identity and **Australian Right to Work** verified via **SEEK Pass**.  
* \[ \] **SEEK visibility & dual CVs configured:** Switched from **Hidden** (Phase 1\) to **Standard** \+ **"Show my approachability"** (Phase 2); two tailored .docx CVs uploaded (PO track \+ Delivery/People Leader track); **3 monthly "Show Strong Interest"** tokens saved for highest-priority bank applications.
