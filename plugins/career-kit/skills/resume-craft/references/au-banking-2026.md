# Australian banking, late 2026

The one market this skill has research for. Read it when the person is applying to an
Australian bank or financial-services employer. Every line is from six research reports with a
cut-off of 19 to 20 November 2026, held in `../../../docs/deep-research/`; hiring systems and ads
change, so re-check anything a decision rests on.

## Conventions

| Item | Convention | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | Two pages; a third only for evidence a role asks for | Hays says about two; SEEK allows two to five for senior people; the reports recommend between two and three |
| Paper | A4 | |
| Spelling | Australian: organisation, prioritise, behaviour | An exact-term search may not match an American variant |
| Personal details | Name, city and state, mobile, email, one web address | No photo, date of birth, marital status or street address |
| Work rights | "Australian citizen" or the true status on the contact line | Employers filter on it; complete the same field on SEEK and on forms |
| Referees | Left off; "Referees available on request" is acceptable | Use "referees", the local word. Internally, expect the current manager to be asked |
| Summary | 60 to 100 words | Hays gives 50 to 150 |
| Background checks | Say nothing about being "cleared" | Banks run their own checks at offer; an industry conduct-check protocol reaches back up to five years, so dates and titles have to match the employer's record |
| File | Word to bank portals, text-based PDF to people | One system's vendor lists DOCX and not PDF for parsing |

## Which system each employer runs

Checked against live careers sites on 20 November 2026. The system says how a file is parsed;
no report could confirm which ranking or AI features any bank has switched on.

| Employer | System |
| --- | --- |
| CBA | Workday. Existing staff must apply through the internal portal |
| Westpac | Oracle Recruiting Cloud for external applicants; Oracle Taleo for internal staff, where recruiters can search exact terms |
| NAB | PageUp for Australian roles; an Eightfold AI portal also exists |
| ANZ | SAP SuccessFactors |
| Macquarie | Avature (inferred from page markup) |
| AMP | Oracle Recruiting Cloud |
| ING Australia | Workday |
| Bendigo and Adelaide | SAP SuccessFactors; assessment games for some roles |
| Up (Ferocia) | Reports disagree: Greenhouse on one check, Ashby on another. Check the vacancy's own link |
| Judo | SmartRecruiters |

What the documented tools do with a résumé: parse it into fields; apply screening questions on
work rights, location and experience; match it to the ad (one grades A to D on required
qualifications, one scores skills on all roles and again on recent roles only, one lets a
recruiter search exact terms); and sometimes show a recruiter a generated summary. No report
found a bank that auto-rejects on a score.

## What the ads ask for

Two counted samples of product, delivery and people-leader ads (7 and 16 ads), small and skewed
to three banks:

| Theme | Sample of 7 | Sample of 16 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stakeholders | 7 | 16 |
| Customer or outcomes | 6 | 16 |
| Risk, governance, compliance | 6 | 16 for risk; 8 each for compliance and governance |
| Data and measurement | 6 | 11 |
| Prioritisation | not counted | 11 |
| Roadmap | 5 | 7 |
| Dependencies | not counted | 9 |
| Discovery | 4 | 6 |
| Backlog, agile, squad | 3 | 5 each |
| AI or automation | 3 | 2 to 3 |
| Jira | 1 | 2 |
| A named certification | 0 | 2, as "advantageous" |
| A prudential standard by name (CPS 230, CPS 234) | 0 | 0 |

So: show decisions, customers, risk and results in context. A certificate is supporting
evidence and no ad sampled made one a hurdle. Name a regulation only inside an achievement the
person actually delivered; the ads say "risk", "controls" and "operational resilience".

Operating-model words differ by bank, and each belongs only in an application to that bank:
CBA uses domain, crew, squad and chapter; Westpac uses squad, Release Train and Chief Product
Owner; ANZ uses squads, value streams and showcases; ING calls a product manager a Customer
Journey Expert.

## Age and long tenure

- AHRI and the Australian Human Rights Commission, July 2025 (148 HR professionals): 24% call
  workers aged 51 to 55 "older", up from 10% in 2023; 56% are open "to a large extent" to hiring
  people aged 50 to 64, against 80% for those aged 25 to 34.
- The same survey has employers rating older workers highly on loyalty (74%), reliability and
  experience (64% each), and attributing tech-savviness and innovation to younger ones. The
  résumé therefore has to show current tools, current methods and recent results.
- Studies of whether AI screening disadvantages older candidates conflict. Graduation year is
  the main age proxy a machine reads.

## Internal moves

- The internal portal is mandatory where one exists; an application through the public site may
  not count.
- The same parsing applies. What differs is that the hiring side can see or ask about the
  person's record.
- Tenure and manager-notification rules are on each employer's intranet and were not public.
  One bank publishes a normal 12 months in role before moving.
- The reports agree on telling the current manager before or when applying, and one says to
  check the policy first.

## Where the reports disagree

| Question | Positions | What this skill does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | Two, "two with a third if needed", 2.5 to 3, three | Two, a third for evidence |
| A count of years in the summary | One report suggests "15+ years"; two say no count | No count |
| DOCX or PDF | Two say DOCX to portals; two say keep both and follow the form | DOCX to portals unless told otherwise |
| Dates on early roles | One suggests dropping pre-2011 dates externally; others keep true dates | Keep dates on roles listed in full; one undated "Earlier roles" line is acceptable |
| Naming regulations | Two reports promote naming them; two say only where the work was done | Only inside a true achievement |
| How many employers screen with AI | 72% from a job board's survey via secondary sources; 17% from AHRI's own survey | Prefer the primary source |
