# Review: Hannah Okoye's résumé against the Lantern Bank Product Owner, Card Disputes ad

Reviewed 21 November 2026. Applications close 5 December 2026.

Sources: `old-resume.md` (the résumé), `facts.md` (what Hannah has confirmed), `ad.md` (the job). Anything not in `facts.md` is treated as unknown.

## Verdict

Do not send this résumé as it stands. Hannah is a credible candidate for this role, but the document has three problems that need fixing first:

1. **Six of its eight bullets are inaccurate, overstated or misplaced**, and one of them discloses a figure Hannah has said must not leave Kestrel Bank.
2. **It hides her best evidence.** The single "2009 – Present" block with three titles on one line buries four promotions and role changes, about 14 years in card disputes, and the one story that directly answers the ad's "backlog prioritisation using data" requirement.
3. **It carries personal details that should not be on an Australian résumé** (date of birth, marital status, street address).

She meets four of the five required criteria on the confirmed facts. She does not meet the fifth, card scheme mandate delivery, and the résumé should not be stretched to suggest she does.

## Fix before anything else

### The chargeback-loss figure must come off

The last bullet says she "saved the bank" a dollar amount a year in chargeback losses. Two separate problems:

- **Confidentiality.** Hannah has said this figure is internal only and she does not want it on anything that leaves Kestrel Bank. A résumé sent to Lantern Bank, a competitor, is exactly that.
- **Accuracy.** The figure is what card disputes *cost* Kestrel in chargeback losses each year. It is not a saving, and nothing in the confirmed facts says she reduced it.

Remove the bullet. There is no accurate version of it that can be kept.

It hides the progression, which is one of the best things about her history, and it makes it impossible to tell how long she has been a Product Owner, which is the first thing this ad asks. It also omits two roles entirely. Replace it with one employer heading and five dated roles:

| Title | Dates |

| Operations Manager, Payments Disputes | Mar 2020 – Aug 2024 |
| Team Leader, Disputes Operations | Sep 2015 – Mar 2020 |

Give the Product Owner role the most space, the Operations Manager role a few lines, and the three earlier roles a line or two each.

**Personal details.** Remove date of birth and marital status. Neither is relevant and both invite the kind of bias employers are legally meant to avoid. Replace the street address with "Brisbane, QLD". Add her LinkedIn URL (linkedin.com/in/hannah-okoye-example), which is confirmed and missing. "Australian citizen" is confirmed and worth one line, since a bank will check work rights.

**Title.** "Hannah Okoye — Curriculum Vitae" can simply be her name.

**Career objective.** "Results-driven … seeking a challenging Product Owner role where I can leverage my skills" says nothing specific to her and could open anyone's résumé. Replace it with two or three lines of fact: what she does now, how long she has worked in disputes, and why that combination suits this job.

**Skills.** The list is bare nouns ("Stakeholders", "Risk", "Leadership", "Communication") that a reader cannot verify. Keep the concrete ones and let the role bullets carry the rest. Add Confluence, which the ad lists as desirable and she uses, and Power BI. "Risk" has no confirmed fact behind it; see the questions at the end.

**Education.** The heading is empty, which looks like an oversight. She has no formal qualifications, so remove the heading and add a short "Training" section: Product Fundamentals, Kestrel Bank internal course, 2025. Describe it as an internal course. **Referees.** The résumé prints a named referee, the Head of Payments Product at Kestrel, with a mobile number and email. Hannah reports to the Head of Payments Product, so this is her current manager. The confirmed facts do not say this person has agreed to be a referee or knows she is applying elsewhere. ## What the résumé leaves out

These are confirmed and absent, and several are stronger than anything currently on the page:

- **The prioritisation story.** Case data showed staff were retyping merchant details on about 70% of cases. She moved a change that auto-fills merchant details ahead of a dashboard that managers had asked for. In the quarter after release, average handling time fell from 18 minutes to 14 minutes. The résumé keeps only the last clause. The full version is direct evidence for "backlog prioritisation using data" and for saying no to stakeholders with a reason.
- **User stories and acceptance criteria, 2-week sprints.** The ad asks for exactly this and the résumé never says she does it.
- **Beacon's scale.** About 140 staff use the tool she owns the backlog for.
- **The ageing-disputes report.** As Operations Manager she introduced a daily report of ageing disputes; disputes older than 45 days fell from about 310 to about 90 over 6 months. This is a measured result and it is missing, while an unmeasured one (rosters) is included.
- **Operational scale.** About 2,400 disputes a month, 31 staff across 2 sites, 3 team leaders reporting to her.
- **Developing people.** As Team Leader she had 9 direct reports and coached 2 disputes officers into team leader roles.
- **Scheme familiarity.** As an operator she worked within Visa and Mastercard chargeback timeframes.

## Questions for Hannah

Answers to these could close gaps in the table above. Until she confirms them, none of it should go on the résumé.

1. **Risk.** Has she worked with Kestrel's Risk or Compliance teams on Beacon changes or as Operations Manager, for example on controls or sign-off? The ad names risk twice.
2. **Discovery.** Does she run interviews, observation sessions or workshops with disputes staff for Beacon? Has she had any contact with customer research or complaints data?
3. **Accepting work.** Does she accept or reject stories at the end of each sprint?
4. **Mandates.** Has she been on the receiving end of a scheme mandate change, such as updating procedures or training when Visa or Mastercard rules changed? That would not meet the requirement, but it would be accurate adjacent experience worth one line.
5. **Referee.** Does the Head of Payments Product know she is applying, and have they agreed to be named?
6. **Earlier roles.** Is there anything worth a line from her Disputes Officer or Customer Service Officer years?
7. **Certification.** With two weeks to the closing date, a short agile or product owner certification is possible but could only be listed as in progress or completed if it actually is.
