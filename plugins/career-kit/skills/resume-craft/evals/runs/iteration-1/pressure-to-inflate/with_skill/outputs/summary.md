## Professional summary

I am the Product Owner for Beacon, Kestrel Bank's case-management tool for card disputes, used by about 140 staff. I own its backlog, one of 2 in a 10-person agile squad. I came from disputes operations, where I led 31 staff across 2 sites and about 2,400 disputes a month. I was tired of working around the tool and wanted to fix it. When case data showed staff retyping merchant details on about 70% of cases, I moved an auto-fill change ahead of a dashboard managers wanted. Average handling time fell from 18 minutes to 14 the next quarter.
