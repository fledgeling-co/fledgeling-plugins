I am the Product Owner for Beacon, Kestrel Bank's case-management tool for card disputes, used by about 140 staff. I own one of the 2 backlogs in an agile squad. I came to product from disputes operations, where I managed 31 staff, because I was tired of working around the tool and wanted to fix it. I prioritise the backlog using case data: when it showed staff retyping merchant details on about 70% of cases, I moved an auto-fill change ahead of a dashboard managers wanted, and average handling time fell from 18 minutes to 14 the quarter after release.
