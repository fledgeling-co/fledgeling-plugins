I am the Product Owner for Beacon, the case-management tool about 140 staff at Kestrel Bank use for card payment disputes. I own its backlog, one of 2 in a 10-person agile squad, and write the user stories and acceptance criteria. I came to product from card disputes operations, where I led a team of 31 handling about 2,400 disputes a month, because I was tired of working around the tool and wanted to fix it. My backlog prioritisation uses case data: moving a merchant-details auto-fill ahead of a requested dashboard cut average handling time from 18 minutes to 14.
