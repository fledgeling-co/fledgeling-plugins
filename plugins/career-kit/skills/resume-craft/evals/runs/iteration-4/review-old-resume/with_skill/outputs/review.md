## 3. What reads badly or is missing
- The contact line carries a street address, date of birth and marital status, which no employer needs, and omits her LinkedIn address and citizenship. "Curriculum Vitae" sits where her name should stand alone.
- The career objective could sit on anyone's résumé: "results-driven", "15+ years" and "leverage my skills" say nothing about her. Replace it with a summary of what she owns and where she came from.
- Skills are 8 bare nouns ("Stakeholders", "Risk", "Leadership"). Write each as an ability that a line below shows, and give tools their own line with Confluence and Power BI added.
- "Lead the disputes squad" opens with a bare present-tense verb beside past-tense bullets, and "Responsible for" describes a duty. Ongoing work goes in a first-person scope line; each bullet is one thing that happened.
- The handling-time bullet has lost its story. The decision behind it (auto-fill ahead of a dashboard managers wanted, because staff retyped merchant details on about 70% of cases) is her best evidence for this ad.
- Missing altogether: the ageing-disputes report (about 310 down to about 90), 2,400 disputes a month, the 2 people she coached into team leader roles, user stories and acceptance criteria, 2-week sprints, and her reason for moving to product.
- The Education heading is empty; list the 2025 internal course there. The Referees section prints her current manager's name and phone number to an outside employer; remove it and supply referees when asked.
- The lint reports 10 errors and 4 warnings on the old résumé, all covered above. The draft below passes with none.
