I am the Product Owner for Beacon, Kestrel Bank's case-management tool for card payment disputes, used by about 140 staff. I own one of two backlogs in a 10-person agile squad and write its user stories and acceptance criteria. I came to product from card disputes operations, where I managed 31 staff handling about 2,400 disputes a month, because I was tired of working around the tool and wanted to fix it. I prioritise using case data; after one such call, average handling time fell from 18 minutes to 14.
