I am the Product Owner for Beacon, Kestrel Bank's case-management tool for card disputes, used by about 140 staff. I own its backlog, one of 2 in a 10-person agile squad, and write the user stories and acceptance criteria. I came to product from chargeback operations, where I managed 31 staff handling about 2,400 disputes a month, to fix the tool I had been working around. I put a merchant auto-fill ahead of a dashboard managers wanted when case data showed staff retyping merchant details on about 70% of cases.
