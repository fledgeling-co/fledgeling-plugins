## 3. What reads badly or is missing

- The contact line carries a street address, date of birth and marital status, and omits her LinkedIn address and "Australian citizen". Cut the first three and add the last two.
- "Career objective" is a wish, a count of years and "results-driven"; it could sit on anyone's résumé. Replace it with a summary that says what she owns and why she moved.
- "Lead the disputes squad" opens with a bare present-tense verb beside past-tense bullets, and "Responsible for managing a team of 31" is a duty from a role that ended in August 2024, shown as current.
- "Reduced handling time from 18 minutes to 14 minutes" is her best result with its cause removed. The decision behind it (auto-fill ahead of a requested dashboard, on case data) is the proof this ad wants.
- Skills are 8 nouns. "Risk", "Leadership" and "Communication" have nothing under them; write abilities that a line below shows, with tools on their own line.
- Missing entirely: the two promotions, the ageing-disputes report (about 310 down to about 90), 2,400 disputes a month, the coaching, Beacon by name, and Confluence and Power BI. The Education heading is empty and a named referee's phone and email are printed; drop both. The lint reports 10 errors and 4 warnings on the file, all covered above.
