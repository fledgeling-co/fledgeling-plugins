# Product Owner, Card Disputes — Lantern Bank (Sydney or Brisbane)

Lantern Bank is rebuilding how customers dispute a card transaction. You will own the backlog for our disputes squad.

## You will
- Own and prioritise the product backlog for the disputes squad, balancing customer impact, risk and effort.
- Lead discovery with frontline disputes staff and customers.
- Write user stories and acceptance criteria and accept work each sprint.
- Work with Risk and Operations so that controls are built into each change.

## Required
- Experience as a Product Owner in an agile squad.
- Backlog prioritisation using data.
- Card disputes or chargeback operations experience.
- Experience delivering card scheme mandate changes (Visa or Mastercard).
- Stakeholder management across operations and risk.

## Desirable
- A product or agile certification.
- Jira and Confluence.

Applications close 5 December 2026. Apply through the Lantern Bank careers site.
