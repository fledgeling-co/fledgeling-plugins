**Ships.** The layout is the family's banner system, taken from home-craft's `banner-src.html`: Instrument Sans wordmark, IBM Plex Mono labels, the porcelain ramp at 141 degrees and one ember. The device on the right is the icon's ladder turned on its side into a page: two rails in `build_icon.py`'s `RAIL_FACE` (#4F453D), eight rungs in `RUNG_FACE` (#B0A28F), one of them in `ACCENT` (#E9562A) with the same glow the icon's ember rung carries. Eight is the number of résumé sections `resume-craft` gives a job to, and the ember one sits second from the top, where the icon's does and where the summary sits on the page. The icon is the real asset, rendered from `icon.svg` at 560px and inlined.

**Known liabilities.** The bars are flat where the icon's rungs are lit tubes, so the device is a diagram of the icon and does not share its material; at 900px that reads as deliberate and at full size it reads as a step down. The two mono labels are 17px and are gone by about 600px displayed width, which leaves the page device unexplained on a catalogue card. The second label, "0 the person has not confirmed", states the plugin's rule as a count, and a reader could take it for a measured result. The essence line runs to three lines at this width, one more than home-craft's.

**Deliberate absences.** No taper on the page device, because a page has parallel edges and the taper belongs to the ladder. No tick or check mark on the ember bar. Verified by eye on the render: both rails are present after a first render dropped the right one to a dot (a class name collided with the rung's), the wordmark does not overflow, and the ember bar survives the downscale to 400px as the one warm mark.
