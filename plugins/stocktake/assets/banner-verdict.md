Ships. Reviewed rather than composed: this banner was built by another session and this verdict is an outside reader's, written on 2026-08-19 to clear a conformance failure, not the author's own account of their intent.

What was changed, and only this. The wordmark was set in `Iowan Old Style` behind an `@font-face` alias whose only source was `src:local(...)`, and the body copy in `Avenir Next`, so nothing in the banner arrived with the document. Re-rendered on another machine or in CI the wordmark would have silently become Georgia and the body a system sans, and the banner would still have passed every mechanical assertion. It now links Newsreader and Instrument Sans, both faces the family already carries (Newsreader in report and dossier-report, Instrument Sans in nine banners), so the fix costs the set no new typeface. The advance measurement confirms Newsreader is actually rendering, which matters because `document.fonts.check()` under-reports on this engine and returns false for a face it is demonstrably using.

What holds. Porcelain ground in the family register, one ember accent, and the accent is spent on the semantic element: the single lit card in the icon and the rule in the tagline, both marking the one claim being counted. The icon is referenced rather than redrawn. The wordmark and the essence line are both legible at 900px, and the wordmark survives 400px.

Known liabilities. The tagline's dash is a drawn vermilion rule rather than a typed character, which is why the em-dash check passes; that is legitimate and worth knowing, because a reader auditing the copy by eye will see a dash the linter cannot. At 200px the three stacked bars and the lit card merge into a single mass and only the card's warmth survives, so the thumbnail read is carried by colour rather than by the device. And rows 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 11 are one reviewer's assessment rather than a panel's, with the additional caveat that I did not write this composition and cannot say which of its values were derived from the icon's constants and which were chosen directly.
