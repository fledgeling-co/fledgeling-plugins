## Detailed Findings

### 1. Which software and SaaS operating expenses of a small Australian software company qualify as eligible R&D expenditure under the Australian R&D Tax Incentive?

#### 1.1 Statutory test for core and supporting R&D activities

**(High Confidence) `<ENACTED>`** Section 355-25 defines a core R&D activity as an experimental activity:

1. whose outcome cannot be known or determined in advance from current knowledge, information or experience;
2. whose outcome can only be determined through a systematic progression of work based on established scientific principles;
3. proceeding from hypothesis through experiment, observation and evaluation to logical conclusions; and
4. conducted for the purpose of generating new knowledge, including a new or improved product, process or service.  
[legislation.gov.au](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-01-01/2026-01-01/text/original/pdf/7) ([legislation.gov.au](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-01-01/2026-01-01/text/original/pdf/7))

**(High Confidence) `<GUIDANCE>`** The Department’s current software guidance applies that test by asking whether a specific technical hurdle could be resolved by existing knowledge or an experienced professional. Merely using agile development, integrating an API, configuring documented software options, building a conventional dashboard, or migrating data using established methods is ordinarily routine development—not core R&D. [business.gov.au](https://business.gov.au/grants-and-programs/research-and-development-tax-incentive/sector-guides-for-r-and-d-tax-incentive-applicants/software-development) ([business.gov.au](https://business.gov.au/grants-and-programs/research-and-development-tax-incentive/sector-guides-for-r-and-d-tax-incentive-applicants/software-development))

**(High Confidence) `<ENACTED>`** A supporting R&D activity under section 355-30 must be directly related to at least one identified core R&D activity. The dominant-purpose test is additional, not universal: it applies where the supporting activity:

- is excluded from being core R&D;
- produces goods or services; or
- is directly related to producing goods or services.  
[legislation.gov.au](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-01-01/2026-01-01/text/original/pdf/7) ([legislation.gov.au](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-01-01/2026-01-01/text/original/pdf/7))

**(High Confidence) `<GUIDANCE>`** “Dominant purpose” means the prevailing or most influential purpose. Relevant indicators include whether the activity would have been undertaken without the core experiment, whether it serves a substantial commercial purpose, and whether its output is consumed in or exists only for the experiment. [business.gov.au](https://business.gov.au/grants-and-programs/research-and-development-tax-incentive/check-if-you-are-eligible-for-the-randd-tax-incentive/conducting-supporting-rd-activities-for-the-rdti) ([business.gov.au](https://business.gov.au/grants-and-programs/research-and-development-tax-incentive/check-if-you-are-eligible-for-the-randd-tax-incentive/conducting-supporting-rd-activities-for-the-rdti))

**(High Confidence) `<GUIDANCE>`** A project-level label is inadequate. The claimant must identify discrete experimental and supporting activities. This is particularly important for software projects, where an eligible experiment may sit within a much larger body of routine design, coding, integration, deployment and maintenance. [ato.gov.au](https://www.ato.gov.au/Business/Research-and-development-tax-incentive/Helping-you-get-R-D-claims-right/) ([ato.gov.au](https://www.ato.gov.au/Business/Research-and-development-tax-incentive/Helping-you-get-R-D-claims-right/?utm_source=openai))

#### 1.2 The “directly related” and expenditure nexus tests

**(High Confidence) `<ENACTED>`** Two related but legally distinct nexus tests operate:

| Test | Decision-maker | Legal question |
|---|---|---|
| Activity nexus | DISR/Industry Innovation and Science Australia | Is the supporting activity directly related to a specified core R&D activity under section 355-30? |
| Expenditure nexus | ATO | To what extent was the expenditure incurred on registered R&D activities under section 355-205? |

[legislation.gov.au](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-01-01/2026-01-01/text/original/pdf/7) ([legislation.gov.au](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A05138/2026-01-01/2026-01-01/text/original/pdf/7))

**(High Confidence) `<GUIDANCE>`** The Department expects a direct, close and relatively immediate relationship between the supporting activity and an element of the systematic progression of work. The claimant should identify how the activity helped refine the hypothesis, conduct the experiment, collect observations, evaluate results or formulate conclusions. [business.gov.au](https://business.gov.au/-/media/grants-and-programs/rdti/rdti-guide-to-interpretation-2020-pdf.pdf?hash=D5214224E75B3E3B98EDFEA7AE05BDD2&amp;sc_lang=en) [business.gov.au](https://business.gov.au/grants-and-programs/research-and-development-tax-incentive/check-if-you-are-eligible-for-the-randd-tax-incentive/conducting-supporting-rd-activities-for-the-rdti) ([business.gov.au](https://business.gov.au/-/media/grants-and-programs/rdti/rdti-guide-to-interpretation-2020-pdf.pdf?hash=D5214224E75B3E3B98EDFEA7AE05BDD2&sc_lang=en&utm_source=openai))

**(High Confidence) `<GUIDANCE>`** The ATO describes qualifying administrative and overhead expenditure as expenditure “directly linked” to R&D activities and requires sufficient evidence of direct use. The recurring nature of a subscription does not disqualify it, but the entitlement is restricted to the identifiable R&D component. [ato.gov.au](https://www.ato.gov.au/api/public/content/0-3c9f9143-7ee9-4ade-b389-b5f19c9f28c1) ([ato.gov.au](https://www.ato.gov.au/api/public/content/0-3c9f9143-7ee9-4ade-b389-b5f19c9f28c1?utm_source=openai))

**(High Confidence) `<GUIDANCE>`** The ATO requires an expense-specific, fair and reasonable apportionment methodology. It expressly warns that a methodology suitable for one expense may be unsuitable for another—for example, an R&D-salary ratio may be suitable for some personnel costs but not utilities. The same principle means that payroll percentages are generally inferior to actual usage data for cloud, API, logging and CI costs. [ato.gov.au](https://www.ato.gov.au/Business/Research-and-development-tax-incentive/Helping-you-get-R-D-claims-right/) ([ato.gov.au](https://www.ato.gov.au/Business/Research-and-development-tax-incentive/Helping-you-get-R-D-claims-right/?utm_source=openai))

**(High Confidence) `<GUIDANCE>`** Therefore, a monthly subscription should not pass merely because an R&D developer had access. The file should identify:

- the registered core or supporting activity;
- the service period;
- the user, project, API key, repository, instance or environment;
- the eligible use during that period;
- the amount allocated; and
- the basis and records supporting that allocation.
