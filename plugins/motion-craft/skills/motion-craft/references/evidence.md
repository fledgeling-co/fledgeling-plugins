## 3. Reference films measured frame by frame (2026-11-15)

Downloaded at 640×360, one frame a second tiled into contact sheets. Hard cuts measured with `hyperframes measure cuts` (frame-to-frame difference). A first pass with ffmpeg's scene score above 0.28 found only 11 and 1 cuts respectively; the scene score misses cuts between similar frames, so the HyperFrames numbers are the ones used.

**en0GuyhieQk, Anthropic's Claude Motion launch (82 s).** 8 hard cuts in 82 s (5.9 a minute), median shot 4.75 s, and a 32-second unbroken stretch from 10.3 s to 42.1 s: a continuous spine with few cuts. Opens on the result (a 3D city of bike docks with real labels and a running clock, 6:10 → 7:05 am), pulls back to reveal it is a video in a player, then a presenter in frame with information cards growing beside him, a dashboard that becomes the 3D grid, the process stated as type on a curved path ("Tracing every morning ride from the hilltop to downtown"), the comment-to-edit loop shown in product, and an ending where app icons burst out around one Export button.

**To5nrYqvR44, a product film the owner chose as a good example (95 s).** 20 hard cuts (12.7 a minute), median shot 2.13 s: an unbroken 20.6-second opening, then nine cuts between 24 and 37 s and six between 42 and 58 s, plus three fast moves the measure lists apart from cuts. A continuous spine broken by two bursts. Frame 0 is a full sentence, not a logo. The words of the title turn into product chips inline. Real product UI throughout with a cursor doing real clicks. Shot scale swings from whole window to extreme close-ups of one toolbar and one Share button. One asset (a jacket and its line "Rain is a given") is carried through deck, social sizes and document. A motion-blurred whip (1:24) carries into the closing line "Claude makes it. You steer it.", typed by two labelled cursors, then the mark holds about four seconds.

## 4. Vendor documents and local tests

- **Anthropic, "Prompting Claude Opus 5.5", Frontend design defaults** (read 2026-11-15): without direction the model "falls back on a few default styles", a general "avoid a generic AI look" swaps one default for another, and the fix is to name patterns (cream or off-white background, italic accent words in headlines, numbered "01/02/03" labels, monospace labels, pill-shaped buttons) and extend the list from what the first result substituted. Source of the BANNED-list extension rule in `direction.md` §5.
- **HyperFrames 0.9.52 docs**: "You can create and render locally without an account" (`docs/guides/authentication.mdx`); telemetry opt-outs `HYPERFRAMES_NO_TELEMETRY` and `DO_NOT_TRACK` (`docs/packages/cli.mdx`).
- **Local tests, 2026-11-15, Apple M4 Max**: `hf.sh render` of a 10 s 1920×1080 30 fps composition took 19.3 s with no sign-in; a 6 s film with three cues at 0.30, 1.40 and 3.20 s rendered with onsets measured at 0.310, 1.395 and 3.195 s and −14 LUFS integrated; `check` flagged the empty frame 0 as `first_frame_empty`; an `<audio>` tag missing from `index.html` produced a silent MP4 with no error. The vendored sound engine rendered claude-motion's 22 s, 129-sound cue sheet to −14.0 LUFS, true peak −2.3 dBFS, in 5 s.
- **Engine decision**: four model families (claude-fable-5-1, gemini-4-hydrogen-high, gpt-6-astra, grok-4.7) chose HyperFrames 4-0 on 2026-11-15; record at `docs/decisions/2026-11-15-motion-craft-engine.html` in the fledgeling-plugins repo.

## 6. This skill's own first blind evaluation (2026-11-15)

Two briefs, each run once with the skill and once with no skill, judged blind on one-frame-per-second contact sheets by four model families (claude-opus at high effort, gemini-4-hydrogen-high, grok-4.7, gpt-6-astra), options in seeded-random order. Overall preference went to the no-skill film on both: 4-0 on a 20 s launch film for design-craft, 3-1 on a 15 s vertical clip from a CSV. The skill's films won craft 4-0 on the launch film and specificity and story 3-1 on the clip, and passed more structural checks (sound present, a non-empty frame 0, a change inside 3 s). The judges' reasons were consistent: the skill's launch film made the icon perform an idea and never showed the product's work or evidence, and the skill's clip left the lower 40 % of the frame an unchanging colour block and dropped two of the data's four headline figures. The must-carry list, the proof rule, the whole-canvas rule and the coverage review row were added the same day in response. Full record: `evals/EVALS.md` at the plugin root (in the marketplace repository, not bundled with the installed skill).

A second round the same day ran the revised skill on both briefs and judged it blind against the same no-skill films, with a new seeded order. The data clip went from 1-3 to 4-0 overall (specificity and story 4-0, craft 2-2). The launch film went from 0-4 to 2-2 overall (craft 3-1), but still lost specificity and story 4-0: it named the problem in a caption over its icon, while the plain film showed generic output on screen. The "problem is shown, not named" rule and two tells, edge labels cropped mid-pan and colliding transition frames, were added after that round and have not been tested by a third.
