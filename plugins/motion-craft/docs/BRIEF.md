# motion-craft — agreed brief (2026-11-15)

**Trigger.** Someone wants a short video made from code: an animated explainer, a launch or feature film, a data story from a spreadsheet or dashboard, a logo sting, a lower third or other overlay, a vertical social clip, a product demo film, or "what Claude Motion does" without access to Claude Motion. Also: editing, retiming or reviewing a film the skill made, and remaking another film's rhythm on a new subject.

**Output.** A project folder holding one deterministic HTML composition, a live preview page (scrubber, scene strip, tweaks panel, point-at-frame ids), a rendered MP4 (or transparent WebM/MOV for overlays, GIF on request), the planning files (BRIEF, STORYLINES, SPEC, timeline) and a REVIEW.md delivery note.

**Audience.** The owner and the people they send the film to; the planning files are read by the next agent turn.

**Done.** The render exists, its frames were reviewed on contact sheets including phone width, the automated checks print no error, every on-screen claim traces to source material, sound is present unless asked for silence, and REVIEW.md names what was measured, what was only looked at and what nobody checked.

**Hard constraints.** Spend no money and call no paid generation service without the user's go-ahead; never post or publish; real product material over invented UI; every frame a pure function of time.

**Routing.** In-page UI motion → design-craft:design-craft. Interface flow and copy questions → ux-craft:ux-craft. Slides → deck-craft:deck-craft. Exact cloning of a web animation into an app → the reanimate skill when installed. Paid image or video generation → whatever generation MCP the session has, only with approval.

**Decided by the user.** Name: motion-craft. Engine and pre-ship proof: delegated to `decision-panel:decision-panel` (records in `docs/decisions/`).

**Decided here, with reason.** Scope is video deliverables, because design-craft already owns in-page motion (`plugins/design-craft/skills/design-craft/references/motion-design.md`). Remotion is not the default because its licence charges companies of four or more.

**Research corpus.** 18 YouTube transcripts read in full, 7 open-source motion skills (claude-motion, huashu-motion, huashu-art-motion, earshot, reanimate, explainroo, HyperFrames), and measured contact sheets plus cut timings of two reference films. Index: `docs/research/CORPUS.md`.
