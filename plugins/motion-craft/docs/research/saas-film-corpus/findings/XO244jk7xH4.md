## What to steal
- A repeated two-line lower-third (name above, relationship line with avatar below) at 4-6% of frame height turns four interviews into one system.
- Show the approval step on screen while the line about consent is spoken; it is the one beat where picture and voice agree on a fact.
- Keep the end card plain and long: icon, wordmark, two badges, parent logo, about 5s.
- Hold one phone shot steady and big enough that the heading reads; do the same for body text, which this film does not.
- Fix what this film leaves open: show the product within 3s, keep to -14 LUFS with a true peak at or below -1 dBTP, and lock a few cuts to music.
