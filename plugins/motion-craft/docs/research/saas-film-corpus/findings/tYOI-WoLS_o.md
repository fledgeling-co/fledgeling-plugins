## What to steal

- Let a task run in real time, interrupt it once on screen and show it carry on. The steering claim is proved in the 70 s moment.
- Make the opening card the same ground colour as the surround of the app window, so the cut into the UI changes only the window contents (f0001.jpg to f0004.jpg).
- Chapter cards with 5 words or fewer and one hand-drawn underline in the accent colour, held 2-3 s.
- Zoom 1.5x only on the control the narration names, hold it about 4 s, then return.
- A screen-recording shot can run 111.8 s without cuts if something changes every couple of seconds, and a chapter card restarts it. This contradicts rule 10.
- Close with three colour-coded 3-4-word cards, staggered about 2 s apart, then a still logo held about 5 s. Add the call to action this film leaves out.
- Do not copy the audio: -16.5 LUFS and +0.3 dBTP need correcting to -14 LUFS and -1 dBTP.
