## What to steal

1. Make the hook the product editing its own title: select, toolbar, bold within 2 s (f0001.jpg, f0002.jpg).
2. Show the problem as an action, a blank slide typed over with a joke, not as a caption (f0007.jpg to f0009.jpg).
3. Crop real UI to the one thing that matters at each beat; keep whole-window shots short and non-essential (f0030.jpg versus f0028.jpg).
4. Draw name cards and captions as editable objects that settle to clean text (f0011.jpg to f0013.jpg).
5. A voice-led story can hold one composition 25 s if something changes every 2 s (28.6-53.6 s).
6. Cut to the output at about 60% of runtime (63.17 s, share 0.607) and spend the next 20 s on edits and sharing.
7. End on a three-step build with a silent last 2 s (f0049.jpg to f0052.jpg).
8. Counter-evidence for rules 8 and 9: this film has no 5-cut burst and masters at -25.7 LUFS and still reads as a finished film, so those rules fit feed ads, not presenter explainers.
