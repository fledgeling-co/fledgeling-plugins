# dossier-report — icon notes

**Direction: "The Folded Sheet".** Tahoe gel-glass sub-register (a) *porcelain + gel object*, crossed with device #23 *fold/self-shadow ribbon* and #16 *the icon performs the verb*. The subject is a skill that reads every source in full and publishes one page, so the icon is a page that has been *opened* — the fold is the reading, and what it reveals is evidence. Runner-up: Direction 8 *Instrument Emblem*, a ring or step chart as the mark. It lost on three counts: a chart is the stock category glyph for anything that touches data and needs positive justification the subject can't supply, the skill's argument is not "we make charts" but "we opened the sources", and a frontal chart tile on porcelain is a second flat mark in a set where `armada-sync` already owns that ground. Also considered and cut: Direction 7 *Diagonal Tool* — a magnifier or pen crossing the plane — the catalogue's most template-worn move, and "research skill, therefore a magnifying glass" is the category trap exactly.

**Glyph (subject-mined, device bank #23 + #16 + #20 + #5):** one porcelain sheet, 580×665 in a 1024 canvas, caught mid-fold. The crease runs 46° from a point on the bottom edge (300, 845) to a point on the right edge (800, 320), so the flap is the sheet's lower-right *third* — a fold, where a small corner would be the page curl the corpus files as legacy-era drag. The page keeps its whole top edge and three of its four corners, because it has to read as a page before it reads as a fold. `FOLD_K = 0.800`: the flap has come 80% of the way over, far enough to show its inner face fully and not so far that it lies flat and stops being an event. Double read (#5): at 128px and below the crease diagonal is also a rising trend line.

**Signature:** the fold reveals a sliver of a chart rule on the *inner* face — a stepped line and its baseline, authored in unfolded page space inside the flap's source region and carried into view by the same fold map that turns the flap, so it foreshortens correctly instead of being drawn onto a triangle. The icon shows evidence held inside the page, which is the whole claim: the report is built from sources that were actually opened. The steps rise across the rule rather than along it, because those are the page-space axes this particular crease maps to screen-horizontal and screen-vertical; getting that backwards would have made the chart lie about the fold. Risk taken: the accent is not an ornament but a whole plane, so vermilion occupies 9.2% of the tile against `armada-sync`'s single band — held off by keeping it strictly to the one revealed face and letting the outer sheet stay porcelain.

**Palette:** two families only. Warm porcelain, for both the cushion and the sheet on it (`#FFFCF6→#F8F3EB→#F1ECE2` tile with a `#9C8B70` vignette; `#FFFEFB→#F9F5EE→#EFE9DE` page face, `#F7F1E4→#D8CEBB` its slab edges), and one vermilion reserved strictly for the revealed inner face (`#F46F41→#E85128→#CE3A16`, occluding to `#8E1E04` and `#7A1602` where the V closes), with the crease crest at `#FFD3B4→#F9834F`, rim scatter `#FCC0A4`, and the chart frosted at `#FFF0E6`. Shadow is `#6B5334` — warm, because nothing in this scene emits cool light. One soft top light at `(-0.44, -0.90)`, biased left; three contact-shadow radii under the sheet; zero hard speculars.

The material numbers were sampled out of the corpus before a line was authored, and two of them changed the design. Apple-05 (Infuse, the same fold-ribbon device) measures its inner face at `#D93621` — L 0.34, hue 7, saturation 0.848 — against an outer face of `#ED722E` at L 0.53, hue 21, saturation 0.806: the turned-away plane is darker, hue-rotated 14° *toward* red, and **more** saturated, because a translucent body keeps its chroma where it turns away. And the porcelain sheet is deliberately *not* darker than the ground it sits on: C1's sheet measures p25–p75 0.943–0.979 against a ground of 0.935–0.989, so the corpus separates a white object from a white ground by shadow, thickness and rim, never by value. Assuming "object darker than ground" would have cost the register.

**Deliberate avoidances:** the dark deep-sea register (held by the sibling `trawl`), the blue/indigo ramp the corpus census records as the template default across 271 chromatic hits, and any second hue for the chart rule — it is frosted white with the flap's hue bleeding through, not a data colour. Also cut: a sub-legible diegetic citation number engraved on the chart baseline. It is sanctioned garnish under Tahoe grammar #8 and it was the most subject-true detail available, since the skill's entire argument is that the sources were opened and counted; it went for costing #12 in exchange for a detail gone by 128px.

**Audit: 11/12, zero failures on the non-negotiable 1–4.** Full-bleed 1024 artwork with the set's exact superellipse as a *clip*, no baked corners or shadow. Sheet 580×665 optically centred at (510, 512) with 220/224/180/179 margins, so the focal sits at 56.6% of tile width inside the 55–65% band. Silhouette and the 16px squint verified on real renders: at 16px the diagonal survives as a clean vermilion wedge, which is what the direction promised would carry it. Four layers `#bg`/`#mid`/`#fg`/`#highlight` map 1:1 onto Icon Composer.

Point deducted at **#7 figure-ground**, and it is the register's price rather than an oversight. The vermilion inner face measures **1.72:1** against the tile ground and **1.71:1** against the page face, well under the required 3:1 and in the same band as the corpus's cited waterlemon failure at 1.4:1. What saves the mark is that the grayscale hierarchy holds the right way up — desaturated, the accent resolves *darker* than everything around it, so the focal stays the darkest element instead of inverting, which is the beat `armada-sync` lost. The weaker half is the sheet, not the accent: page against tile is **1.01:1**, so in grayscale the porcelain sheet is carried entirely by its edge crest and its contact shadow. That is what a white sheet on a porcelain ground is, and fixing it by darkening either one abandons the direction. **#3 passes but weakly** and is worth naming: the flap folds inward, so the filled-black silhouette is a plain rounded rectangle — nameable as a sheet of paper, but the fold, the whole signature, is invisible in outline and rides entirely on value and hue.

**The set collision, checked on renders rather than argued.** `armada-sync` also owns paper on porcelain with a vermilion accent, so the two were rendered side by side at 128px. They do not read as siblings beyond the palette: armada-sync is horizontal-banded, graphite-dominant, frontal and flat, with vermilion as one narrow band on one line; this one is a single large diagonal wedge, vermilion-dominant at 9.2% of the tile, with genuine dimensional form. Different silhouette family, different value structure, different accent share. The differentiator held.

**Engines:** all three ran. Engine B (Arrow vector, `icon-engineB-arrow-ecc20a.svg`) scored 5/12 and hard-failed three of the four non-negotiables — a square card with baked square corners and a baked drop shadow, offset with no safe zone, and a vermilion needle whose tip is sub-pixel by 32px. It also lost the brief, reading as a swallow rather than a fold. Its one contribution was independently reaching a ~45° diagonal as the axis. Engine C returned two rasters: C1 (`icon-engineC-c57a8c`) won the material read outright — real volumetric frosted gel, a thick translucent edge, the accent visible *through* the underside — and became the blind panel's reference; C2 (`icon-engineC2-597699`) shares the master's frontal-card geometry and became the fidelity loop's reference so the residual would isolate material instead of arguing about pose. Neither ships. Both are freestanding objects on a white backdrop rather than full-bleed on a cushion, so masking them to the squircle just crops a photograph, and both are the flat pre-masked rasters that are the corpus's named 76% failure at #10. C1 additionally lost the subject: judges reading it blind called it a tilted cone.

**v1 → v2, and the instrument that reversed the decision.** The first eight fidelity rounds (r00–r07) were scored on a degraded metric tier — luminance, SSIM and edges, no LPIPS — and shipped r07. Re-scored at full tier against **both** rasters, that run went backwards at every size and shipped its worst take: r07 is last of the eight on 1024 LPIPS (0.4883 against C2, 0.4115 against C1) and last on absolute self-contrast at every size. The two references agree independently, which is what makes it a finding rather than an artifact of C2's flat flap. The regression decomposed cleanly, each step measured on its own: reverting the r07 gel ramp moved LPIPS by **0.0000** while recovering **+0.030** of self-contrast; reverting the r05 contact-shadow round moved LPIPS **−0.0148** and was the whole of it; deleting the crease occlusion bought a further **−0.0035** and was declined, because that shadow is the fold's volume and the reference it would converge on has a flat flap. The occlusion was probed *absent* before being kept, which is the loop's own r03 lesson applied on purpose.

**v3, the round the judges bought.** With r10 assembled, a blind panel preferred the old r07 **2–0** — and both judges that ran named the same defect unprompted: a "tighter diagonal specular… more like a gloss streak than volume". It was a pure `#FFFFFF` hairline at 0.55 opacity along the crease spine, a hard specular in a scene lit by nothing white, and a straight violation of Tahoe grammar #6. r11 warmed it to the measured rim scatter (`#FFE7D6`), halved it to 0.30, widened it from 6 to 9px and moved it to a softer blur. Re-panelled with the order reseeded, the panel reversed **2–0 for r11** on both overall and material, both judges citing the edit itself — "less hard-edged specular streak on the orange face", "highlight falloff… gentler and less pin-sharp… nearer the reference's frosted translucency". The composite scored that change as noise (+0.0011 LPIPS); the panel scored it as the difference between losing and winning, which is the clearest evidence in this commission for why the panel outranks the gate. The OpenAI judge failed to run in both rounds for want of a key; recorded, not silently dropped, so both tallies rest on two judges rather than three.

**What the metric could never pay for.** Edge F1 sits at 0.057 at 1024, so a fifth of the composite was unavailable for the entire run: the reference is an inset card floating on a backdrop and the master is full-bleed on the shelf, so their edges are ~120px apart by construction. That gap is not a defect to close. Closing it means shrinking the artwork off the tile, which is the one thing the house aesthetic forbids.
