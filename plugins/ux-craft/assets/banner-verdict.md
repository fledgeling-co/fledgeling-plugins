**Ships.** The crossing is computed rather than drawn. `build_icon.py`'s own `vesica()` intersects the two reach envelopes in the flat plan and foreshortens the result, `lens_centre()` places the core ramp where the doubled gel is thickest, and `puck()` and `key()` emit both objects, all against the icon's constants scaled by one factor of 0.52. `R_SMALL` is left as the derived `R_BIG` x `ENVELOPE_AA` / `ENVELOPE_AAA`, so the 44:24 ratio survives the scaling instead of being re-entered by hand: measured back out of the emitted SVG, 81.7 / 149.8 = 0.5455, which is 24/44 exactly. `SQUASH` 0.66, `THICK` 32, `OFFSET_X` 246 and `OFFSET_Y` 222 are the icon's, as are the one shared light ramp both pucks hang off, the occlusion crescent, the rim light, the porcelain bounce, `GEL_ALPHA`, the frosted keys at `KEY_ALPHA`, and the accent's four stops from `ACCENT_CORE` through `ACCENT_DEEP`. The ground falls at 152 degrees, which is the shade direction `LIGHT_ANGLE_DEG` 118 produces.

**Known liabilities.** The 44 and 24 are the only type on the right half and they are set at 20px, so they are gone by about 600px displayed width: at the 400px catalogue card and the 200px thumbnail the device is two dark pucks and a hot crossing with no figures at all, and the arithmetic that is the whole point of the geometry is unavailable to anyone who never sees it larger. They read cleanly at 900px, which is where most people meet a banner, so this was accepted rather than fixed by enlarging them, but it is the sharpest thing wrong with this banner. Second, the accent occupies a genuinely small area by construction, because it exists only where the two envelopes overlap; at 200px the banner is very nearly monochrome and the one hot pixel cluster is easy to miss. Third, the two numerals sit beside their pucks with no leader, so which number belongs to which envelope is inferred from size rather than stated. A tick or a rule would fix that and would also be one more mark than this composition can carry.

**Deliberate absences.** No accent rule under the wordmark, because the accent here is a property of a relationship rather than a colour this skill owns, and painting it on the type would say the opposite. The tile's own inner rim light is not carried either, since there is no tile out here. Verified by eye rather than by assertion: the crossing's hot core survives the downscale to 400px as a legible warm lens rather than a smear, and the small puck's key still reads as a separate frosted object at 900px rather than merging into the gel it sits on.
