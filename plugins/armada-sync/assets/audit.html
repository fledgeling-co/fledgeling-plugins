C2 · icon-engineC-7896e3-2.pngEngine C, GPT Image 2 raster, second take 8 / 12 The better material take of the pair: real soft-extruded gel on the graphite bars with honest contact shadows, and the frosted core inside the seal is the closest any engine got to the intended overlap. Fails the same four as C1, and its red is hotter still, landing squarely on the alert-red reading the whole direction was built to avoid. Salvaged into the master: confirmation that the frosted-core-inside-the-seal move renders convincingly, which is why the master's frostGrad carries the seal's hue into its lower half rather than sitting flat white.
Recommendation: ship A-v2 (icon.svg). It is the only take that passes all four non-negotiables on real renders, and the only one authored as separate layers, so #10 variant robustness holds by construction rather than by luck: identity rides on shape and value (the fattest banded row with a knocked-out core), with colour as the last ten percent. Material provenance is honest: the cushion tile, the frosted bleed-through and the authored overlap are hand-built in SVG, with C2 confirming the frosted-core read and C1 driving the ragged line-length pass. Known liabilities to revisit: (1) the #7 deduction is real and was verified on a rendered grayscale proof, not assumed. Desaturated, the vermilion seal resolves lighter than the graphite lines, so the value hierarchy inverts and the focal stops being the darkest element; the mark still reads because the seal is the fattest, widest row with a knocked-out core, but under Tinted the figure-ground argument is carried entirely by shape. (2) At 16px on a non-retina display the frosted core is roughly one device pixel and can collapse into the seal, which costs the entry-inside-the-seal nuance while leaving the identity intact. (3) Red on a stack of rows carries a standing error-state association; it is held off by making the accent a band that contains the row rather than a red row, and both raster takes are on the sheet as evidence of what happens when that discipline slips.
Rubric: the 12-point icon audit from icon-directions.md (mask discipline, grid, silhouette, 16px identity, single light model, palette discipline, figure-ground, depth coherence, era coherence, variant robustness, personality, no-text). Checks 1–4 non-negotiable, delivery bar ≥10/12. Raster takes audited on their squircle-masked 1024 versions. Renders produced by python3 audit_sheet.py render <dir> (1024 / 256 / 128 / 96 / 64 / 32 sources); verified by its check subcommand.
