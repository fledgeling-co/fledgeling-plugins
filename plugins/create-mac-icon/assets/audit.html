create-mac-icon icon: contact sheet and audit

Direction "The Cast" · one shared spec, three engines per the create-mac-icon pipeline · every take audited on real renders at 1024 / 128 / 64 / 48 / 32 / 16. The skill was dogfooded on its own icon: Engine A was authored through build_icon.py, the raster takes carried four porcelain-register exemplars from corpus/apple-2026/ as reference images, and the shipping master was then closed against the winning raster through the skill's own measured fidelity loop — twice, and the second loop's state is unresolved. See the two loop tables and the provenance note at the foot of this sheet.

Its cavity is the best-realised element anywhere in the set: a bright plaster floor holding a pooled peach bounce that pales toward the near rim, which is the whole signature stated in one gradient. It cannot ship. #10, the corpus's highest-leverage failure, is what a flat pre-masked raster IS. #9 also goes: the ground is dead-flat white with no cushion, no inner rim and no vignette, which the Tahoe grammar names as the previous era's tell. #2 goes on focal size, at roughly 78% of the tile.
C1 · icon-engineC-27539d-masked.pngEngine C raster, second take, material control 9 / 12 The strongest 16px read in the set, and it lost the reference slot anyway. Its cavity has no visible floor, so the mould reads as a hollow frame and the negative stops being a negative of anything, which costs the concept more than the extra mass buys. Same structural failures as C2 at #9 and #10, and #2 is worse at roughly 85% of the tile with the block running to the frame. Its measured contribution was the gel chroma: sampling it and C2 together is what showed the master's shadows were going brown when translucent resin holds its saturation.
Recommendation: ship take A (icon.svg). It is the only take that is Icon-Composer-ready: four layers, #bg / #mid / #fg / #highlight, mapping one to one, full-bleed 1024 artwork with the marketplace superellipse as a clip and no baked corner or drop shadow. Checks 1–4 were verified on the renders above rather than assumed. Its material provenance is C2, closed by seven measured fidelity rounds plus one measured correction, rather than by eye. Known liabilities to revisit: (1) figure-ground measures 2.72:1, under the 3:1 bar, so the cast is separated from the porcelain by 99 grayscale levels and by shape, not by a compliant contrast ratio; (2) the focal group spans 65.6% of tile width, a whisker over the 55–65% composition constant, taken deliberately to close the scale gap against the raster; (3) the plaster block sits close in value to its own porcelain ground, which is the register's nature but makes the light Tinted variant the weakest of the four, carried by the cast alone; (4) at 16px the cavity survives as a warm smudge rather than as a readable superellipse, so the icon keeps its subject but loses the "same shape twice" argument at menu-bar size; (5) the cast is authored parallel to the mould where both rasters tilt it, a deliberate divergence that keeps the mirroring legible and permanently caps how close the fidelity score can get.

r07 passed the Pareto gate on tolerance, because no single size regressed by more than 0.005, and was rolled back anyway: all five sizes moved the wrong way, so the round bought nothing. r08 was a single measured correction taken after the loop had closed, for a visible artifact rather than for a score: the block's lit edge peaked exactly where the cast occludes it, so the surviving stub read as a stray hairline against the porcelain. It was re-scored rather than eyeballed, and kept because it cost nothing. Baseline to shipped: 1024 composite 0.6546 → 0.6685, 32px 0.6947 → 0.7206, 16px 0.7684 → 0.7855, with no size worse than baseline. Metric tier: numpy, luminance field plus SSIM plus edge F1 plus mask IoU. LPIPS did not run because torch is not installed on this host, so the 1024 material number is the weaker of the two available stacks and should be read as directional. Per-round state, residuals and edge maps are in fidelity-runs/.

The state this sheet has to be honest about. The master on disk is r04: icon.svg is 50,471 bytes stamped 9 Aug 2026, and loop-runs/r04/cand-test.png is byte-identical to the shipped icon.png. r04 has a score.json and no gate.txt, so the Pareto gate never ruled on it — and on its own numbers it regressed at all five sizes against r03 (net −0.0179). loop-runs/last-accepted/ still holds r03 (44,440 bytes, 8 Aug), and icon-notes.md documents the loop only through r03. So the last accepted master and the shipped master are different files, and the difference has never been adjudicated. That is a defect in the commission, not in this sheet, and it is recorded here rather than papered over: nothing in this sheet claims r04 passed a gate, and take A's 11/12 remains the r08-era judgment carried forward, re-confirmed at 11/12 through r03 by icon-notes.md and not re-scored for r04.
Rubric: the 12-point icon audit from icon-directions.md (mask discipline, grid, silhouette, 16px identity, single light model, palette discipline, figure-ground, depth coherence, era coherence, variant robustness, personality, no-text). Checks 1–4 are non-negotiable, delivery bar 10 of 12 or better. Raster takes audited on their squircle-masked 1024 versions, masked with the set's exact superellipse path and never a rounded-rect approximation. Renders produced by python3 audit_sheet.py render <dir> (1024 / 256 / 128 / 96 / 64 / 32 sources); verified by its check subcommand. Take B is rendered svg-letterbox, because its 150×130.4 viewBox is not square artwork and stretching it to a square would flatter it.
