## Synthesis — the Tahoe gel-glass grammar (what these 32 teach that the gallery corpus lags on)

**Era census: 32/32 are fully current-era.** This set is the rendered answer key for Liquid Glass / macOS 26 ("Tahoe") icon design. Nine tells recur:

1. **The tile is a cushion, not a print.** Every squircle ground — saturated or porcelain — is itself softly extruded: a subtle inner rim light around the perimeter, gentle edge-darkening/vignette, reading as a gel slab. A dead-flat background is instantly previous-era.
2. **Glyphs are poured, not drawn.** The dominant material is a **soft-extruded translucent gel/resin**: generous rounded corners on every shape, a soft top-edge highlight, a faint inner glow, and a small soft drop shadow onto the ground. Flat brand marks (News N, Slack pinwheel, Shortcuts diamonds) were re-materialised, not redrawn — silhouettes unchanged.
3. **Translucency is authored, and it shows at overlaps.** Wherever two shapes cross, hue visibly blends (Photos petals multiply darker; Weather's sun bleeds through the cloud; Shortcuts' diamonds mix to lavender; frosted white glyphs let the ground's hue through). This bleed-through is the single most reliable "current-era" tell — and it is exactly what a baked flat raster cannot fake under system tinting.
4. **Two ground registers dominate:** (a) **porcelain near-white** (`#FAFAFB`-ish, soft rim, faint vignette) carrying a colored gel object — Safari, Photos, News, Slack, Reminders, Find My, Home, Calculator, ChatGPT, Infuse; (b) **saturated single-hue gel tile** (blue, violet, coral) carrying a **white frosted glyph** — Mail, App Store, Weather, Keynote, Shortcuts, rocket. Dark charcoal/navy grounds appear (stickers, butterfly) but are the minority register.
5. **White is a material now.** The recurring white glyph is *frosted glass/porcelain* — translucent, tinted by its ground, with fold/emboss shading — never flat `#FFFFFF`. If a white glyph shows zero ground-hue bleed, it reads pre-Tahoe.
6. **One soft top light, zero hard speculars.** Diffuse top/top-slightly-forward light everywhere; the glossy Aqua-style sharp specular streak is gone. Depth comes from rim highlights + soft AO, not gloss.
7. **3D miniatures survive, softened.** Object icons (loupe, rocket, binoculars, tower, calculator, podium, scanner) persist as toy-scale renders in **matte-satin/clay** materials with real contact shadows — never chrome-glossy, and always one clearly-lit object.
8. **Micro diegetic text is a craft signal, not a violation.** Mail's address line, Preview's "PREVIEW 10×" — sub-legible engraved text on an object is used as an easter egg. (Still no headline text.)
9. **Patterned grounds carry meaning quietly:** blueprint grids (Xcode, rocket), sparkle stars, beacon stripes — extremely low-contrast, thematic, never competing with the glyph. Edge-bleed devices (Contacts' index tabs, the printer's slot) treat the mask as a physical object boundary.

**Composition constants:** single focal object at ~55–65% tile width, optically centred (or a deliberate offset badge, à la Maps); concentric radii; palette economy holds (porcelain + one gel hue family, or saturated tile + white frost; multi-hue only when the brand IS multi-hue, and then bounded inside one object).

**Layer implication:** every one of these icons decomposes naturally into Icon Composer layers — ground slab / gel glyph / highlight+bloom — which is why they will survive Dark/Clear/Tinted variants. This is the discipline the ICONS.md corpus found missing in 76% of shipping third-party icons.
