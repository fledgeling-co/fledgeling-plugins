Recommendation: ship A (icon.svg). It is the only take that is both a real layered master and free of a non-negotiable failure: four named layers (bg / mid / fg / highlight) over the family squircle with no baked corner or shadow, so it decomposes for a variant pass; `fidelity.py structure` passes at 35 paths and 19KB; and its 16px identity comes from a large dark mass rather than from the accent, which is what took the shelf contrast from 0.050 to 0.266. The material that C won on has been rebuilt into it rather than copied: rounded slab, barrel hinges, modelled handwheel, and a warm bounce on the door's leading edge measured off apple-12's own body-bottom relationship. Known liabilities to revisit: (1) the record plate still reads as a book at 1024 — the lean helps, a chamfer or a squarer plate would help more; (2) at 16px the icon shares a signature with `tui-craft` (dark panel, warm mark) and with `mockup-fidelity`, and only the porcelain surround separates them, so a shelf change on either of those needs re-checking; (3) #10 is a partial pass — a mono tint keeps the portal and loses the light that carries the meaning; (4) the handwheel, the feature that says strongroom rather than doorway, is gone by 32px, so the small-size read is "dark portal with a lit interior" and not the full device; (5) the fidelity loop was not run — torch is not installed here, LPIPS engages only at 256 and 1024, and the gate refuses a degraded tier rather than guessing, so the material gap against C was closed by hand from the corpus and by measurement rather than scored.
