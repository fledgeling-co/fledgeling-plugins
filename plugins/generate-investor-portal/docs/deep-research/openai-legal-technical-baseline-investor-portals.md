## Executive Summary

- **(High Confidence)** `<ENACTED>` An investor portal is not a substitute for the legally authoritative disclosure channel. For an ASX-listed entity, market-sensitive information must be disclosed to ASX immediately and information intended for market release must not be released to others before ASX has released it; a portal may mirror the released announcement only after that control point. [asx.com.au](https://www.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/rules-guidance-notes-waivers/asx-listing-rules/rules/Chapter15.pdf) ([asx.com.au](https://www.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/rules-guidance-notes-waivers/asx-listing-rules/rules/Chapter15.pdf?utm_source=openai))

- **(High Confidence)** `<ENACTED>` Australian private-company reporting duties are entity-status dependent, not portal dependent: public companies, large proprietary companies and disclosing entities have annual-reporting duties, while a domestic small proprietary company ordinarily has no general duty to prepare, circulate or lodge annual financial reports unless an applicable trigger—such as a qualifying shareholder direction—applies. A portal should therefore distinguish **“not required / not prepared / not held”** from zero, nil, or an invented estimate. [asic.gov.au](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/financial-reporting-and-audit/preparers-of-financial-reports/small-proprietary-companies/) ([asic.gov.au](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/financial-reporting-and-audit/preparers-of-financial-reports/small-proprietary-companies/?utm_source=openai))

Every portal figure should therefore carry a source identifier, reporting period, “as at” date, currency, unit, accounting basis, and status such as audited, reviewed, unaudited, forecast, or management estimate. The last sentence is an operational inference, not a literal prescribed field list. ### 1. Answer this decisively: Evidence-backed conventions and failure modes for automated generation of investor-facing disclosure documents for private and listed companies.

#### 1.1 Disclosure perimeter: what the portal or update should carry

**Australia — listed entities.** `<ENACTED>` An ASX-listed issuer’s investor-facing surface must be designed around continuous disclosure, not around a periodic marketing calendar. Listing Rule 3.1 requires immediate disclosure of information a reasonable person would expect to have a material effect on the price or value of securities, subject to defined exceptions; “immediately” means promptly and without delay. [www2.asx.com.au](https://www2.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/about/compliance/abridged-cd-guide.pdf) ([www2.asx.com.au](https://www2.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/about/compliance/abridged-cd-guide.pdf?utm_source=openai)) `<ENACTED>` Listing Rule 15.7 then prevents an issuer releasing information intended for the market to any person before ASX has released it. [asx.com.au](https://www.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/rules-guidance-notes-waivers/asx-listing-rules/rules/Chapter15.pdf) ([asx.com.au](https://www.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/rules-guidance-notes-waivers/asx-listing-rules/rules/Chapter15.pdf?utm_source=openai))

**Control consequence.** `<INFERENCE from="[ASX Listing Rule 15.7; ASX GN 8 rule 3.1 guidance]">For an ASX issuer, the portal-generation workflow must first query the canonical ASX announcement record and publication status. If the proposed content is already published, the system should refuse re-crawl and paid re-generation, return the official released document or approved portal mirror, and log its source hash. If it is not published but could contain market-sensitive information, it must be blocked and escalated to the disclosure officer rather than generated for a portal.</INFERENCE>` (Confidence: High.) [asx.com.au](https://www.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/rules-guidance-notes-waivers/asx-listing-rules/rules/Chapter15.pdf) ([asx.com.au](https://www.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/rules-guidance-notes-waivers/asx-listing-rules/rules/Chapter15.pdf?utm_source=openai))

**Australia — periodic financial reporting.** `<ENACTED>` ASIC states that disclosing entities lodge annual financial reports within **three months** after financial year-end; other companies generally lodge within **four months**. Disclosing entities also lodge half-year financial reports. Public companies and disclosing entities must send financial reports to members by the earlier of 21 days before the next AGM or four months after year-end; large proprietary companies send them within four months. #### 1.4 LLM failure modes and enforceable mitigations

## Recommended Next Steps

1. **Create a jurisdiction-and-entity disclosure matrix.**  
   **Rationale:** Map each target company as Australian small proprietary, large proprietary, public unlisted, ASX listed, US Exchange Act reporting, UK traded, or EU Transparency Directive issuer. This determines whether `Unavailable` is an acceptable portal state, a publication blocker, or an escalation trigger.

2. **Define an authoritative-fact schema and source hierarchy before using generation.**  
   **Rationale:** Require immutable source IDs, source hashes, reporting periods, “as at” dates, units, currency, assurance status, calculation lineage and approval identity. Make the language model consume approved fact IDs rather than raw figures.

3. **Implement the publication gate as a separate trusted service.**  
   **Rationale:** Enforce source existence, duplicate-publication checks, arithmetic reconciliation, source/citation entailment, mandatory-field completeness, contrast/table linting and release-channel sequencing outside the LLM. Deny database write access to crawler and generation agents.

4. **Run a disclosure red-team focused on financial-table and crawler attacks.**  
   **Rationale:** Test prompt injection embedded in PDFs, HTML, alt text, footnotes and copied investor presentations; stale cap-table events; contradictory filings; unit/currency confusion; selective quotation; and colour-only negative-value or unavailable-state presentation.

5. **Obtain Australian, US and UK/EU counsel sign-off on the exception taxonomy.**  
   **Rationale:** Counsel should approve which omissions create a hard stop; who can certify a `Not held`, `Not required`, `Unaudited`, `Preliminary` or `Corrected` status; whether portal material is a regulated announcement; and the correction/escalation protocol.
