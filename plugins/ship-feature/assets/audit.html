Recommendation: ship A (icon.svg). It is the only take that clears all four non-negotiables on real renders, and the only one that is genuinely layered — bg / mid / fg / highlight map 1:1 onto Icon Composer, the waterline crossing is an authored overlap of two real planes rather than a baked gradient, and the ember is a highlight layer the hull survives without. Material provenance: thirteen scored fidelity rounds against C1 (fidelity-runs/r00 → r13), mean composite 0.5827 → 0.6177, 1024px 0.4909 → 0.5090, 32px 0.7307 → 0.7673, 16px 0.7648 → 0.7861. Fourteen scored rounds, four of them rejected by the gate and two of those kept anyway: r02 (the ember bounce that had turned the whole bow bronze) and r11 (the vessel scaled up for 16px identity) both cost composite and both fixed a rubric-visible defect, which is the loop's own rule that a gate ACCEPT is evidence and never a verdict.

Known liabilities to revisit: (1) the vessel spans 69% of tile width against the catalogue's 55–65% band — bought deliberately for 16px identity in r11/r12 and pulled back from 72% and the first number a future round should test. (2) The bow wave took three separate scaffolds (smooth mound → filament comb → explicit breaking profile) and the third is the least wrong rather than clearly right; at 128px it still reads slightly as a lit mass rather than as broken water. (3) create-swe-project already owns a boat-on-a-slipway glyph on this shelf; the separation here is deliberate and complete at 128px (decked vessel vs open dinghy, porcelain/graphite vs cool blue, ember on the water vs amber on the hull, motion vs repose) but at 16px both reduce to a raked hull, and that collision is real. (4) The three wave-train arcs are the only element that reads as drawn rather than lit; they were dimmed by a third in r11 and could still go. (5) The gate rejected r09 and r11 and the rubric shipped them anyway — the reference frames its vessel smaller and carries less small-size definition than the master needs, so the last two rounds deliberately moved away from it.
Rubric: the 12-point icon audit from icon-directions.md (mask discipline, grid, silhouette, 16px identity, single light model, palette discipline, figure-ground, depth coherence, era fit, variant robustness, personality, no-text) — checks 1–4 non-negotiable, delivery bar ≥10/12. Raster takes audited on their squircle-masked 1024 versions. Renders produced by python3 scripts/audit_sheet.py render . (1024 / 256 / 128 / 96 / 64 / 32 sources); verified by its check subcommand.
