Recommendation: ship A-master (icon.svg). It is the only take that is Icon-Composer ready — four named layers over a swappable ground, the superellipse as a clip rather than a baked corner, no baked drop shadow, and the family's exact ground, graphite ramp and vermilion lifted from the sibling rather than re-derived, so the two read as a pair. Checks 1–4 were read off the renders above. Its device — one clean fracture cut square across the descending stem, vermilion filling the void — is the only one of the four that is still legible at 32px and in grayscale. Known liabilities to revisit: (1) identity rests on 0.54% accent coverage, so a Tinted variant that recolours the accent returns the icon to the sibling's silhouette and the pair stops being distinguishable — the sharpest open question here; (2) at 16px the cut is roughly one warm pixel, so what survives the squint is the letterform and its mass, not the device, the same bargain the sibling makes with its gate; (3) the groove's two faces are lit by hand-placed bands rather than by the rim and bounce masks, because both of those are keyed on the whole letterform's silhouette and cannot see a boundary the cut created — a renderer that treats those blurs differently will change the cut's depth before it changes anything else; (4) Engine B did not run: Arrow refused on a gateway key over budget, so Engine A was widened to two genuinely different hand-authored takes, which is the sanctioned substitution but is one fewer independent reading than the pipeline asks for; (5) rendering verified in librsvg only.
