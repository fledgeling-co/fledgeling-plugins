Recommendation: ship A (icon.svg). Five real groups — #bg cushion and spill, #tree the accessibility keyline, #mid the solid geometry plane, #fg the capture samples, #highlight the rim lights — so it stays editable and re-themeable, which no raster take is. Full-bleed, no baked mask or shadow. The non-negotiables were checked on the real renders above, not imagined: the three-window stack is nameable as stacked windows at 48 and 32, and at 16 it collapses cleanly to a pale window with two dark rows and a warm near-corner. The depth stack, inter-plane shadows and dissolving fringe are C1/C2’s ideas rebuilt as geometry rather than copied as pixels. Known liabilities to revisit: (1) the geometry plane is 1.01:1 against the tile, so half the object separates from the ground by rim light and keyline alone and will soften under a heavy tint; (2) at 16px the three registrations collapse into one form — the icon still reads, but the tri-observer idea that makes it this icon is a 48px-and-up device; (3) the capture’s sample grid and the two content bars are legible only from 64px, so 32 and 16 lean entirely on the keyline and the delta; (4) a warm fringe on the lower-right of a pale rectangle can still be misread as a coloured drop shadow at a glance — the 1.5° rotation and the dissolve are what argue against that reading, and both are large-size cues; (5) the sibling design-review also owns a window form, and the separation rests on register (warm vs its cool tile), on materials rather than depth, and on this one having no reticle.
