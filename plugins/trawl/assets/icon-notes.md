# Trawl — icon notes

**Direction: "Deep Cast"** — hybrid of the studio's *Tahoe Gel-Glass* sub-register (c) *dark glass* × *Dark-Field Emissive*. The subject is literally a deep-water sweep, so the charcoal-teal cushion tile is earned rather than borrowed, and it sidesteps the corpus's template-default (stock glyph on an indigo ramp) that every AI-agent icon lands on. Runner-up: sub-register (b), saturated tile + white frosted net — rejected because a frosted *mesh* smears to nothing at 16px and white-on-hue is the corpus's named #2 failure.

**Glyph (subject-mined, device bank #5 + #21 + #22):** the net is one **solid gel bag** — wide mouth ring, steady taper, a gentle belly holding the catch — so the carrying silhouette is a filled shape and the mesh is only internal texture that drops out harmlessly when small. Warp strands land spread across the throat and run on through the cod-end; pinching them made it read as a balloon.

**Signature:** the catch glows *through* the net — cod-end gel and strands are authored *over* the warm core so hue visibly blends where they cross. That authored overlap is the era's craft tell and the one thing a flat pre-masked raster cannot fake. Risk taken: the mote field warms teal→amber as it converges, so colour carries the app's argument (cast wide, sift, converge) rather than decorating it.

**Palette:** two families only — deep-sea teal (`#1B4A59→#07131B` ground; `#CDF6FB/#5CC7D6/#1C7889` gel) and one warm accent reserved strictly for the catch (`#FFFAF0→#FFD98F→#FFA84E→#EE7027`). One soft top light plus the sanctioned second source, the emissive interior. No hard speculars.

**Audit — 11/12, zero failures on the non-negotiable 1–4.** Full-bleed 1024 artwork with the squircle as a *clip* (no baked corners or shadow); focal 592px = 57.8% of tile, optically centred; silhouette and 16px squint verified on real renders. #10 variant robustness passes by construction: four layers `#bg/#mid/#fg/#highlight` mapping 1:1 onto Icon Composer, identity carried by shape and value. Point deducted at the smallest size — at 16px the mouth ring flattens to a bar and can read as a lamp before it reads as a net.

**Register note:** the dark register is the *minority* pro/system register, making the light Tinted variant the weakest of the four — the net silhouette carries it there, the ground does not.
