A · icon.svghand-authored layered SVG, built by build_icon.py — vector alternate 11 / 12 Passes all four non-negotiables and remains the maintainable artifact: geometry and material are named constants in build_icon.py, so a change is a parameter edit. Ground sampled from the family's own icons rather than Apple's cooler porcelain. Kept as the vector alternate because it is what survives a palette change or a variant requirement, and because C cannot be edited at all. It lost on material, which is the one thing the brief asked to improve. Worth recording that the defect reported three times against this take was never its wedge placement: measured on the render, the hole spanned -80 to -31 degrees and the ember -80 to -30, identical. The fault was a visible track making the gap read as a filled segment; removing it fixed the read. Two rounds were spent moving the wrong element.
C · icon-engineC-clean.pngGPT Image 2, corpus-referenced raster, deframed and remasked — SHIPS 10 / 12 Wins the material read outright, and once deframed it wins outright. The original failed check 1 by baking its own dark rounded-square frame inside the tile; that frame cropped away cleanly (inner tile at x 78-948, y 66-953, aspect 0.981), and the content was squared, scaled full bleed and remasked with the family superellipse. What remains is genuine gel: volumetric body, strong rim, authored translucency, richer than anything the vector master reached. Silhouette and 16px both hold. Loses two points. Check 10, variant robustness: a baked raster cannot adapt to Dark, Clear or Tinted at all, where the vector could. And it is not editable, so a future palette change means regenerating rather than changing a constant. Both are real costs of shipping pixels, accepted deliberately.
B · icon-engineB-arrow.svgArrow 1.1 vector take 6 / 12 Real vector and cleanly editable, but the geometry is wrong in the way that matters. The ring is nearly complete with only a small notch, so it does not read as a gauge that is nearly full, which is the entire concept. A second stray arc sits at the lower right with a visible seam. The ground is flat with no vignette or rim light, so the tile reads as a print rather than a cushion, and it supplies its own rounded-rectangle corners instead of the family superellipse. Nothing salvaged.
Recommendation: ship C (icon-engineC-clean.png). The raster wins the material judgment decisively and its only disqualifying fault, a baked dark frame, turned out to be croppable rather than inherent. Deframed and remasked with the family superellipse it passes checks 1 to 4 on real renders, and this is a decorative marketplace mark rather than an Icon Composer package, so nothing downstream needs the layers. Known liabilities to revisit: a baked raster fails check 10 outright, since it cannot adapt to Dark, Clear or Tinted, and it cannot be edited, so any palette or geometry change means a fresh generation rather than a constant. The vector master A is kept alongside for exactly that reason and remains the fallback if the mark ever needs to be variant-aware. The crop also cost a little of the original framing, so C sits marginally tighter in the tile than an authored-to-the-squircle take would.
Rubric: the 12-point icon audit from icon-directions.md (mask discipline, grid, silhouette, 16px identity, single light model, palette discipline, figure-ground, depth coherence, focal sizing, variant robustness, texture restraint, era fit) — checks 1–4 non-negotiable, delivery bar ≥10/12.
