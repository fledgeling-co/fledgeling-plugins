A · icon.svghand-authored layered SVG, emitted by build_icon.py — SHIPS 11 / 12 Ships. Passes all four non-negotiables on real renders. Round 7 changed three things, each measured rather than adjusted. The ember is redder. Sampled over the full 1024 render, its mean went (225,124,70) to (236,102,64) against take C's (235,110,77), and its luminance spread 0.376 to 0.407 against C's 0.424. The ramp is C's hue, not the family accent's: C sits at 10.5° at its dark end and 14.6° at its lit end where the old ramp ran 18–26° and read orange. The hole is tighter. 80° with 20° clear each side became 66° with 13°, chosen off a sweep of 10–14: below 12 the clearance on the ring's clockwise side stops resolving at 32px, and clearance on both sides is what stops the segment reading as continuous with what is occupied. The wedge stands proud. 10% of the band width past the ring's outer radius, so the outer circle is deliberately broken — measured on the render, the graphite arc ends at r=331 and the ember at r=345. What did not change. The inner boundary. It is 189–190 at every angle sampled through both segments, because band() still emits the whole material stack for either arc and the protrusion is a parameter it takes, not a second code path. Loses one point on 11: a ring gauge is legible but conventional, and no amount of material fixes that.

Recommendation: ship A (icon.svg). It is the only take carrying the composition the mark needs, and it clears every non-negotiable on real renders: designed for the family superellipse, optically centred, nameable as a solid, and legible at 16px where the ring, the hole and the reclaimed segment still separate. Round 7's three changes all measure toward take C without giving anything back to it: ember mean (236,102,64) against C's (235,110,77), ember spread 0.407 against 0.424, ring spread 0.450 against 0.439, figure-ground 3.02:1 against C's own 2.50:1, and the Pareto gate ACCEPTs at every one of the five sizes for the first time in this fixture. The 32/16px liability is closed rather than restated. A radial profile through the key side found what the judges were describing: at full strength the specular lifted the stroke's outer third to L 0.53 against a body of 0.23 at 32px, so the lit edge dissolved toward the porcelain. The 64px and smaller rasters now render from icon-small.svg with the specular's peak opacity at 0.30, which drops that lift to +0.18 and raises 32px self-contrast from 0.733 to 0.741. The 1024 master is untouched. This is the size-aware parameter the previous round named as the fix, not a retreat from the material. The blind panel agrees, including where it previously did not. Two families (Claude Opus, grok-4.5 via cursor-agent), seeing only renders and not knowing which take was which, voted this round the winner on overall, material and small size, with silhouette a tie — where round 6's panel had preferred the older take at 32 and 16px. Both named the accent's chroma as the reason at small size. Note the honest limit: the panel's small-size strips are rendered from the master, so it judged the un-floored specular; the rasters that ship at those sizes are the floored ones, which measure strictly better. Known liabilities: (1) at 16px the tighter hole costs some clarity — the clearance pixels read L 0.79 and 0.89 against a ground of 0.94, where at 20° a side they read 0.95 and 0.92. The break is still present and still much lighter than the object, but it is softer. At 32px both gaps still reach full ground. (2) The protrusion is sub-pixel below about 128px (0.44px at 32), so it is a large-size feature that pays nothing and costs nothing small. (3) The ember's darkest pixel is (213,55,25) at saturation 0.88 against C's (227,21,1) at 1.00 — the dark end is still shallower and less saturated than the reference's, the one place the ramp did not fully close. (4) The third judge family (gpt-5.6-sol) still did not run, for a missing API key, so the panel is two families rather than three — recorded in fidelity/runs/r03/panel/verdict-openai.json, never silently dropped. (5) A ring gauge remains a conventional device, which is the one rubric point this mark has never held.

The sweeps this round decided from

Neither the hole nor the protrusion was picked; both were swept and measured, and the sweep renders are in fidelity/runs/r03/sweep/. Hole. GAP_HALF 30–34, i.e. 10–14° clear each side of the 40° ember, rendered at 1024/32/16. At 32px the clockwise-side clearance peaks at L 0.856 against a ground of 0.941 for 10–12° and only opens to full ground at 13°; the ember must keep clearance on both sides or it reads as continuous with the used arc. 13° chosen. Protrusion. PROTRUDE_FRAC 0–13% of the band width. Take C's own wedge measures 48% proud, but it is a separate triangle that also floats 11px clear of the ring's inner radius, which is the composition this mark rejects. 10% reads as unmistakably proud at 256 and above while the segment still belongs to the band. Self-contrast at 32 and 16px is flat across the whole protrusion sweep, because 10% of the band is 0.44px at 32. Ember ramp. Three sweeps, the first two discarded for measuring the wrong thing: a multiplicative value gain clipped three of five face stops at V=1.0 and collapsed the cross-section while the whole-image spread still rose (the specular owns that number), and an error term over R and G alone bought its score by driving blue to zero. The kept ramp is an affine remap into V 0.82–1.00 with a 9°→14° hue tilt and 5% more saturation, scored on all three channels at p10 and p50.
