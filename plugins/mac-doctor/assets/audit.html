Recommendation: ship A (icon.svg). It is the only take passing checks 1 to 4 on real renders. This is a decorative marketplace mark rather than an Icon Composer package, so the material belongs in the file; nothing downstream will apply glass a second time. Material provenance is explicit: the ground and the gel falloff were sampled from the family icons and the Apple corpus, and the edge treatment was rebuilt from take C after C won the material judgment. Known liabilities to revisit: the master is still less volumetric than C at 1024, and closing that gap properly means a bounded fidelity run against a raster whose composition is corrected first, since converging on the current C would import its baked frame. Check 10, variant robustness, is untested; the mark has not been rendered against Dark, Clear or Tinted grounds, and a warm porcelain ground is exactly the kind that can collapse under Tinted. The ring gauge is legible but conventional, which is where the one lost rubric point sits.
