### 2. UI and UX

Rules below are from the CarPlay HIG, the Widgets HIG, or the October guide. The CarPlay HIG’s own changelog still says “May 2, 2023,” but the fetched page contains the HomeView sentence, so the page has moved since that log line. [CarPlay HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/carplay)

**Liquid Glass.** The CarPlay HIG does not mention it. Templates are drawn by the system, so an app does not apply a material to a list or a tab. Liquid Glass shows up in the Widgets HIG: a clear Home Screen appearance desaturates the widget and adds the Liquid Glass material, and CarPlay widgets are specified as full-color with the background removed. On HomeView, the Home Screen offers light, dark, clear, and tinted, and the Modular face strips the background and, below an ambient-light threshold, renders the widget in monochromatic red, the same treatment as StandBy night mode. [Widgets HIG, Appearances and the rendering-mode table](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/widgets)

**Template limits, from the October guide.**

- List: read `maximumItemCount` and `maximumSectionCount` at runtime. Some cars show 12 items, and the app must handle that. A Frameworks Engineer in December 2025 said 500 is the app-side cap and that images are not lazy-loaded. [thread 798894, Dec 2025](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/798894)
- Point of interest: at most 12 locations. Quick food ordering: recent orders or favourites, at most 12 each. No full menu.
- Tabs: read `maximumTabCount`. Current iOS allows 4 for audio and 5 for every other category, and the guide says not to hard-code those numbers. A Now Playing button in the tab bar may disappear above 4 tabs.
- Depth, including the root: 5 for audio, communication, EV charging, parking, public safety, and navigation. 3 for fueling and voice-based conversation. 2 through iOS 26.3 and 3 from iOS 26.4 for driving task and quick food ordering. Food ordering was previously described as two levels on the list-template page. The guide is the later, finer rule.
- Voice control, from iOS 26.4: at most two action buttons, plus leading and trailing bar buttons. From iOS 27 it may also be an overlay, including over a map. The app must show it while voice is active.
- Only a list may be pushed on top of Now Playing.

**Glanceability and the locked phone.** The HIG says to put primary content in the upper half, keep the layout scannable from the driver’s seat, and supply @2x and @3x art for light and dark. Common canvases are 800×480, 960×540, 1280×720, and 1920×720. The app must work while the iPhone is locked and must not send the driver back to the phone. Files protected with `NSFileProtectionComplete` or `NSFileProtectionCompleteUnlessOpen`, and keychain items accessible only when unlocked, are unavailable. [CarPlay HIG, Layout, iPhone interactions, Error handling](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/carplay) [Guide, pp. 5, 29–30](https://developer.apple.com/download/files/CarPlay-Developer-Guide.pdf)

**Day and night.** CarPlay supports light and dark and may switch with cabin light. Read `contentStyle` on the scene and update on `contentStyleDidChange`. Test colour in a real car. HomeView’s night behaviour for widgets is the red monochrome treatment above.

**Voice, Siri, and App Intents.** Communication apps must implement the three messaging intents, or CallKit plus `INStartCallIntent`. Audio and communication lists may show an assistant cell. A Frameworks Engineer wrote in June 2026 that Siri plus App Intents is enough for a voice path in the car, and that the conversational entitlement is what unlocks templates beyond system UI. [thread 831674, 10 Jun 2026](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/831674) Conversational apps must launch into voice, answer and act, hold an audio session only while voice is in use, and not answer a query with text or imagery. Recording is otherwise forbidden in CarPlay. Navigation and conversational apps may record only while the voice-control template is on screen. [Guide, pp. 8, 30](https://developer.apple.com/download/files/CarPlay-Developer-Guide.pdf)

**What review rejects.** These are the guide’s rules, which the CarPlay Entitlement Addendum incorporates. No games or social networking. Never show message, text, or email bodies. Audio apps never show lyrics. Never tell the driver to pick up the iPhone. Every flow has to be completable on the car screen and meaningful while driving. Driving-task apps cannot refresh UI faster than every 10 seconds, or points of interest faster than every 60 seconds, and cannot be a store finder. EV, fueling, and parking maps show only their own kind of location. Quick food ordering is drive-through or pickup, not a supermarket. Video apps exist to play video and must support AirPlay video. Navigation’s base view is a map only, and route guidance stops immediately when the car cancels it. [Guide, pp. 5–8](https://developer.apple.com/download/files/CarPlay-Developer-Guide.pdf)

HomeView’s own viewing distance is a product fact, a 7-inch 1088×1088 display at 220 ppi and 500 nits, not a third-party layout guide. [Technical specifications](https://www.apple.com/apple-homeview/specs/) <MISSING_DATA>A HomeView HIG for CarPlay-projected apps, with type sizes or safe areas for the square display, was not found. The CarPlay HIG still specifies landscape car canvases.</MISSING_DATA>

### 3. Code

Every CarPlay app declares a scene. The guide’s manifest puts a phone `UIWindowScene` and a `CPTemplateApplicationScene` under `UIApplicationSceneManifest`. The delegate conforms to `CPTemplateApplicationSceneDelegate`, keeps the `CPInterfaceController`, and sets a root template in `templateApplicationScene(_:didConnect:)`. The app can launch into CarPlay with no phone UI. Only navigation apps receive a drawable `CPWindow`. [Guide, pp. 31–32](https://developer.apple.com/download/files/CarPlay-Developer-Guide.pdf) [CPTemplateApplicationScene](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/carplay/cptemplateapplicationscene)

Now Playing is the shared `CPNowPlayingTemplate`. Configure it at connect, because the system can present it from the car’s Now Playing button before your list is up. From iOS 27, `allowsMiniPlayer` defaults to true. Metadata and remote commands are the system now-playing item. The CarPlay Music sample pushes lists, sets a tab bar as the root, and listens for `MPMusicPlayerController` playback and now-playing notifications. A custom player publishes the same item through `MPNowPlayingInfoCenter` and `MPRemoteCommandCenter`. [Guide, pp. 22, 33–34](https://developer.apple.com/download/files/CarPlay-Developer-Guide.pdf) [Integrating CarPlay with Your Music App](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/carplay/integrating-carplay-with-your-music-app)

Video does not embed an `AVPlayerViewController` in a template. The video framework page says to start playback with AVKit or AVFoundation. `CPPlaybackConfiguration` with `preferredPresentation: .video` asks the system to present its video player when `CPSessionConfiguration.videoPlaybackSupported` is true. `.audio` presents Now Playing. The same binary is what HomeView runs. [Video](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/carplay/video) [CPPlaybackConfiguration](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/carplay/cpplaybackconfiguration)

Widgets: support `.systemSmall`. If the widget is a game, needs repeated tapping, depends on data-protection class A or B, or exists to launch a non-CarPlay app, set `.disfavoredLocations([.carPlay], for: [.systemSmall])`. `WidgetLocation` as documented has `carPlay` and no `homeView` case. [WidgetLocation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit/widgetlocation) <INFERENCE from="Widgets HIG lists HomeView as a system-small context; WidgetLocation publishes carPlay and not homeView; agreement 6.4.1 names disfavoredLocations for CarPlay suitability">There is no documented way to allow a widget on HomeView while disfavouring it in the car, or the reverse. Disfavouring `.carPlay` is the only opt-out the API exposes, and the agreement discusses suitability in driving terms.</INFERENCE>

**(a) Audio app.** One scene delegate, one list, the shared Now Playing template. The entitlement is `com.apple.developer.carplay-audio`. This is the HomeView path for a music, podcast, or audiobook app.

```swift
import CarPlay
import MediaPlayer

final class CarPlaySceneDelegate: UIResponder, CPTemplateApplicationSceneDelegate {
    private var interfaceController: CPInterfaceController?

    func templateApplicationScene(_ scene: CPTemplateApplicationScene,
                                  didConnect interfaceController: CPInterfaceController) {
        self.interfaceController = interfaceController
        CPNowPlayingTemplate.shared.updateNowPlayingButtons([
            CPNowPlayingPlaybackRateButton { /* change rate */ }
        ])
        if #available(iOS 27, *) {
            CPNowPlayingTemplate.shared.allowsMiniPlayer = true
        }
        let item = CPListItem(text: "Album", detailText: "Artist")
        item.handler = { _, completion in
            MPNowPlayingInfoCenter.default().nowPlayingInfo = [
                MPMediaItemPropertyTitle: "Album",
                MPMediaItemPropertyArtist: "Artist"
            ]
            try? AVAudioSession.sharedInstance().setCategory(.playback)
            try? AVAudioSession.sharedInstance().setActive(true)
            interfaceController.pushTemplate(CPNowPlayingTemplate.shared, animated: true, completion: nil)
            completion()
        }
        let list = CPListTemplate(title: "Library",
                                  sections: [CPListSection(items: [item])])
        interfaceController.setRootTemplate(list, animated: true, completion: nil)
    }

    func templateApplicationScene(_ scene: CPTemplateApplicationScene,
                                  didDisconnect interfaceController: CPInterfaceController) {
        self.interfaceController = nil
    }
}
```

**(b) Voice-based conversational app.** Entitlement `com.apple.developer.carplay-voice-based-conversation`, minimum iOS 26.4. Launch into the voice-control template, record only while it is visible, and do not render the answer as text. This binary does not, on current documentation, appear on HomeView. A system-small widget is the HomeView surface.

```swift
import CarPlay
import AVFoundation

@available(iOS 26.4, *)
final class ConversationSceneDelegate: UIResponder, CPTemplateApplicationSceneDelegate {
    private let listening = CPVoiceControlState(
        identifier: "listening", titleVariants: ["Listening"], image: nil, repeats: true)
    private let speaking = CPVoiceControlState(
        identifier: "speaking", titleVariants: ["Speaking"], image: nil, repeats: true)

    func templateApplicationScene(_ scene: CPTemplateApplicationScene,
                                  didConnect interfaceController: CPInterfaceController) {
        let voice = CPVoiceControlTemplate(voiceControlStates: [listening, speaking])
        voice.activateVoiceControlState(withIdentifier: "listening")
        interfaceController.setRootTemplate(voice, animated: true, completion: nil)
        let session = AVAudioSession.sharedInstance()
        try? session.setCategory(.playAndRecord, mode: .voiceChat, options: [.allowBluetoothHFP])
        try? session.setActive(true)
    }

    func templateApplicationScene(_ scene: CPTemplateApplicationScene,
                                  didDisconnect interfaceController: CPInterfaceController) {
        try? AVAudioSession.sharedInstance().setActive(false, options: [.notifyOthersOnDeactivation])
    }
}
```

What else breaks under the iOS 26 and 27 SDKs, from Apple’s signing article rather than from the library: automatic signing does not install the app into CarPlay, a missing entitlements file does not either, and a video app stays hidden in a simulator configuration that lacks video playback. Keep the JavaScript bridge to the templates the granted category allows. Pushing a map template from an audio app throws.

HomeKit and Matter stay on the existing `HomeKit` and Matter frameworks in the iOS app. HomeView, once it is a hub in the home, controls those accessories through the Home app. There is no HomeView-specific accessory call.

### What changed, and the trajectory

From June 2025 through November 2026 the car gained widgets and Live Activities (iOS 26), a conversational category (iOS 26.4), and a real video category plus voice control everywhere (iOS 27). HomeView, announced 13 October and on sale 13 November, reused the two media entitlements and the system-small widget rather than opening a platform. The October guide simultaneously tightened the entitlement rule to one category per app. The direction of the documents is a wider set of categories on the phone, and a narrow, already-shipping projection onto the home screen. A conversational HomeView would be a new gate on that projection, and the only evidence for it is a beta string.

### Submission checklist

1. One category, matching the primary task. Audio or video if the app itself must appear on HomeView. Conversational if the car UI is a voice agent, and a system-small widget for the home screen.
2. Request the entitlement from the account holder before the UI is finished. There is no published SLA.
3. Capability on the App ID, manual provisioning profile, boolean key in the entitlements file, automatic signing off.
4. Scene manifest with `CPTemplateApplicationSceneSessionRoleApplication`. On the Expo app, a config plugin merges this into the React Native 0.88 manifest and does not leave `UIApplicationSupportsMultipleScenes` false.
5. Root template set in `didConnect`. Now Playing configured at connect for audio and video. Voice-control template on screen for the whole time a conversational app holds the microphone.
6. Template list restricted to the category. Depth, tab count, and the 12-item car limit respected. No message bodies, no lyrics, no “pick up your iPhone.”
7. `CPPlaybackConfiguration` set per playable row. Video presentation only when `videoPlaybackSupported` is true. AirPlay video supported if an audio app also plays video.
8. Widget: `.systemSmall`, background removable, `.disfavoredLocations([.carPlay])` if it needs an unlock or repeated taps. Live Activity: `ActivityFamily.small` if it should be in the car.
9. CarPlay Simulator on a USB iPhone, locked-phone pass, light and dark, then one wireless head unit. HomeView only if a device is available. Do not claim the square display was tested from the car simulator.
10. Disclose CarPlay API use in the App Store Connect review notes, per addendum section 4. Ship the entitlement only when every user should see the icon, including TestFlight.
