Recommendation: ship A (icon.svg). It is the only take with a real layer plan — bg / mid / fg / highlight as named groups, identity carried by shape and value rather than by hue — so it is the only one that can survive Dark / Clear / Tinted, and the only one whose geometry is a parameter edit next time. Checks 1–4 were read on the renders above rather than assumed: family squircle, optical centring nudged +10px against the shadow bias, a nameable filled silhouette, and 0.179 RMS luminance contrast at 16px. Known liabilities to revisit: (a) the three porcelain plates measure 1.18:1 against the cushion, so at 16px the deck behind the leading slab is carried almost entirely by its cast shadows; (b) take A measures flatter than C1 at every size (own-contrast 0.46–0.49 against C1’s 0.53–0.55), which is real material still missing from the gel faces; (c) #10 is satisfied by construction, not by test — the tinted and Clear variants have not been rendered; (d) four rounded rectangles is one step from a card stack, and report owns stacked document cards, so the climbing title band is doing all the differentiating work at 16px; (e) the fidelity score ran on the numpy tier with no torch, so the material gap in (b) is measured by luminance, SSIM and edges only and the number is not trustworthy as a material verdict.
