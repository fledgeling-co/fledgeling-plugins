# Content-Area Ideation: Distinct Inside the Native Envelope

Chrome stays native. Variety lives in the content area, the identity tokens, and the one signature move. Use this at step 2 of `SKILL.md`, after the corpus cluster is named and before the metric block.

The corpus is a taste education, not a style library (`SKILL.md` Variety discipline). Copying a cluster's surface without its discipline produces the templated Mac-app mock this skill exists to prevent: Warm Paper writing app, Terminal Dark developer tool, every time.

## What may vary, and what may not

**May not.** Titlebar height, traffic lights, control ladder, 13pt body, 10pt floor, label tiers, 8pt grid, menu-bar presence, keyboard and menu pairing. These are kit and Human Interface Guidelines. A direction that restyles them is a reskin, and the gate will say so.

**May.** Identity tokens inside the envelope: ground, one accent (or the choice to withhold it), display treatment of the *content* (the document, the readout, the gallery), density of the content list, the signature move, and the first-run empty state.

Warm Paper and Terminal Dark remain simultaneously corpus-proven and the two looks models default to. When the aesthetic axis is free, they are the runner-up, not the pick (`design-directions.md` calibration note).

## When to run `trawl:trawl`

Only for the **content-area world**, never for chrome. The packet is "what does this app's document / readout / gallery feel like", not "invent a new titlebar".

Use `--any` (two frames). Standard tier is the wrong spend for a settings pane. Skip entirely when:

- the corpus cluster is forced by audience × subject in the picker and the user did not ask for divergence
- the brief names an aesthetic
- this is a targeted edit

If `trawl:trawl` is not installed, say so and subject-mine the app's own world (the log, the score, the photo, the quota) for the signature instead.

## Session and project ledger

Track directions, palettes, and glyph types already produced this session (`SKILL.md` Variety discipline) **and** the project ledger at `<project>/.design-craft/diversity-ledger.json`. Native chrome is never a rotation axis. The next commission may not reuse the winning content-area direction or the same signature class (hero figure, sparkline, mascot, paper texture, notch merge) unless the corpus picker or an explicit aesthetic forces it.

```bash
python3 plugins/design-craft/skills/design-craft/scripts/diversity_ledger.py check   <project>/.design-craft/diversity-ledger.json --kind mac-content   --family "Warm Paper" --signature "36pt tabular quota"
```

Write four lines into the spec, then `record --kind mac-content` after the direction is settled:

```
CONTENT-AREA IDEATION
  cluster: Instrument (runner-up: The Notch)
  signature: 36pt tabular quota, one sparkline, no second accent
  declined: Warm Paper — free axis, and the last commission used it
  trawl: --any, frames: night-shift SRE × wild (kitchen timer)
  ledger: recorded | skipped-absent | override: <reason>
```
