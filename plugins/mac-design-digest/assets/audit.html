The 12-point rubric, scored 19 Aug against the renders

CheckResult
1. Mask discipline — designed for the squircle, no baked corners or shadow (non-negotiable)PASS — full-bleed 1024 with the family superellipse read from create-mac-icon/assets/squircle-path.txt and applied as a clipPath. No baked corner radius and no baked tile shadow; every shadow in the file belongs to an object inside the clip.
2. Grid adherence — optically centred, safe-zone margins (non-negotiable)PASS — the plate is 680px wide at x=172, so its left and right margins are identical, and 536px tall at y=232, sitting 24px above centre for optical balance. At 66.4% of tile width it is a shade over the 55–65% composition constant; noted, not deducted.
3. Silhouette test — nameable filled solid black (non-negotiable)PASS — filled to one tone it is a rounded rectangle with a lobed disc breaking its lower-right corner. That is nameable as a plate with a seal on it, and the seal is what stops the silhouette being a generic card.
4. 16px squint — survives menu-bar and Spotlight (non-negotiable)PASS — verified on master-32.png at true 16px and again at ×6: a pale plate, one dark bar across its upper half, one warm disc lower right. The label and footnote rules dissolve, which is the intended order of reads.
5. Single light modelPASS — one key from the upper left. The plate takes a white rim on its top-left edges and falls to #E4DFD3 bottom-right; the seal's own rim and its cast shadow on the plate both agree with that direction.
6. Palette economy — ≤2 hue families, accent reserved for the focalPASS — warm porcelain, neutral slate marks, and one vermilion. ACCENT_HI/ACCENT/ACCENT_DEEP are three values of the same hue rather than a second family, and the accent is spent on the seal and nowhere else.
7. Figure-ground — glyph vs ground ≥3:1, survives grayscaleFAIL — the plate fill #F7F4ED against the ground #E7E4DC is 1.16:1. The main mass is separated from its field by a hairline edge and a contact shadow, not by value, so in grayscale the plate very nearly disappears and what remains is a dark bar and a mid-grey disc floating on porcelain. The glyph-level numbers do clear the bar — the figure bar reads 6.85:1 against the plate and the seal 3.34:1 — so this is a deduction on the object, not on the marks.
8. Depth coherence — planes ordered, shadows match lightPASS — ground, then plate above it, then a countersunk socket cut into the plate, then the seal proud in the socket. Four planes in a consistent order with the socket's dark side and the seal's cast shadow on opposite sides, which is what makes the seal read as pressed in rather than drawn on.
9. Era coherencePASS — cushioned ground, soft single key, no hard specular. The seal's highlight is a soft crescent rather than a gloss dot.
10. Variant robustness — survives Default/Dark/Clear/Tinted, identity not hostage to one background (76% of the corpus fails here)FAIL — on the variant read alone, and the structural half of this row is now closed. The sheet first recorded 0 named <g id> layers in icon-src.svg, measured; that was fixed in build_icon.py on 19 Aug and fidelity.py structure now reports 5 named layers (art / bg / mid / fg / highlight) and passes. The wrappers carry no presentation attributes, so the 1024 render came back byte-identical at rms 0.000000 with no changed bounding box, and the three shipped PNGs needed no re-export. What still drops the point: the identity is porcelain-on-porcelain by the #7 measurement above, so the moment the ground changes register the plate stops separating and the seal carries the icon alone. Dark, Clear and Tinted were never rendered, so that half is a reasoned read rather than a capture.
11. Personality — ≥1 nameable device beyond glyph-on-gradientPASS — a twelve-lobed press seal seated in a visible countersunk impression. Not a document glyph, not a checkmark, not a gear.
12. No-textPASS — the recorded value is three abstract bars. No glyphs, no words, no screenshot.
Recommendation: ship take A (icon-src.svg → icon.png / icon-256.png / icon-128.png), 10 / 12. It clears all four non-negotiable checks on real renders, its material is authored in the file rather than photographed into it, and the seal-in-a-socket device is the only one of its kind in the marketplace. It sits exactly on the delivery bar, which is worth saying plainly: two points down, both of them real. Known liabilities to revisit, in the order a revision should take them: (1) The layer plan is missing (#10). This is a defect in the master, not in this sheet: the four strata need authoring as named <g id="bg|mid|fg|highlight"> groups in build_icon.py. Until that lands, audit_sheet.py check fails here on the structure gate. Deliberately not fixed on this pass, because it changes the shipped artwork. (2) Figure-ground at 1.16:1 (#7). The plate is carried by its hairline and its shadow. Taking the plate a step lighter or the ground a step darker is a two-constant edit and would close it, but it would also change every shipped raster, so it belongs in the same revision as (1). (3) One take, not three. Engines B and C never ran, so nothing on this page is a comparison — a single take written up as though a set had been judged is the failure mode this marketplace has already had once, and the shortfall is stated at the top rather than hidden here. (4) Variants unrendered. Dark, Clear and Tinted were never captured, so the #10 verdict rests on the structural measurement plus the value scheme, not on variant screenshots. (5) Focal width 66.4% against the 55–65% constant — the smallest of the five and the only one that may simply be right as drawn.
