Ships. First banner for this plugin, and it is the banner debt this plugin has carried since 18 Aug 2026, taken hours after take A2 was promoted to `icon-src.svg`.

Composition derived from the new master and from nothing else. Every number in `banner-src.html` is read out of this plugin's own `build_icon.py` and scaled by one factor, K = 0.72: the plate box 172/232/680/536 r 60, the three mark boxes with their 68px inset from the plate's left edge, the seal at 692/594 with r_out 112, r_in 95, 12 lobes, ring 66, boss 40 and a 132 countersink leaving the 20px collar, and the plate's own shadow of dy 18 against a 536 plate. The banner's ground is the icon's ground gradient restated: same centre at 42% / 34%, same three stops at 0, 0.62 and 1. The light axis is the glaze gradient's own (0.1, 0) to (0.88, 1), which is 45.3 degrees down-right, so the specular sits on the top-left arris and the porcelain fillet on the far bottom-right exactly as the master declares them. The tile's contact shadow is dy 9.67 and blur 11.82, which is the master's 18 and 22 taken as fractions of its 536 plate and re-applied to the 288px tile rather than borrowed from a sibling. `render_banner.py` records all of it under `derived` in its report, so row 6 is evidence rather than a claim in a comment.

Wordmark is Newsreader 600 at 70px, with IBM Plex Mono 450 carrying the two provenance marks. Both faces are already in the set and neither is a thirteenth: the pairing is the one `report` ships. Newsreader is this marketplace's document face, used by the three skills whose output is a written document somebody reads, and that is precisely what this skill produces: a persistent corpus with a ledger read first on every invocation, not a rendered surface. Choosing the house Instrument Sans would have filed it beside `mac-craft`, the generator, when this skill's own README says generation is somebody else's job. The mono has exactly one job here, which is to set `(measured)(canon)` in the corpus's own syntax, the same role `mac-craft` gives it.

The field is the same plate at reading size, cropped by the right edge and the bottom, carrying the master's own three inlaid marks and its own seal at their derived positions. Nothing in it is invented and nothing is added: the tile on the left is one entry seen as an icon, and the field is that same entry seen as a page. It fades in from the left so it never competes with the copy and it is held at 0.52 so the tile stays the darkest mass in the frame, the master's plate reading L 0.046 against the field's composite of roughly L 0.39.

Row 5, one warm accent. Held. The vermilion is spent on the seal and on the two provenance marks in the eyebrow, nowhere else, and both are the master's own ramp values. The seal rides its own 0.78 group rather than the field's 0.52, because inside the field group the wax composited to a pale salmon and a washed-out accent is the same defect as a second hue.

Row 4, ground register. Agrees. Porcelain, and it is the icon's own porcelain rather than an approximation of it.

Row 8, overflow. Clean, asserted by `render_banner.py` rather than by eye: no text node's own box passes 1600.

Row 9, legibility at 900px, a GitHub README column. Wordmark and essence both hold comfortably. The mono eyebrow lands at 9px there and is readable rather than comfortable, which is the honest read.

Row 10, legibility at 400px, the catalogue card. The wordmark holds. The essence becomes texture and the eyebrow becomes a grey and vermilion smudge, which is expected at 4px and is why the wordmark is the row that is checked.

Row 11, overlap. Nothing overlaps. The wordmark ends at roughly x 1045, the eyebrow at 1006 and the essence at 988, against a field plate that starts at 1128, so the narrowest clearance is 83px.

Three liabilities, recorded rather than closed. First, the field plate's left edge is still a visible boundary despite the fade, so at 1:1 it reads as a second object placed on the right as much as it reads as a page continuing past the frame. Pushing the mask ramp right would dissolve it and would also wash out the marks, which start only 49px inside the plate; that is a real trade and it has not been made. Second, the field's seal is 2.6 times the diameter of the tile's, so the accent's visual weight sits on the right rather than on the tile, and at 200px the two vermilion discs read as a pair rather than as an object and its enlargement. Third, at 200px the wordmark survives as a shape and not as letters, which is true of every long name in this set and is not specific to this face.

One thing this banner cannot fix, carried forward from the icon's own sheet. The shipping master reads as a payment card rather than as a sealed certificate, which is rubric #11 and the cost of the 19 Aug swap. The banner does not argue against that read; if anything the field plate at reading size makes the card proportion more legible, not less. The named next step for the icon, moving `SEAL_CX` / `SEAL_CY` outward so the seal straddles the plate edge, would change this banner's field as well, and re-rendering it is part of that commission rather than this one.

Rows 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 11 are one person's assessment rather than a panel's.
