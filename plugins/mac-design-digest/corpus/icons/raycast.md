This is almost certainly a corrupted / alternate / AI-upscaled web render, **not** the icon Raycast ships. ## Signature devices
- **Neon glow rim** — red light emanating from the bottom edge, bleeding up the sides (`#852E2E` at mid-left). A web/gaming-badge move, foreign to mac icon convention.
- **Baked bevel frame** — a grey squircle border ringing a darker inner panel; reads as a hand-drawn corner radius fighting the system mask.
- **Corner-crammed glyph with motion streaks** — the pointer mark pushed to the top-right with diagonal tick/streak marks suggesting a "ray" firing.
- **Baked wordmark** — "raycast" set in the lower third (the single most disqualifying device for a mac icon).

## Failures
- **#1 Mask discipline** — baked grey bevel frame + inner-panel border is a hand-drawn corner radius fighting the system squircle; render also sits squircle-on-white rather than full-bleed.
- **#2 Grid adherence** — dominant glyph is jammed into the top-right corner near the safe-zone edge, not optically centred; wordmark occupies the bottom band. No single centred anchor.
- **#3 Silhouette test** — filled solid, you get a square with a small corner tick + a text blob; two competing masses, no instantly-nameable single subject.
- **#4 16px squint test** — confirmed by downscale: the "raycast" wordmark collapses to an unreadable pink smear (`#6E4847`→`#B06F6F` blobs, no letterforms) and the glyph becomes a red dot. Fails Dock/Spotlight/menu-bar duty.
- **#5 Single light model** — bottom red glow vs. white glyph specular; a glow-based web light, not one coherent mac source.
- **#9 Era coherence** — devices are a cross-idiom mashup (skeuo-ish bevel frame + web neon glow + baked text); belongs to no single mac era's language.
- **#10 Variant robustness** — composition depends entirely on the dark panel + red glow; glyph and wordmark would vanish under clear/tinted/light renders.
- **#12 No-text check** — a literal "raycast" wordmark is baked into the artwork.

Soft passes (counted as passes, flagged): **#6** palette economy (red-monochrome, but accent not reserved to one focal); **#7** figure-ground (white glyph on charcoal is high-contrast, but the red-on-dark wordmark is weak); **#8** depth coherence (layers order sensibly, no z-fighting); **#11** personality (it does commit to a nameable dark-neon device — its one genuine virtue, even if derivative).
