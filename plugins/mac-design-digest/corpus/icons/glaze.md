# Icon: Glaze

- **Era:** **Custom** — a 3D-rendered iridescent glass object on a near-black studio field. Quotes two languages at once: **skeuomorphic glass materiality** (photoreal refraction, caustics, literal molten-glass surface) fused with **Liquid Glass** iridescence/translucency, but as *baked render art*, not system-composed Icon Composer layers. Not Big Sur (no front-facing tool-on-squircle), not native Liquid Glass (no light/dark/tinted variant logic). · **Rubric:** 11/12 (1 failure, 5 soft passes) · **Digested:** 2026-07-19

## Signature devices
- **Brand mark as molten glass marble** — the flat quatrefoil logo is rendered as a volumetric, back-lit fluid blob. Subject-mining the brand's own mark into 3D material rather than drawing an abstract glyph *about* the app. The whole personality budget is spent here. `[GOLDEN-NUGGET]`
- **Warm/cool thermal split** — an emissive amber core against a refractive indigo rim, inside a single object. Complementary temperature does the depth and drama work; it also reads as "molten glass," on-name for *Glaze*. `[GOLDEN-NUGGET]`
- **Caustic sparkle dust** — tiny suspended star/particle speckles inside the glass sell the "live fluid simulation" and add a jewel-like glint. Cheap richness, high frequency (first casualty at small sizes).
- **Obsidian gallery field** — the near-black vignette isn't a colour choice so much as a lighting rig; the glass only glows because the ground is dark. Dark-mode-native by construction.

## Failures
- **#10 variant robustness (Liquid Glass)** — the composition is *wholly* dependent on the dark ground and on full-colour iridescence. The subject **is** the multicolour glass, so there is no way to produce macOS 26 Mono / Tinted / Clear renders without destroying it (a mono or single-tint render collapses the amber-vs-indigo thermal split that is the entire idea). Not authored as appearance-aware layers; it is a fixed, dark-only piece of render art. Fails forward-compatibility with system tinting.

## Soft passes (borderline — scored pass, flagged for synthesis)
- **#1 mask discipline** — designed correctly for the squircle, but the delivered asset **bakes** the rounded corners (transparent) *and* an edge-bevel rim highlight into the PNG, rather than shipping a full-bleed unmasked layer for the system to round and light. Fine for a shipped/screenshot render; noted because HIG wants the bevel system-applied.
- **#3 silhouette test** — filled solid black, it is a puffy four-lobed pillow: pleasant, but an abstract quatrefoil, not an instantly-nameable object. It passes only because it *is* the brand mark (within-brand it reads as "the glaze shape"). Genericness risk: the 4-lobed blob is close to many abstract app marks.
- **#4 16px squint** — survives as a **glowing amber blob on black** (the high warm/dark contrast holds), but every internal structure — caustics, sparkles, refraction, the indigo rim detail — smears away below ~48px. At menu-bar size it is a colour smudge, not a legible object. Identity survives as a colour, not a shape.
- **#6 palette economy** — passes cleanly (2 families + neutral field), flagged only because the internal render packs a *wide tonal range* per family (bright `#F1CC3B` down to near-black rust) that could read as more than two hues in a thumbnail. The discipline holds; the richness is high.
- **#9 era coherence** — internally consistent, but it fuses skeuomorphic-glass photorealism with Liquid-Glass iridescence into a "custom" language that belongs to no single Apple era. Coherent as *one* contemporary render; not a clean single-era quotation.

## Provenance / caveats
- All hex `(measured)` from a 512px web render; `(inferred)` — single icon, single source. Corners transparent (pre-masked); edge bevel baked. No 1024 master, no dark/tinted variant shown.
- **Brand-coherence with `cover.jpg`:** partial-and-deliberate. The cover is a strictly **monochrome** system — pure black ground, flowing dark **liquid-glass** waves, a white quatrefoil mark + white "glaze" wordmark — with only faint **chromatic-aberration rainbow glints** on the wave crests. The icon shares the *black liquid-glass-on-black* motif and the quatrefoil silhouette, but **breaks monochrome**: it cranks those faint prismatic edge-glints into a full amber/indigo iridescent fill. So the icon is the loud, saturated member of an otherwise disciplined black-and-white brand — the marketing surface whispers glass, the app icon shouts molten glass. The shared thread ("liquid glass on black") is strong; the colour departure is the icon earning its Dock presence.
- **Era-lag note for synthesis:** unlike the flat Big-Sur dev-utility icons in the corpus, this AI-category icon is *ahead* of / adjacent to the current material language (it apes Liquid Glass) rather than lagging it — but does so as un-layered render art, so it still fails the actual system-variant test (#10). Evidence that AI-category icons chase the material trend while dev-utility icons keep shipping flat Big-Sur marks.
