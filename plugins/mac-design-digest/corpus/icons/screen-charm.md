- **Source:** macapp.supply / user · **Subject:** Video — "Mac Screen Recorder for Polished Videos" (zoom effects, cursor tracking, beautiful backgrounds; screencharm.com)
- **Resolution caveat:** 100×100 **WebP** web render (Apple masters at 1024). This is an unusually small source — barely 6× the menu-bar size — so the 16px squint (#4), grid adherence (#2), and any micro-detail are assessed from an already-small render, not a downscale of a 1024 master. Treat #2/#3/#4 as **provisional**. The PNG conversion shows the four corners baked **opaque white** (`#FFFFFF`), i.e. the squircle was already flattened onto white for the web — so mask discipline is inferred (composition sits well inside the mask), not verified from full-bleed unmasked layers. All hexes are `(measured)` from the render but are low-res approximations.

## Signature devices
- **Woven translucent triangle knot** `[GOLDEN-NUGGET]` — three overlapping rounded capsule bars form an open (hollow-center) triangle, the vertices alpha-blending where bars cross. This is near-identical in construction to the **Affinity suite** mark (Photo/Designer/Publisher), rendered here in a blue/indigo colorway. The entire identity is this one geometric device.
- **Two-tone cornflower→indigo ribbon** — the glyph's only "material": a flat brand gradient from sky-blue highlights to saturated indigo, with translucency doing the work that lighting would do in a Big-Sur icon.
- **Dark-premium field with faint center-glow** — a self-colored mark floated on near-black indigo, the current "premium indie" dark-icon idiom (Linear/Raycast/Arc register) rather than an Apple depth language.

## Failures
- **#5 Single light model — FAIL.** There is no light model at all: no top-down cast, no specular, no baked micro-shadow, no glass refraction. The two-tone is decorative, and the three ribbons each brighten at a different end, so no coherent source is implied. It is internally consistent (uniformly flat, not *mixed* lighting), so it is a *flat-by-choice decal* rather than a mixed-lighting defect — but against a macOS icon rubric expecting a committed depth/light treatment, it fails to commit. A generating AI should read this as the honest cost of the flat-brand-mark route.
- **#10 Variant robustness — FAIL.** The mark's whole identity is the **color-dependent two-tone weave**. It is not authored as separable Icon-Composer background/foreground layers, so under mono/tinted (and to a large degree dark) system variants the cornflower↔indigo split collapses to a single tint and the woven triangle flattens to an indistinct one-tone triangle. Unlike an object icon whose *silhouette* carries the meaning, here the silhouette is just "a triangle" — the personality does not survive the variant flatten.

**Soft passes** (pass, but flagged for synthesis):
- **#7 Figure-ground** — the **cornflower segments** (`#6C96FE`, L≈0.31 on ground L≈0.02) blow past 3:1 and carry the silhouette; the **indigo-only segments** (`#614AF3`, L≈0.13) measure only ~2.6:1 against the near-black field — **under the 3:1 floor** and nearly merging in grayscale. The glyph reads only because the light segments rescue it. Borderline toward failure.
- **#3 Silhouette** — filled solid, it's nameable as "a rounded triangle," but only generically; the distinguishing weave is a *color* event, not a *shape* event, so the silhouette alone is anonymous. (A triangle faintly nods at a play glyph, but it's an outline knot, not a play button.)
- **#4 16px squint** — the gross blue-violet triangle survives as a Dock/menu-bar presence, but the three-bar weave, the hollow center, and the vertex overlaps smear into a blob well before 16px. Provisional given the 100px source.
- **#8 Depth coherence** — depth is *implied* only by the alpha-overlap at the vertices; there is no committed over/under weave order (a true impossible-triangle) and no shadow to be consistent or inconsistent. No z-fighting, but no crisp depth either.

## Cross-icon / brand notes
- **Palette coherence with cover: tight.** The cover is the same deep navy/indigo field (faint grid) carrying white headline text with a periwinkle-violet accent (`~#8B7CF5` on "look stunning" and the CTA pill), and the identical woven-triangle mark beside the "Screen Charm" wordmark. The icon's cornflower→indigo ramp is the exact brand accent compressed into the logo. Brand = periwinkle/indigo on near-black — the icon is a faithful miniature.
- **Subject muteness — the key learning.** For a screen recorder promising "recordings that look stunning" (zoom, cursor tracking, backgrounds), the icon communicates **nothing about screen recording**: no camera, no record dot, no screen frame, no explicit play glyph. It is a pure abstract brand mark that would read equally as a design tool, a crypto app, or a productivity suite. The convention "an icon should communicate its subject" is deliberately traded away for a premium-generic brand mark. Committed direction (dark-premium), but subject-mute and derivative of Affinity.
- **Era honesty:** logged **custom**. It borrows the modern uniform-squircle, front-facing framing (post-Big-Sur) but the glyph commits to neither Big Sur's baked top-down micro-shadow depth nor Liquid Glass's specular/refractive layered glass — the alpha-overlap translucency is a flat *gesture* toward glass, not glass. "Flat two-tone brand decal on a dark squircle" is the honest description.
