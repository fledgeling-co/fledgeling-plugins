# Icon: Dia

- **Era:** liquid-glass (translucent gradient-glass blob on a near-white pedestal) · **Rubric:** 10/12 · **Digested:** 2026-07-19
- **Source:** macapp.supply — 204×204 PNG web render (not the 1024 master), corners already alpha-masked into the file; all hex `(measured)` from this render, gradient stops `(estimated)` — banding, true specular treatment and any edge refraction are below this resolution's reach
- **App:** Dia — "the browser for your best work" / "A browser you won't dread opening" (Productivity). The Browser Company's second browser. An icon whose whole job is to feel warm and human, not utilitarian — the subject is *mood*, not *tool*.

A single organic brand blob filled top-to-bottom with a full sunset spectrum — deep blue crown fading through a pale near-white horizon into gold, orange, and a coral-red foot — floated as a translucent glass layer on a white-to-lavender pedestal. The composition is Big-Sur-simple in bones (one glyph, soft top-down light, no tool overlay) but Liquid-Glass in dress (the blob reads as coloured glass tinting the ground beneath it rather than a flat fill casting a hard shadow). The spectrum *is* the identity; that same spectrum is also its two rubric liabilities.

## Palette (measured from 204px render)

- **Blob ramp, top → bottom:** `#3C71D3` (saturated blue crown) → `#4683DE` → `#5B94DE` → `#77A8DD` → `#96BCE1` (lightening blue) → `#D0DDDD` → `#E1E3C7` (pale near-white horizon band) → `#EBE2A1` → `#F2DC78` → `#F6CF53` (gold) → `#F9B73F` → `#F59440` (orange) → coral-red foot ~`#F26B4E`/`#F48055` at the scalloped rim
- **Pedestal:** `#FFFFFF` → `#E7E7F3` (vertical, subtle)
- **Accent:** none reserved — the spectrum *is* the palette

## Signature devices
- **Sunset / aurora spectrum ramp** `[GOLDEN-NUGGET]` — a blue→red vertical gradient read as sky-at-dusk poured into a brand shape. Optimism and warmth expressed purely through hue temperature travel; it is the single move the icon is remembered by.
- **Scalloped-bottom gumdrop mark** — an abstract, non-object silhouette (dome top, concave wave cut into the base). Consistent with the wordmark's logomark on the cover; the *shape* is the brand constant, the spectrum is the icon's special dress.
- **Glass-on-white pedestal** — the coloured layer floats as translucent glass and tints its near-white ground with a cool lavender contact-halo instead of a grey drop shadow. The Liquid-Glass "glass tints its ground" tell, done quietly.

## Failures
- **#6 Palette economy (FAIL):** four hue families in one glyph (blue → cyan-neutral → yellow → orange → red) with no single reserved accent. Deliberate — the full spectrum is the brand — but it is the opposite of the ≤2-family / accent-reserved rubric ideal. Recorded as a *signature-driven* fail, not a defect.
- **#10 Variant robustness (FAIL):** the identity is spectrum-dependent. On dark it survives (a coloured blob still reads), but a **tinted / mono** system render flattens the whole sunset to one hue and erases the entire point of the mark. The pale horizon band (`#E1E3C7`/`#D0DDDD`) also leans on the white ground for its read and would weaken on a dark pedestal. An icon whose signature cannot survive its own tinted variant. *Caveat: only the flattened web render was seen; the shipping Icon Composer asset may carry a purpose-built mono variant this render doesn't show.*

## Soft passes (flagged for synthesis)
- **#2 Grid:** optically centred on x, but the blob seats slightly high on y (more pedestal shows below than above); reads centred, pixel-precise grid adherence unverifiable at 204px.
- **#3 Silhouette:** distinctive and brand-consistent filled solid, but *abstract* — not a literal-object metaphor. Nameable as "a rising sun / a tooth / a rounded blob," not as one agreed thing.
- **#4 16px squint:** the blue-crown-to-warm-foot temperature travel survives and IDs the app; the concave bottom scallop smears away, reading as a plain dome. Colour signature holds, shape detail is lost.
- **#7 Figure-ground:** the blue crown and orange/coral foot clear ≥3:1 against white, but the pale horizon band nearly dissolves into the pedestal — in grayscale the blob reads as a blue cap + orange foot with a near-invisible middle.

## Rhymes with
- Full-spectrum / aurora-gradient brand-mark icons — a single organic shape poured full of a sky ramp. Style family: Arc browser's gradient blob, Apple's Photos/Siri spectral orbs, Raycast-class gradient marks. The seed of a possible icon cluster **"spectrum-gradient organic brand blob"** (translucent multi-hue glass shape on a near-white ground) — needs ≥3 independent icons to promote to ICONS.md canon.
- Brand-context note: the cover pairs this spectrum icon with a *monochrome white* version of the identical blob + an editorial **serif** "Dia" wordmark on stark black. The palette does not carry between icon and cover by design — the shape is palette-agnostic, the sunset is reserved as the app's face. A coherent, disciplined identity, not a mismatch.
