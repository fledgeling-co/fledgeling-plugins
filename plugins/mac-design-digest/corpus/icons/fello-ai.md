## Signature devices
- **Interwoven quaternary knot** `[GOLDEN-NUGGET]` — four laced rings in 4-fold rosette symmetry, over-under woven; the one distinctive move and an on-subject metaphor for a multi-model aggregator (strands = models).
- **Pearl/chrome tube material** — baked roundness gradient + contact-shadow occlusion at every crossing gives a soft glossy-plastic 3D read (Big Sur render, not glass).
- **Monochrome-on-charcoal restraint** — no accent hue at all; unusual discipline that also means the icon leans entirely on silhouette + material.

## Failures
- **#10 Variant robustness (Liquid Glass):** the near-white glyph depends on the dark background for its ~14:1 contrast. In a clear or light-tinted Liquid Glass render the knot would wash out — the composition does not carry a tintable silhouette independent of one background colour. (Root: it's a Big-Sur inverted-luminance icon, not authored as flat layers for macOS 26's variant system.)

Soft passes (scored as pass, flagged):
- **#4 16px squint:** the rounded 4-lobe rosette silhouette survives, but the over-under weave detail smears at menu-bar/Spotlight size — reads as "a knot/flower", specific interlacing lost.
- **#11 Personality:** distinctive as a form, but rhymes hard with the OpenAI blossom/knot mark lineage — memorable yet derivative of the AI-knot trope, not proprietary.

HIG note (not scored, current-era): baked highlights + AO shadows conflict with macOS 26 guidance (ship flat, unmasked layers; let the system apply specular/shadow). This is era-correct for Big Sur but lags Liquid Glass.
