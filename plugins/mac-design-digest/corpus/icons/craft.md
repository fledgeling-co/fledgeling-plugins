## Signature devices
- **Quartered pinwheel mark** — four rounded quarter-petals rotating around a plus-shaped negative-space gutter; the gutter is the frosted background showing through, not a drawn line.
- **Two-bright / two-dark quadrant split** — the top row (magenta + blue) glows while the bottom row (navy) grounds. A built-in light-weighting asymmetry that gives a flat geometric mark a top-lit read.
- **Saturated glass on a frosted field** — the segments float on a translucent near-white base rather than filling an opaque gradient squircle; the whole composition is layered-glass, not a solid brand-color tile.
- **Brand mark over subject metaphor** — a pure abstract identity mark with no pen/paper/document cue. Legible-brand strategy (Notion/Linear register), but the icon does not communicate what the app *does*; it relies on recognition.

## Failures
- **#10 Variant robustness (Liquid Glass):** the two navy quadrants and the light plus-gutter are near-white-background-dependent. A dark or clear render would sink the navy pair into the ground and would have to invert the gutter — only the magenta+blue identity survives a tinted/dark variant unaided.

## Soft passes (flagged, scored as pass)
- **#3 Silhouette:** filled solid black the mark reads as a coherent four-quadrant/plus shape, but it is abstract — nameable as *a mark*, not as an object. Quadrant identity leans on colour, which the silhouette test strips.
- **#5 Single light model:** top-lit specular is consistent across all four segments, but the 102px source can't fully confirm true specular vs. webp bloom.
- **#6 Palette economy:** two full-saturation hues (magenta + electric blue) share focus rather than one reserved accent over a subordinate field — within the ≤2-hue-family limit, but stretches "accent reserved for the focal element."

## Brand-context (icon vs cover)
- Cover is a soft pastel-sky scene (light blue #a8d4f0-range, white clouds, a torn/perforated paper edge) with "CRAFT" in a heavy rounded-geometric black display face. The icon and cover **share a rounded-geometric, friendly-airy language** (petal curves ↔ ball-terminal letterforms) but **diverge on palette**: the icon's electric blue is far more saturated than the cover's pastel sky, and the icon's magenta has no echo on the cover. Coherent in *shape mood*, loose in *colour*.
