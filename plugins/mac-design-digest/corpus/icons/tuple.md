## Signature devices

- **Paired translucent panes** `[GOLDEN-NUGGET]` — two offset frosted-glass rounded squares that *are* the wordmark: tuple = pair, and in context = two shared screens / two pairing developers. The whole brand idea in one shape.
- **Additive-translucency overlap** — where the panes cross, colour brightens (`#EEECF8`) instead of one occluding the other; this is the tell that the panes are genuine glass, and it separates the icon from a flat Big-Sur two-card design.
- **Single-hue violet sky-ramp** — the entire field is one violet, light-at-top → dark-at-bottom; no second hue anywhere. Coheres exactly with the brand wordmark logo on the cover (`#5F52D5` mark, `#CCC7F0` pane).

## Failures

None outright. Four soft passes carry the real critique:

- **#3 Silhouette (soft):** two overlapping rounded rectangles is on the generic side; filled solid black the offset still reads as "two stacked cards," but it's a low-distinctiveness shape versus a nameable object or tool.
- **#4 16px squint (soft):** the icon survives as "a bright panel on violet," but the front and back panes are close in value (`#D4CEF0` vs `#B4ACE1`) so the *two-ness* — the entire point of the name — tends to collapse into a single card at menu-bar/Spotlight size. This is the icon's most consequential weakness: it under-communicates "pair" exactly where it matters most.
- **#2 Grid (soft):** the pane cluster sits a touch high-and-left of geometric centre; the trailing back pane is what re-centres it optically. Balanced, but not planted.
- **#10 Variant robustness (soft):** the light panes don't depend on the violet field, so dark/tinted renders survive — but mono/tinted modes flatten the slight front/back hue delta that already carries the pairing, pushing #4's collapse further.

## Rhymes with

- Liquid-Glass / Big-Sur productivity icons built from **abstract overlapping translucent shapes** rather than a literal object or a diagonal tool — the Apple "stacked-pages / overlapping-cards" family (Freeform-like, Shortcuts-like layered motifs).
- Single-hue gradient-squircle icons where the glyph is *lightness on a saturated field* (glass panel over a sky-ramp), no second accent hue.
- Distinct from the diagonal-tool tradition (TextEdit/Preview) and from mascot/monogram icons — this is a pure abstract-glass mark.
