## Signature moves

- **The name is the icon is the joke:** "Waterlemon" = watermelon silhouette rendered in lemon yellow with dark seeds — the wordplay is carried entirely by the 3D mark. A genuinely memorable brand atom. `[GOLDEN-NUGGET]` (brand, not UI.)
- **Output-as-hero marketing:** the cover spends its whole lower half on five specimen icons rather than feature copy — showing capability instead of claiming it (demonstrate-don't-describe). Brand strategy, not app design.
- **Clean white gallery stage under a toy subject:** the saturated toy renders are floated on flat #FAFAFA with soft shadows — restraint around the color rather than a drenched playful background. If this discipline carries into the actual app chrome it would be worth confirming.
