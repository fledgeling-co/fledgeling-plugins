## Signature moves
- **[GOLDEN-NUGGET] Editorial serif as the entire brand voice.** A high-contrast literary serif (double-story g, teardrop terminals) — not a product sans — carries the identity. In the warm-indie-productivity register this is the whole personality in one type decision (cf. the serif-over-system-body move seen in editorial writing apps).
- **[GOLDEN-NUGGET] Warm iridescent aura around a hard white "spotlight."** A soft coral/peach/amber mesh gradient bleeding around a crisp white content-circle — atmosphere on the outside, discipline in the center. Warm-dominant with a single cool lilac note keeps it from reading generic-pastel.
- **[GOLDEN-NUGGET] Tactile object icon that rejects the platform glyph grammar.** A flocked-clay orange "Thinker" mascot rendered as a photographable object — no squircle field, no SF-Symbol abstraction, no Liquid-Glass layers. Memorable and un-appish; a deliberate brand-over-platform choice worth flagging (it will *not* sit natively beside stock Dock icons — that is the trade).
