## Signature moves

- **[GOLDEN-NUGGET — brand, not UI] The dark LED dot-matrix as an App Store leaderboard.** The background is a field of dark rounded-square cells with exactly one lit vivid-green cell riding at the top edge — a literal visual metaphor for an app climbing to #1. It is the app's entire proposition ("track your rankings, reach the top") compressed into one lit pixel against a dark grid, and it exploits the Von Restorff isolation effect: the single lit cell is where the eye lands. Genuinely good marketing invention — but it lives in the brand layer; there is no evidence it appears in the product UI.
