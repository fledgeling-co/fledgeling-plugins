## Signature moves (icon)

- **[GOLDEN-NUGGET] The page-curl peel as product thesis.** The blue "screenshot" sheet is lifted from the lower-left corner to reveal a dimmed desktop and five floating markup glyphs beneath. One gesture encodes the entire product — *capture a screenshot, peel it off the screen, annotate it* — with zero text. This is high information-scent (Jakob's Law / recognition-over-recall): the metaphor is legible at Dock size.

They are drawn from the macOS 12-hue system palette, tying a playful device back to platform colour.
- **Duotone-plus-confetti economy.** Two dominant hue families (electric-blue sheet, cool-graphite panel) carry ~90% of pixels; five saturated accents carry the rest. Disciplined palette, high figure/ground.
