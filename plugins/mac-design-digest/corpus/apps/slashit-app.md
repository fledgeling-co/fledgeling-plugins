## Signature moves

- **The forward-slash brand device** — the "/" replaces the ascender of the "S" in both the wordmark and the icon, and doubles as the accent's only saturated moment against an otherwise navy/monochrome system. It's literal subject-mining: the app triggers text expansion via slash-commands (Slack/Notion/Raycast convention), and the mark *is* that gesture. Systematic across both supplied assets → a genuine brand signature (not a UI signature — the UI is unseen).
- **Dot-matrix / halftone dither as texture** — the pixel-grid corner motif and the halftone-rendered typewriter evoke dot-matrix printing and early-computer text output. Cheap, thematically earned "designed-not-generated" texture that ties the visual world to *text/typing*. Brand-only.
- **Subject-mining coherence** — typewriter illustration for a text tool; the 50ms first-impression reads "typing / text" before a word is parsed. Aesthetic-usability leverage on the marketing surface.
