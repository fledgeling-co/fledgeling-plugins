## Signature moves
- **[GOLDEN-NUGGET] The icon is the gesture.** The glyph is the ␣ space symbol / a spacebar keycap — and Quick Look on macOS is invoked by pressing **spacebar**. The icon doesn't depict the app; it depicts the *action the user already performs*, leaning entirely on the trained convention (Jakob's Law). One decision carries the whole brand.
- **Monochrome discipline.** Zero hue anywhere — near-black stage, metallic-white glyph. In a Dock full of saturated squircles the absence of colour is the differentiator (Von Restorff by subtraction).
- **Demonstrative backdrop.** Rather than claiming "supports many formats," the cover *shows* ~100+ real file extensions as a constellation — description→experience: proof by display, not adjective.
