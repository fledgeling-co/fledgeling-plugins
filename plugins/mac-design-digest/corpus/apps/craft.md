## Signature moves

- **[GOLDEN-NUGGET] Scene-as-pitch.** The composition *stages the product concept* instead of describing it: an open sky of "big ideas" meets a white page (the writing surface) along a torn-notebook seam of ascending paper tabs. It is subject-mined — built from the product's own materials (paper, notebook, sky) — so the imagery is specific and ownable rather than a stock gradient. The memorable budget is spent on the built scene.
- **[GOLDEN-NUGGET] Riso-print register on a digital app.** Ben-day halftone screenprint grain + a bold rounded-deco wordmark gives a *printed-poster* feel to a software brand — a deliberate, committed direction (Playful/toy display type × Organic/soft grain over a near-drenched sky palette), not a template gradient. The dot texture is the cheapest, most reliable "hand-crafted" signal and it is used with restraint.
- **Bespoke wordmark as identity.** "CRAFT" is set in a custom single-story geometric-rounded display (circular bowls, flat cuts); brand identity rides entirely on the letterforms, with the near-black ink as the one Von-Restorff pop on a pale-blue field.
