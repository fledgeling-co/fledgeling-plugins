## Signature moves

- **[GOLDEN-NUGGET] Apple-heritage skeuomorphism as positioning.** The icon revives the discontinued Passbook/Wallet leather-and-stitching object language — a deliberate nostalgia signal that says "native to *your* Apple stuff" more literally than any flat glass icon could. In an "AI/MCP" category dominated by cold abstract-gradient marks, a warm tactile leather wallet is a genuine differentiator. (Icon-level signature; no UI signature observable.)
- **Ecosystem-by-iconography brand.** The whole value prop (any MCP AI ↔ every Apple app) is communicated through borrowed system-app icons and a connector graph rather than prose — recognition over explanation.
