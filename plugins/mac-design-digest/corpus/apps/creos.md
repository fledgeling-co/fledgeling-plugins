## Layout skeletons

**Marketing cover composite (1200×630, no window):** centred brand column — chrome emblem + "CREOS" wordmark, then a two-line white display headline, then a grey tagline — floated over a near-black stage. Around the four edges, a free-scatter **mosaic of save-cards** (deliberately off-grid, bleeding past the frame): two card archetypes — (a) *text-forward* cards = coloured content-type badge → bold title → 2–4-line secondary body (HOOK/SCRIPT/IDEA); (b) *media* cards = full-bleed photo thumbnail with overlay caption ("Café shoot ideas", "Colour grading · 5 saves"), platform provenance badge, and status line. A distinct *thread-notification* card (avatar, name, @handle, ✕, body, "Show this thread · 1/7") shows an ingested X/Twitter save. **This is brand + information-model evidence; it is not a screenshot of the running app.**

## Signature moves
- **[GOLDEN-NUGGET] The stage-and-paint inversion.** The UI is a pure near-black void (#050505) and the *user's own photographic saves supply 100% of the colour* — café, strawberries, coffee pour, cliffs, desk gear read as a curated gallery. The entire "second brain for creators" identity is carried by one decision: give the content the pigment and let the chrome be black. Peer logic: mymind, Pinterest, Cosmos.
- **Role-coded content-type tags** (HOOK amber / SCRIPT blue / IDEA green): a save isn't a bookmark, it's a *typed creative artifact*. Muted mid-saturation hues on dark translucent pills, ~10px tracked caps — chunks the corpus into 3–5 recall-friendly categories.
- **Brand tension:** a photoreal chrome/Y2K emblem (retro-futurist luxe) bolted to an otherwise restrained dark-mode content tool. Deliberate, coherent across icon + cover, but off-platform — communicates "premium marque" more than "creator second brain."

## Defects
- **Container contrast dilution (mild, intentional):** text cards (#111111) sit only ~6/255 above the #050505 backdrop → non-text container contrast <3:1 (rubric #10). Cards read as containers only via their content and a faint edge; a hard-edged product list would need more separation. Recorded as a moody-look house choice on a *marketing* surface, not a shipped-UI verdict.
- **Evidentiary defect (the important one):** no window chrome anywhere → lineage unverifiable → **contributes zero to macOS canon**. Covers also systematically over-polish; the real app may look nothing like this. A single real window screenshot would change this profile more than anything else.
- **Off-platform accent:** link + tags use a bespoke muted palette, not the system accent — a native-feel tell *if* it carries into the real app (unverified here).
