# Banner Design Specification: create-luke-content

## 1. Visual Hierarchy & Composition
- **Dimensions:** 1600 × 520 px (rendered at 2× retina: 3200 × 1040 px).
- **Layout:** Asymmetric horizontal split.
  - Left focal area (40% width): Shipped 1024px `icon.png` rendered with volumetric Tahoe shadow and depth.
  - Right content area (60% width): Structured typography stack.
- **Palette (Subject-Mined & Marketplace Calibrated):**
  - Background: Daylight porcelain `#f8f7f5` with fine sub-pixel grain and soft radial ambient warmth.
  - Accent / Craft layer: Warm glowing ember-orange `#c4622d` / `#d9581e` with subtle inner glow.
  - Ink / Primary Text: Deep charcoal `#1a1918` (high-contrast, AA compliant >14:1).
  - Secondary Text / Badges: Muted slate `#6c6965` / `#8f8a84`.
  - Border / Container: Ultra-fine 1px hairline `#e6e4df` with 24px corner radius.

## 2. Typography
- **Wordmark:** Inter / SF Pro Display, 64px, weight 750 (Bold), tracking -0.03em. Clean, non-gimmicky, authoritative.
- **Tagline / Hero Line:** 28px, weight 500 (Medium), `#1a1918`. "Authentic founder voice, backed by empirical B2B copywriting craft."
- **Meta pill row:** Three semantic badges:
  - `6 Personas` (LinkedIn · Marketing · Review · Slack · Short · Book)
  - `B2B Copywriting Research` (208 sources · 5-backend panel)
  - `Deterministic Voice Lint` (Zero em dashes · AU spelling · Stylometrics)
