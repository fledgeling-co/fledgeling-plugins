# Aesthetic Worlds — 24 anchored directions, each one buildable

A catalogue of named aesthetic worlds, each anchored to a real studio, publication, designer or object tradition, with concrete tokens so a direction is buildable rather than a vibe. Use it when the range map in `frontend-aesthetic-direction.md` needs candidates that are not this model's own reflexes, or when a user names an anchor ("Stripe Press feeling", "Are.na vibe") and needs the family it belongs to.

Two external sources informed this file: garden-skills' 25-recipe style catalogue (`design-directions.md`, `style-recipes/`, MIT licence), including the selected recipe files linked below, and taste-skill 2.0's premium-consumer palette rotations. The anchors are factual references to public work, not cloned trade dress: **mechanisms transfer, identity does not** (`mobbin-trawl.md`). This file names the school; it never ships a named product's wordmark, illustration style or signature colour.

## How to use

- **The user names an anchor.** Find the row, then load **one** matching recipe from `references/aesthetic-worlds/`. Do not read the whole table or every recipe into context.
- **Greenfield, candidates needed.** Candidates for the seven-list in `frontend-aesthetic-direction.md` must span at least three material families. The grouping below makes a spread one glance rather than a memory test. After the shortlist exists, load at most two recipe files from different groups.
- **Ration overlapping rows.** Where two rows share a ground, take the one whose signature move fits the subject and name the other in the direction block as the runner-up you declined.
- **Remix one axis, not two.** Pull one family's type with another's surface treatment when the brief supports it. Combining two whole rows is usually confused rather than original unless the user asked for the marriage and the reason is concrete.
- **No row is the default.** Every row is legitimate for some briefs and slop when it arrives regardless of subject. Warm-editorial, dark-plus-acid, and broadsheet carry the warning inline because they are common model defaults.
- **Check the diversity ledger first.** `references/diversity-ledger.md` records the last five visual decisions. A family, display face, topology, signature, or palette already retained is a conflict unless product truth or an explicit request overrides it.

## Group A — grids and data

| World | Type | Palette and surface | Signature moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Swiss / International** | One grotesk: Helvetica Now, Suisse Intl, or Founders Grotesk | Near-white, near-black, one red or blue | Exposed grid, hard alignment, no radius, no shadow, number-as-image |
| **Vignelli / Helvetica discipline** | One grotesk, six sizes for a lifetime | Black, white, one accent | Strict six-column programme; type size carries hierarchy; timetable-like labelling |
| **Tufte / data-ink** | ET Book serif with Gill Sans-class body | Paper white, ink, sparing accent | Smallest effective difference (Tufte: still visible, no extra ink); small multiples; sparklines in prose; wide annotating margins |
| **Pentagram / type-as-image** | Bold grotesk display at enormous scale | Restrained ground, one or two colours | Headline is the illustration; identity through type relationships; image-like word breaks |
| **NYT / broadsheet editorial** | Serif display with sans captions | Newsprint, ink, one spot colour | Multi-deck hierarchy; column rules; dense but ordered. **AI-default warning:** this is the broadsheet look when used without a subject reason |
| **Monocle / internationalist magazine** | Grotesk with serif deck | Toned paper, ink, red accent | Kicker, dek, standfirst rhythm; numbered agenda only where sequence is real; place-rich imagery |

## Group B — quiet and human

| World | Type | Palette and surface | Signature moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MUJI / Kenya Hara** | Quiet humanist sans, restrained weights | White or true neutral, one material tone | Emptiness as information; one thought per view; product or object as protagonist |
| **Aesop / apothecary** | Conversational serif with plain sans utility | Mineral or botanical tones, dark ink, tactile imagery | Ingredient-led hierarchy; generous label-like spacing; real object photography. Do not counterfeit the material with CSS |
| **Dieter Rams / Braun** | Functional grotesk, compact scale | Warm white, graphite, one signal colour | Honest controls; visible structure; no decorative elevation; the object explains itself |
| **Stripe Press / tactile editorial** | Bookish serif with humanist sans | Warm surface, ink, one restrained accent | Object photography as argument; pull quote or marginal note; paper rhythm. **AI-default warning:** warm-editorial is a reflex when it is not earned |
| **Headspace / soft humanist** | Rounded humanist sans, bold but not black | Saturated calm colours with two or three named roles | Breathing space; friendly illustration or real imagery; spring motion used for feedback, not every element |

## Group C — tools and builders

| World | Type | Palette and surface | Signature moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Linear / tool quietness** | Deliberately tracked sans with mono utility | Warm dark or restrained light, one accent under 5% of pixels | Hairline borders; keyboard-first chips; dense-calm hierarchy; shortcut as real affordance |
| **Vercel / precision with one event** | Neutral sans with strong weight contrast | Black and white with one feathered mesh or light event | Empty space around a single proof; deploy-log realism; one gradient moment, not a gradient page |
| **Raycast / command surface** | Neutral sans with compact mono metadata | Dark surface, small per-extension colour marks | Command palette as hero; keyboard path; colour belongs to the item it identifies |
| **Notion pre-AI / friendly tool** | Friendly sans or restrained serif accent, one UI family | Light neutral, quiet warm tint, small functional colour set | Page-as-canvas; flexible blocks; warmth in copy and structure rather than decoration |

## Group D — motion and rawness

| World | Type | Palette and surface | Signature moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Field.io / generative identity** | Technical grotesk or variable display face | Dark or saturated field with controlled spectral accents | Data-driven forms; identity generated by the system; motion is the brand. Build a reduced-motion still composition |
| **Active Theory / cinematic space** | Display type kept sparse beside a strong visual system | Deep tonal ground, light as material | WebGL or spatial hero; one physics interaction; the exit and the primary action stay legible |
| **Resn / scroll narrative** | Editorial display with quiet utility text | Scene-specific palette, not a universal dark mode | Chapter-like scroll; payoff for exploration; counted transitions: one story beat per pinned scene, named in the chapter list |
| **Are.na / raw content** | System sans or intentionally plain face | Browser-like neutral, content-led colour | Content over chrome; irregular collection; links as material; no polish layer pretending the content is absent |
| **Bloomberg Businessweek / typographic violence** | Grotesk and display type used at conflicting scales | Strong primaries or black and white | Copy as image; abused grid; hard crop; one deliberate collision. Half-commitment reads broken |

## Group E — genre and material

| World | Type | Palette and surface | Signature moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Y2K retro-futurist** | Extended or condensed display pair with pixel utility | Chrome, iridescence, saturated blue or pink | Outlines, dither, starburst, marquee energy; effects are structural. Do not sprinkle chrome onto a normal SaaS layout |
| **Mid-century modern** | Geometric sans with a humanist companion | Mustard, teal, rust, cream or a deliberate alternate | Atomic geometry; low organic curves; object-like composition; rhythm from shape, not rounded cards |
| **Industrial / utilitarian** | DIN, Univers-class, or a named condensed grotesk | Concrete neutral, safety orange or signal yellow | Rulers, specs, stencils, exposed metadata; measurements must be real or marked sample data |
| **Playful / toy** | Rounded sans with chunky weights | Saturated pastels, two or three hues | Sticker-like layers; pill controls only where affordance supports them; spring feedback; no decorative emoji by default |

## Palette rotations for premium-consumer briefs

Taste-skill 2.0 identifies the warm beige, brass, clay, oxblood and espresso combination as a repeated model default on cookware, wellness, artisan, heritage and DTC briefs. It is not banned when the brand genuinely owns it. It is banned as the unexamined first answer and must not repeat across consecutive premium-consumer commissions in the same session.

When that family arrives without a named brand reason, choose one materially different family and explain the choice in the direction contract:

| Rotation | Ground | Accent and material cue | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cold luxury** | Silver-grey, smoke, or near-black | Chrome, ice, or a single pale metallic cue | The object is precise, engineered, or light-led |
| **Forest** | Deep green with bone or neutral support | Amber, lichen, or one warm signal | The subject earns outdoor, botanical, or durable-material associations |
| **Black and tan** | True off-black with a warm tan | No beige field; one hard contrast | The product needs editorial severity without paper softness |
| **Cobalt and neutral** | Saturated cobalt with white or cool grey | No brass family; colour carries identity | The product is energetic, technical, or graphic |
| **Terracotta and slate** | Cool slate with rust | Rust as the only warm note | The subject is tactile but the page must not become craft beige |
| **Monochrome and one pop** | Off-white and off-black | Electric blue, emerald, or hot pink, one only | The product's form or photography is the hero |

A rotation is not a new recipe by itself. Name the subject-derived mechanism that makes it belong to the page, then choose its type, density, radius, imagery, and motion. A palette swap over the same centred hero, three-card row, and zigzag is not variety.

## Anti-convergence check

Before committing, answer these four questions in the direction contract:

1. **What category default did we name?** Write the actual arrangement, not "generic SaaS".
2. **What predictable opposite did we reject?** Avoiding navy and gold by choosing terminal dark is still a reflex.
3. **Which two structural decisions differ?** At least two of layout topology, hierarchy, density, type system, and material treatment must change. A new colour alone does not count.
4. **What will a neighbouring product be unable to borrow unchanged?** This is the swap test. If the answer is "nothing", the direction is not committed.

If the chosen world could be copied to a neighbouring product by changing the nouns and colours, return to `references/aesthetic-ideation.md` and run `trawl:trawl` before writing CSS.
