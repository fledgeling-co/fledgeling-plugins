# Generative identity - Field.io / motion as the medium

- **Family:** motion and rawness
- **Best for:** launch moments, brand films, experimental portfolios, experience-led pages
- **Touchstones:** Field.io work and kinetic identity case studies
- **Source:** [garden-skills recipe](https://github.com/ConardLi/garden-skills/blob/aaf9a82f5efd73e87cc0998edc398e75bfc35901/skills/web-design-engineer/references/style-recipes/field-io.md) (snapshot `aaf9a82f`)

## Tokens

- **Palette:** one dark or saturated field with one controlled generative colour system.
- **Type:** variable display face whose axis changes for a named narrative reason; restrained body face.
- **Scale:** one display scale, one body scale, six or fewer moving objects in the hero.
- **Spacing:** irregular only where the motion system explains the landing point.
- **Shape:** usually sharp. Lighting comes from the generated scene, not arbitrary CSS shadows.
- **Motion:** choreographed sequence, scroll-driven state changes, and a still reduced-motion composition.

## Signature moves

1. The identity is generated by a rule the visitor can perceive.
2. Type changes weight, width, or position to show a state, not to jitter.
3. One canvas or spatial field transforms the page between narrative beats.
4. Staggered arrivals have a readable order and a settled end state.

## Avoid

Do not use a static screenshot of a motion-led recipe as the whole deliverable. Do not add cursor-reactive effects everywhere, heavy reading content, or a motion mode with no reduced-motion still.

## Do not use when

The artifact must be read at length, is a small repair, or the audience has low hardware/performance tolerance and no budget for a spatial experience.

## Subject-mining prompt

What changes in the product's world over time: a signal, a field, a score, a material, or a decision? Make that change the motion system.
