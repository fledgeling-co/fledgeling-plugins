# Raw content - Are.na / honest web

- **Family:** motion and rawness
- **Best for:** research communities, artist platforms, indie tools, content collections
- **Touchstones:** Are.na and the Are.na annual
- **Source:** [garden-skills recipe](https://github.com/ConardLi/garden-skills/blob/aaf9a82f5efd73e87cc0998edc398e75bfc35901/skills/web-design-engineer/references/style-recipes/are-na.md) (snapshot `aaf9a82f`)

## Tokens

- **Palette:** browser-like neutral ground and ink, link blue, visited purple, one hairline grey.
- **Type:** system serif and/or system sans by deliberate choice; no custom display face.
- **Scale:** 14 to 16px body, 1.45 line-height, headings as larger text rather than a separate identity face.
- **Spacing:** tight 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32px.
- **Shape:** zero radius, no shadow, flat rectangles only when grouping carries meaning.
- **Motion:** none or instant. Do not add polish motion to prove that the page was designed.

## Signature moves

1. Links retain a clear underline and a visible visited state.
2. Lists, tables, and prose flow without decorative containers.
3. A link-rich footer is useful navigation, not a brand strip.
4. Content density comes from real material, never invented microcopy.

## Avoid

Do not add premium signals, modals, tooltips, rounded SaaS cards, custom cursors, or decorative micro-interactions. This recipe needs full commitment; half-raw reads broken.

## Do not use when

The audience expects a conventional product shell, a premium object treatment, or a conversion journey with a filled primary action and a named first-viewport offer.

## Subject-mining prompt

What content would still matter if every visual treatment disappeared? Let that content determine the page's hierarchy and links.
