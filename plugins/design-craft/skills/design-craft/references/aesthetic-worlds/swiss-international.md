# Swiss International - Vignelli / wayfinding

- **Family:** grids and data
- **Best for:** wayfinding, institutional identity, transport, ordered information
- **Touchstones:** Vignelli's transit work, Knoll catalogues, *The Vignelli Canon*
- **Source:** [garden-skills recipe](https://github.com/ConardLi/garden-skills/blob/aaf9a82f5efd73e87cc0998edc398e75bfc35901/skills/web-design-engineer/references/style-recipes/vignelli-swiss-helvetica.md) (snapshot `aaf9a82f`)

## Tokens

- **Palette:** near-black ink, near-white ground, one red, yellow, blue, or orange signal. Pick one.
- **Type:** one grotesk with 400, 500, and 700 weights. Use size, not colour, for most hierarchy.
- **Scale:** 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 72px on an 8px baseline.
- **Grid:** 12 columns, hard alignment, one readable left-aligned measure.
- **Shape:** zero radius, zero shadow, no gradient.
- **Motion:** none or one instant signage-like change.

## Signature moves

1. A horizontal or vertical signal bar carries a section heading.
2. A single oversized number acts as a navigational landmark.
3. Tables, schedules, and lists align to one baseline.
4. The grid becomes visible only when it explains real relationships.

## Avoid

Do not add soft cards, decorative gradients, multiple accents, friendly copy, or centred-everything. This recipe is cold by design; do not warm it with unrelated editorial ornament.

## Do not use when

The content is emotional, intimate, tactile, or dependent on photography. Use a humanist or editorial recipe instead.

## Subject-mining prompt

What does this audience already trust as a wayfinding system: a timetable, a catalogue, a labelling standard, a score, or a map? Borrow that system's relationships, not its marks.
