# Builder tool - Linear / quiet product precision

- **Family:** tools and builders
- **Best for:** developer tools, infrastructure, B2B SaaS, keyboard-first products
- **Touchstones:** Linear product surfaces and changelog, Linear Method
- **Source:** [garden-skills recipe](https://github.com/ConardLi/garden-skills/blob/aaf9a82f5efd73e87cc0998edc398e75bfc35901/skills/web-design-engineer/references/style-recipes/linear.md) (snapshot `aaf9a82f`)

## Tokens

- **Palette:** warm dark or restrained light ground, layered surfaces, one accent under 5% of pixels.
- **Type:** tracked sans for display and body; mono only for code, commands, shortcuts, or measurements.
- **Scale:** 14 to 15px body, a compact product ramp, display tracking around -0.02em.
- **Spacing:** 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96px.
- **Shape:** modest 6 / 12 / 16px radius. One soft elevation level; never glow.
- **Motion:** 150ms state feedback, 350 to 450ms layout movement, no bounce.

## Signature moves

1. Hairline borders clarify a real panel relationship.
2. Keyboard shortcuts appear where the action can actually be invoked.
3. The hero shows the real product surface instead of a fake dashboard.
4. Accent colour belongs to focus, active state, or one proof moment.

## Avoid

Do not use a dark ground and acid accent merely because the product is technical. Do not fill the page with gradients, pills, stock photography, or identical feature cards.

## Do not use when

The audience is non-technical, the product needs warmth or play, or the brief explicitly rejects tool-like density.

## Subject-mining prompt

What action does this tool make faster for a person who already knows the work? Let the shortcut, instrument, or output of that action become the signature.
