What actually predicts duration

Stage labels predict loosely. Volume of work predicts well. Counting Edit and Write calls per unit separates the population cleanly, and it is the signal the tier assignment rests on. The gap between the ends of each row is the finding: the spread is enormous, and it widens with size.

The gap is the point

A read-only unit's range is 16 minutes wide. A 40-file unit's is 100. Sizing work by its median alone throws away the thing that actually decides whether an afternoon is enough.

Why not the median

Measured p90 ÷ median is 3.4 for build work and 3.1 for research. A point estimate is wrong by a factor of three in the ordinary case, and it reads as precision.

The connector is a gap

It spans two percentiles of one population. It is not a trajectory — nothing here says a unit moves from one end to the other, or that the distribution between them is even.

The four tiers reckon prints

The bands above become four tiers. A work item is assigned one from its class and the wording of its title, and every assignment records the reason — because the ranges are measured and the assignment is a heuristic, and conflating those two would be this skill's own failure mode.

How a wave is costed

A wave runs its members in parallel, so it costs its slowest member rather than their sum — measured wall-clock ÷ slowest member was 1.05 at the median and 1.8 at p90 across 253 real waves. But that rule alone lets a big wave claim a speed nothing has achieved, so the low bound is the largest of three candidates, not the first.

Why the third bound exists

Twenty items across eight slots is arithmetically fast. Measured speedup over serial was 2.2× median and 4.0× at p90, so a plan implying better than 4.0× is describing something that has never happened.

Rounding has a direction

A randomised audit over 500 trials caught the last leak: rounding a low bound down by half a minute produced a 4.03× schedule. Bounds now round away from optimism.

Concurrency has a lid

Peak actually reached was 5 at the median, 10 at p90, 16 at most. Past about 8 members the slowest-member property degrades to 1.55, so a bigger wave widens its own upper bound.

What these numbers cannot say

They carry no failure rate

A unit that ran forty minutes and produced work later rejected counts identically to one that landed. These answer how long an agent will be busy — not how long until the work is accepted.

Wall-clock includes waiting

A unit stalled behind a rate limit measures longer than the same work would today. That is honest for planning and wrong for costing, so nothing here is a claim about model spend.

One machine, one operator

31 projects, one hardware profile, one convention that serialises merges. A repo with a slower test suite will differ, and no correction factor is offered because none was measured.

The tier assignment is unvalidated

The ranges are measured; deciding which tier an item belongs to reads its wording, not its work. That heuristic has never been checked against outcomes, which is why every estimate ships with the reason it landed where it did.
