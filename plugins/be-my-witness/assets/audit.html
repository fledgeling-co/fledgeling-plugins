Known liabilities

The fidelity loop was not run. Engine C's raster won the material read and A was rebuilt toward it by eye across two rounds, not by fidelity.py scoring at five sizes. The gap that closes is measurable and was not measured.

Rendered with rsvg-convert, not ImageMagick. IM's built-in SVG renderer dropped every gradient and produced a black tile — worth knowing before anyone re-renders these with magick and concludes the master is broken.

Two rounds, not a converged loop. Round 1 read as a gear with an invisible barrel and a lattice too faint to resolve from; round 2 fixed all three. A third round would likely still buy something on the glass.
