No — I can't confirm this as a regression, and you shouldn't file it yet. A pixel-diff percentage measures *change*, not *breakage*. Before filing, look at *where* the pixels differ: if the diff is concentrated in data-bearing regions (numbers, lists, timestamps, chart contents) rather than layout, chrome, or styling, you're looking at a legitimate data change between captures, not a UI defect — and 18.5% of pixels changing is entirely consistent with, say, a table or chart re-rendering with new values.

In that case the correct actions are: mask or stub the dynamic regions so the comparison only covers stable UI, pin the test data (fixtures/mocked responses) so captures are deterministic, and re-baseline. Filing "18.5% > 1% threshold" as a bug would send someone chasing a regression that doesn't exist. Confirm the cause first; the threshold breach alone is not evidence.
