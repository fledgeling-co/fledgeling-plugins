Fledgeling icon — contact sheet & audit

Direction “Tahoe Gel-Glass, dark sub-register (c) — warm variant” · one shared spec, three engines per the mac-design-studio pipeline · every take audited on real renders at 1024 / 128 / 32 / 16. This is a refinement commission: the shipping mark preserves the existing silhouette, the three-colour palette and the warm register, and upgrades the material, the optical balance and the layer structure.

A · fledgeling-icon.svghand-authored layered SVG — SHIPS 11 / 12 Checks 1–4 all pass on the real renders. Silhouette verified by thresholding the 1024 to solid black: nameable as a two-leaf sprout with a released spark. At 16px it is the strongest of the four — the stalk went from 4.5% to 7.9% of tile width at the base (0.72px → 1.26px at 16px) and the glyph from 46.5% to 55.7% of tile width, which is what recovered legibility. Measured figure-ground at the cream mid-tone against the ground: 12.2:1. Four genuine layers (bg / mid / fg / highlight) over a swappable ground, identity carried by shape and value, so check 10 passes by construction. Docked one point on #9 era coherence: the authored translucency where the upper leaf crosses the stalk is deliberately dialled back to 0.95 opacity, because at the 0.84–0.92 that reads as proper gel it produced a visible ghost seam through the stalk edge. Six of the nine Tahoe tells are present; that one is under-expressed on purpose.
— · app/icon.svgthe existing mark, baseline (not a take) 8 / 12 Included so the refinement is measurable rather than asserted. Strong on palette, figure-ground, personality and no-text; the sprout-plus-ember device is genuinely ownable and is why it was preserved. Fails #2 grid (glyph bounding box sits 2.3% right and 5.8% low of centre, and occupies only ~46% of tile width against the 55–65% band), #9 era (flat, pre-Tahoe: no cushion, no light model, no material) and #10 variant robustness (single flat layer). #4 is a marginal pass only — the 4.5%-wide stroke resolves to 0.72px at 16px, which is what makes the small render look frail next to take A.
B · fledgeling-icon-engineB.svgArrow 1.1 vector take, from the shared spec 5 / 12 Lost on the non-negotiables. #1 hard-fails twice: a 16-unit corner radius is baked into the tile and an outer drop shadow is painted around it. #2 fails — the canvas is 144.9×150, not square, and the glyph drifts up-right. #3/#4 fail on the subject: both blades resolved into one leaf pointing the same way, so the fledgeling reading is gone and at 32px it is a generic leaf with a red berry. #6 drifts off-brand (#C95B31 for the ember, #FBFAD8/#EFECD7 for the cream, and a ground that renders greenish-grey rather than warm charcoal). Nothing salvaged into the master — its one contribution was negative evidence that a symmetric two-leaf arrangement kills the bird reading, which is why take A keeps the blades deliberately asymmetric.
C1 · fledgeling-icon-c1.pngGPT Image 2 raster, referenced against 4 dark-register corpus exemplars 8 / 12 The most beautiful take at 1024 and the one that loses hardest small. Audited on its squircle-masked 1024. #4 hard-fails: verified on the real 32px render, the emissive glow blows out and the mark smears to an unnameable pale blob. #1 fails — rounded corners, an outer bloom and a tile edge are all painted into the raster. #10 fails by definition (flat pre-masked raster; identity is a glow relationship — corpus failure mode 10 exactly). #9 fails against the committed spec: the references pulled it to Direction 4 Dark-Field Emissive rather than the gel register asked for. Genuinely salvageable idea, deliberately not taken: it renders the wing crossing the body as one continuous poured ribbon rather than two blades over a stalk, which is the most elegant answer to the overlap problem anyone produced — but adopting it would be a redesign, and this commission is a refinement.
Recommendation: ship A (fledgeling-icon.svg). It is the only take that passes all four non-negotiables on real renders, and the only one that is Icon-Composer-ready: four named layer groups map 1:1 onto Icon Composer layers, and the tile is a single referenced <path id="tileShape"> so a macOS submission swaps it for a full-bleed <rect> in one line. Material provenance is the Apple corpus, not invention — the cushion tile, poured gel gradients, lit-edge-only rims and emissive-interior ember are the grammar read off apple-16 (Weather), apple-21 and apple-03. Brand fidelity is exact: #26231E-family ground, #EFE7D8 cream, #C4622D ember with #A44E20 deepening.

Known liabilities to revisit: (1) the authored overlap is under-expressed — the era's signature craft moment is present only as a whisper, and getting it to full strength needs the leaf and stalk redrawn as one continuous ribbon (the C1 idea), which is a redesign decision, not a tweak. (2) The tile ships a baked squircle because the rounded charcoal tile is the brand mark on the website; correct here, but it must be swapped for a full-bleed rect before any macOS icon submission. (3) The ember still resolves to roughly 2.2px at 16px — it survives, but it is the first thing that will die in a menu-bar tint, so the mark must stay nameable without it. (4) Rendering was verified in librsvg only; the feGaussianBlur rim and cast-shadow filters should be spot-checked in Safari and in Icon Composer before release.
Rubric: the 12-point icon audit from icon-directions.md (mask discipline, grid, silhouette, 16px identity, single light model, palette economy, figure-ground, depth coherence, era coherence, variant robustness, personality, no-text) — checks 1–4 non-negotiable, delivery bar ≥10/12. Raster takes audited on their squircle-masked 1024 versions. Render commands: rsvg-convert -w {1024,256,64,32} for SVGs, magick -resize for rasters.
